# Dark Stalker

Source page: [job-dark-stalker.html](job-dark-stalker.html)

Technical job · Fighter

A job that mixes physical attacks with dark spells, drains life and spends its own HP for more damage.

1. [Fencer Base](job-fencer.md)
2. [Knight Expert](job-knight.md)
3. [⇄](job-switching.md)
4. Dark Stalker Technical
5. [Guardian Chronicle](job-guardian.md)

## Job change

**Requirement:** STR 30 or higher, as a [Knight](job-knight.md) at any Job Level.

1. Go to the [Master Sage's House](maps.html#map:10023000) in [Uptown](maps.html#map:10023000).
2. Talk to the **Sage Master** and choose to browse the items **on top of the shelves**.
   **Sage Master**
3. Buy a **Forbidden Book** for **100,000 G**.
4. Talk to the **Sage Master** again and choose to change your job.
5. When asked, **take off all your equipment**.
6. Choose the skills to take with you: **1 skill**, plus 1 more for every 10 Job Levels. See [Job Switching](job-switching.md).
7. You are now a Dark Stalker. You can switch back to Knight the same way.
