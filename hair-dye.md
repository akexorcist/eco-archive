# Hair Dye

Source page: [hair-dye.html](hair-dye.html)

**Hair Color** items change your hair colour.

## Make a hair colour

Every recipe uses the same pattern, at the item maker's **Drug Create** menu:

| Step | You need | Cost | Success |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1. Make [Dry Powder](materials.html#item:10001110) | 1 × [Kuko Fruit](materials.html#item:10003601) | 1,000 G | 80% |
| 2. Make a coloured powder | 4 × [Jerico](materials.html#item:10032800) of that colour + 1 × [Dry Powder](materials.html#item:10001110) | 200 G | 99% |
| 3. Make the hair colour | 1 × [Carrier Oil](materials.html#item:10001907) + 1 × coloured powder | 1,000 G | 99% |

- An NPC item maker can do these for you if you don't have the crafting skill.
- One extraction costs **1,000 G** whether you process 1 item or 100.

## Good to know

- **Nine colours can be made** from monster drops. The rest come from [boxes](boxes.md).
- **There is no white hair dye.** The white-looking colour from [boxes](boxes.md) is **Silver Hair Color**, and it can't be made. [White Jerico](materials.html#item:10032811) is only used for Flying Garden paint.
- **Race colours come from boxes.** The Titania colours and these drop from treasure boxes:
   - **Light Dominion Hair Color**
   - **Medium Dominion Hair Color**
   - **Dark Dominion Hair Color**
   - **Silver Hair Color**
