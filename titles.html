    Titles

    Names you earn in the game and show with your character.

    On this page
    • The Title window
    • Rarity
    • How titles are earned
    • Bonuses
    • Title list
    • Starting titles
    • Connect

    The Title window

    • Open it from the shortcut menu: Title.
    • The window has two parts:
      • Battle title: one title whose Ability (stat bonus) you get.
      • Appearance title: the title on show, built from a Left Title, a Connect and a Right Title. Each part can be removed on its own.
    • Each title shows its Category, Rarity, Acquisition Info (the condition and your progress) and Ability.
    • Titles you don't have yet are darkened, and their Ability shows as ? ? ? ? ?. A new title is marked [New] until you select it.
    • The list shows one of these:
      • All: Basic and Special titles
      • Basic: titles you can earn at any time
      • Special: titles from special conditions
      • Favorite: titles you marked as favorite
    • You can search the list by name and sort it by rarity, category, name or any Ability stat.
    • When you earn a title, the game shows Learned title [name].

    Rarity

    Each title has a rarity that shows how hard it is to earn:

    • Initial: the starting titles
    • Rookie
    • Easy
    • Normal
    • Hard

    How titles are earned

    Each title you can earn has one condition, or a few that you must all meet. The Acquisition Info shows your progress as a count.

    • Defeat monsters: a set number of one monster, or 100 monsters of one type (Plant, Bird, Undead …). A kill counts only when it gives you EXP or Job EXP.
    • Enter a dungeon: reach a floor of a Dimension dungeon.
    • Clear EX Captures: the Another Chronicle captures for Lv 100–110.
    • Knight Training: take part, place 1st with your faction, win an award, or reach the top 10 on the Rank Board. A battle counts only when you receive its reward. World Competition doesn't count.
    • Quest Counter: finish quests that cost 1 quest point.
    • Growth and stories: reach a level, change to an Expert job, or finish an Event Navigation story.
    • Events: rewards from the Bingo event and the Guardians of the Stars event.
    • Other: own a Ring Castle, or use an introduction letter at Nibelungen Beautician or Nibelungen Hair Salon.

    Bonuses

    • Only your battle title adds stats. An appearance title is for show.
    • A bonus can be negative: some titles raise one stat and lower another.
    • The stats a title can change:
      • Max HP, Max MP and Max SP
      • ATK (Blow), ATK (Slash) and ATK (Thrust), each as a minimum and a maximum
      • MATK, as a minimum and a maximum
      • Add DEF and Add MDEF
      • Short Range, Long Range and Magic Accuracy
      • Short Range, Long Range and Magic Dodge
      • Critical rate and Critical dodge
      • ATK Speed and Cast Speed

    Title list

    A range such as ATK (Blow) +15–20 adds 15 to the minimum and 20 to the maximum.
