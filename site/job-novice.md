# Novice

Source page: [job-novice.html](job-novice.html)

Starting job

The job every new character starts with, with few equipment limits and no special benefits.

**Next job:**

- Any base job: [Swordsman](job-swordsman.md), [Fencer](job-fencer.md), [Scout](job-scout.md), [Archer](job-archer.md), [Wizard](job-wizard.md), [Shaman](job-shaman.md), [Vates](job-vates.md), [Warlock](job-warlock.md), [Miner (Tatarabe)](job-miner.md), [Farmer](job-farmer.md), [Ranger](job-ranger.md) or [Merchant](job-merchant.md).
- [Joker](job-joker.md): only if you never change jobs, at Lv 100 and Job Level 50. A Novice who changes to a base job can't become a Joker.

## Job change

**Requirement:** each base job has its own requirement, listed below.

1. Enter [Uptown](maps.html#map:10023000). You need an **Acropolis City Pass**. Every master below is in [Uptown](maps.html#map:10023000).
2. Choose a job and check that you meet its requirement.

A Novice who never changes jobs can become a [Joker](job-joker.md) at Lv 100 and Job Level 50.
