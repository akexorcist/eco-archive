# Marionettist

Source page: [job-marionettist.html](job-marionettist.html)

Technical job · Backpacker

A job that builds and controls marionettes, including elemental ones that fight for it.

1. [Farmer Base](job-farmer.md)
2. [Alchemist Expert](job-alchemist.md)
3. [⇄](job-switching.md)
4. Marionettist Technical
5. [Harvester Chronicle](job-harvester.md)

## Job change

**Requirement:** Alchemist at any Job Level.

1. Go to [Master Sage's House](maps.html#map:10023000) in [Uptown](maps.html#map:10023000).
2. Talk to the **Sage Master** and choose to browse the items **on top of the shelves**.
   **Sage Master**
3. Buy a **Forbidden Book** for **100,000 G**.
4. Talk to the **Sage Master** again and choose to change your job.
5. When asked, **take off all your equipment**.
6. Choose the skills to take with you: **1 skill**, plus 1 more for every 10 Job Levels. See [Job Switching](job-switching.md).
7. You are now a Marionettist. You can switch back to Alchemist the same way.
