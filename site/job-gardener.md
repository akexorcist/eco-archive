# Gardener

Source page: [job-gardener.html](job-gardener.html)

Joint job

A Joint Job that grows plants in pots, builds decorations and changes the weather and sky over your Flying Garden.

## Job change

**Requirement:** Job Level 30 or higher in the job you have now.

1. Buy [Mineral Water](materials.html#item:10001800) to bring along. Grocery shops and cafes sell it, for example the [Grocery Shop](maps.html#map:10057000) in [Fareast City](maps.html#map:10057000).
2. Talk to the [Bad Merchant](maps.html#map:10023000) in [Uptown](maps.html#map:10023000) and give him the [Mineral Water](materials.html#item:10001800). Go through all his dialog. He tells you **Elizabeth** was stolen and the Flying Garden flew east toward [Fareast City](maps.html#map:10057000).
   **Bad Merchant**
3. Travel to [Fareast City](maps.html#map:10057000) and enter the [Fareast Cafe](maps.html#map:10057000). Talk to the **Strange Merchant** surrounded by Mandra plants. He tells you **Juliet** was stolen too.
   **Strange Merchant**
4. Leave the cafe and talk to the [Commoner](maps.html#map:10057000) in front of it. He saw the Flying Garden fly toward [Mai Mai Island](maps.html#map:10059000).
   **Commoner**
5. Travel to [Mai Mai Island](maps.html#map:10059000). Climb the [Airship Rope](maps.html#map:10059000) on the beach to board the [Gardener Guild's Garden](maps.html#map:10059000).
   No picture **Airship Rope**
   - If the rope isn't there, you still have the Breeder Dark Dragon quest open. Finish it first.
6. Talk to the **Gardener Master** next to the fountain behind the [Gardener Guild](maps.html#map:10059000). Answer the master's questions.
   **Gardener Master**
   - First set: **Help bring Juliet back** → **It may be a plant** → **Tell me** → **One** → **Two** → **None**.
   - Second set: **Help bring Elizabeth back** → **Slippery skin** → **Charming** → **Smooth dark green hair** → **That's all**.
   - Then choose **You are the culprit**, and agree to help the master.
7. Go into the [Gardener Guild](maps.html#map:10059000) and talk to the **Gardener**.
   **Gardener**
8. Return to [Fareast City](maps.html#map:10057000) and ask around for someone who knows plants. Talk to the old man by the trash can past [Mother](maps.html#map:10057000).
9. Return to the **Gardener** in the [Gardener Guild](maps.html#map:10059000) on the [Gardener Guild's Garden](maps.html#map:10059000) ([Mai Mai Island](maps.html#map:10059000)).
10. On [Mai Mai Island](maps.html#map:10059000), talk to the [Mandra Turnip](maps.html#map:10059000) near the shop and choose **Follow it**. This starts the rescue quest and takes you into [Within the Mai Mai Ruins](maps.html#map:10059000).
   **Mandra Turnip**
   - The quest costs no quest points. You have **40 minutes** inside the ruins.
11. At the entrance, the **Gardener** gives you **30** seeds to throw. Only these seeds hurt the monsters in the ruins. You get more seeds from trees and wooden boxes inside.
   **Gardener**
12. Find your way through the maze to the boss and defeat it.
   - In a room with no way on, defeat all the white vegetables. A warp appears.
   - If you die, you continue from where you were. Rooms you opened stay open.
13. After the boss, leave through the warp within **60 seconds**, before the ruins collapse. Otherwise you start the quest again.
   - Release any possession first, or the warp won't take you out.
14. Return to the [Gardener Guild's Garden](maps.html#map:10059000) and talk to the **Gardener Master** and the **Gardener**. You receive the **Gardener Brooch**. Wear it to become a Gardener.
   **Gardener Master**
   **Gardener**

## Skills

Gardener skills cost no skill points. You learn each one automatically when you reach its Job Level.

**Job Lv 1 I'm a Tree Active self-buff · Max Lv 1**

- Transforms the character into a tree for 60 seconds.
- The state uses the Hiding buff internally and ends when removed by normal buff-ending behavior.

| Lv | SP | Cast time | Cooldown |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | 25 | 0.5 sec | 1 sec |

**Job Lv 5 Gathering Active field skill · Max Lv 1**

- Consumes 8 selected seed items and plants them around the character.
- It checks the surrounding 3x3 area and spawns plants on walkable empty cells, excluding the caster's own cell.

| Lv | Cast time | Cooldown | Area | Seed cost |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | 2.5 sec | 1 sec | 3x3 around caster | 8 selected seeds |

**Job Lv 8 Weed Killer Active area attack · Max Lv 5**

- Damages plant-type enemies around the caster.
- The attack checks a 3x3 area and only affects valid plant targets.

| Lv | SP | Cast time | Cooldown | Area | Target | Damage |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | 6 | 0.5 sec | 1 sec | 3x3 | Plant enemies only | 10 fixed Neutral damage |
| 2 | 6 | 0.5 sec | 1 sec | 3x3 | Plant enemies only | 10 fixed Neutral damage |
| 3 | 6 | 0.5 sec | 1 sec | 3x3 | Plant enemies only | 10 fixed Neutral damage |
| 4 | 6 | 0.5 sec | 1 sec | 3x3 | Plant enemies only | 10 fixed Neutral damage |
| 5 | 6 | 0.5 sec | 1 sec | 3x3 | Plant enemies only | 10 fixed Neutral damage |
