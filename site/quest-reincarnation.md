# Reincarnation Quest

Source page: [quest-reincarnation.html](quest-reincarnation.html)

Reincarnate into the higher form of your race and get your **Chronicle job**.

## Before you start

- You need all of these:
   - **Level 100** or higher.
   - **Job Level 50** in your Expert job, your Technical job, or both.
   - Finish the fight with the [Emil Dragon](monsters.html#monster:14520000), which follows the **Lv 99 weapon event**.
- **Novice:** you need Lv 100 and Job Level 50 as a Novice. You become a [Joker](job-joker.md), and the end of the quest is different (see step 7).
- Your reward depends on **your level when you reincarnate**.
- Bring HP potions. HP regenerates on its own in the fights.

## Walkthrough

### 1. Find the mysterious cat

- Go to [Northern Promenade](maps.html#map:10065000). When you meet the requirements, a **mysterious creature** (a cat) appears there. If it doesn't appear, you don't meet them yet.
- Talk to the creature until it runs away.
   **? ? ?**

### 2. Talk to Valeria

- Go to the [Northern Cafe](maps.html#map:10065000) and talk to **Valeria**.
   **Valeria**

### 3. Chase the cat

- Find the creature again in [Northern Promenade](maps.html#map:10065000) and follow it into the [Queen Audience Room](maps.html#map:10065000).
- Follow it into the **magic circle**.

### 4. Answer the cat's questions

- In the [White World](maps.html#map:10065000), walk straight ahead to the creature sleeping there. It asks you 3 questions.
   - Choose **not to wake it**.
   - Answer that **it is real**.
   - Any answer works for the third question.
- A wrong answer sends you back. Walk to the creature again.

### 5. Talk to Mucha and look in the White Book

- In the room with the book, talk to **Mucha**.
   **Mucha**
- Look inside the **White Book**. It sends you to face the opponent of your job line. Then return to the room with the book.
   **White Book**
- Talk to **Mucha** again.

### 6. Earn the proofs of strength

- Look inside the **White Book** and choose **Moment of Emptiness (Time)**. There you earn the **Proof of Great Hero (Event)** and the [Proof of Soul Business Talent (Event)](crafting.html#recipe:2272).
- Defeat the [Being Trapped in the Emptiness](monsters.html#monster:10410103) there for [Pearl Soul](materials.html#item:10048015) and [Great Pearl Soul](materials.html#item:10048016).
- Combine them:
   - **50** [Pearl Soul](materials.html#item:10048015) or **1** [Great Pearl Soul](materials.html#item:10048016) make **1** [Talisman of 100 Battles Practice](crafting.html#recipe:77). A golem there also sells it.
   - **250** [Pearl Soul](materials.html#item:10048015) or **5** [Great Pearl Soul](materials.html#item:10048016) make **1** [Proof of Soul Business Talent (Event)](crafting.html#recipe:2272).
- Use the [Talisman of 100 Battles Practice](crafting.html#recipe:77) to fight a **copy of yourself**. You have **10 minutes**.
   - Each talisman works once. Everyone who goes in needs a talisman and the quest.
   - **Backpacker lines and the Vates line** skip this fight.
- Fight the opponent of your job line.
   | Job line | Opponent | Chronicle job |
   |---|---|---|
   | [Swordsman](job-swordsman.md) | **Kamui** | [Gladiator](job-gladiator.md) |
   | [Fencer](job-fencer.md) | **Risel** | [Guardian](job-guardian.md) |
   | [Scout](job-scout.md) | **Kino** | [Eraser](job-eraser.md) |
   | [Archer](job-archer.md) | **Griver** | [Hawkeye](job-hawkeye.md) |
   | [Wizard](job-wizard.md) | **Renette** | [Force Master](job-force-master.md) |
   | [Shaman](job-shaman.md) | **Renette** | [Astralist](job-astralist.md) |
   | [Warlock](job-warlock.md) | **Renette** | [Soul Taker](job-soultaker.md) |
   | [Vates](job-vates.md) | **Flor** | [Cardinal](job-cardinal.md) |
   | [Miner](job-miner.md) | **Camellia** | [Maestro](job-maestro.md) |
   | [Farmer](job-farmer.md) | **Canon** | [Harvester](job-harvester.md) |
   | [Merchant](job-merchant.md) | **Canon** | [Royal Dealer](job-royal-dealer.md) |
   | [Ranger](job-ranger.md) | **Prish** | [Strider](job-strider.md) |

### 7. Reincarnate

- Look inside the **White Book** again. **Take off your equipment** first.
- You get your Chronicle job and reincarnate into the higher form of your race. Your level and Job Level go back to **1**.
   | Race | Reincarnates into |
   |---|---|
   | Emil | **High Emil** |
   | Titania | **High Titania** |
   | Dominion | **High Dominion** |
- **Novice:** you get the **Book of Rebirth: A Journey to Find Yourself**. Fill it at 7 spots around the world, show it to **Mucha**, then look inside the **White Book** again. See [Joker](job-joker.md) for the spots.

## Rewards

You get the bonus for **every level from 100 up to the level you reincarnate at**. If you reincarnate at Lv 109, you get the bonuses for Lv 100 to 109, but not Lv 110.

| Level | High Emil | High Titania | High Dominion |
|---|---|---|---|
| 100 | Grace effect | 2 Feathers | 2 Feathers |
| 101 | Aureola effect | 3 Feathers | 3 Feathers |
| 102 | Ankle Feather effect | Wing color | Wing color |
| 103 | **Catty Medamar Keeper** | Wing color | Wing color |
| 104 | **Catty Medamar Assault** | Wing color | Wing color |
| 105 | **Catty Medamar General** | Wheel (Powers) | Tail (Trident) |
| 106 | [Marionette Nekogurumi (Purple)](marionettes.html#marionette:10084300) | Ring (Seraphim) | Tail (Slim) |
| 107 | [Marionette Nekogurumi (Blue)](marionettes.html#marionette:10084301) | Wing color | Wing color |
| 108 | [Marionette Nekogurumi (Orange)](marionettes.html#marionette:10084302) | Ex Feather | Ex Feather |
| 109 | [Black Cat](partners.html#item:10084400) | Ex 2 Feathers | Ex 2 Feathers |
| 110 | [Cat Sith](partners.html#item:10084500) | Ex 3 Feathers | Ex 3 Feathers |

- From Lv 103, the High Emil bonuses are **items**.
- Switch **effects, wings, halos and tails** in the **Rebirth window**, opened from the equipment window. Default is your old look.
- Bonuses above the level you reincarnated at can be added later with **Page EXP**, in **Increase Rebirth Privilege**. This needs the **Another Book**, from the Another Chronicle quest **Obtain Another Chronicle**.
   - The High Emil marionettes and partners can't be received a second time.

## Good to know

- **You keep your looks and the skills you learned.** After you reincarnate, you can use all your Expert and Technical skills up to the Job Level you reached. There is no 5-skill limit like in Switch Job.
- **No Reserve needed.** You can learn every Expert and Technical skill without [Reserve](job-switching.md), so the Reserve tab is gone from the skill window. Each skill opens at its job's Job Level: a Job Level 50 skill needs that job at Job Level 50.
- **At most 40 skills in total**, counting first, second and third job skills.
- **You can wear gear up to 10 levels above your own level.**
- ****Renette** is the hardest opponent.** She is very hard for the Wizard and Shaman lines.
- **Vates line tip:** **Flor** heals itself and stands on light-element ground.
