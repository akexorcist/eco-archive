    Catty (Anzu) Quest

    A strange pot turns you into a cat spirit, and you must get your body back.

    Level
    30 or higher, with 20 Fame
    Items needed
    1 × Piece of Mind
    Reward
    Catty (Anzu)
    On this page
    • Before you start
    • Walkthrough
    • Rewards
    • Good to know
    • Related

    Before you start

    • Finish the Catty (Momo) Quest and equip a Catty.
    • Carry only the Catty you wear. Later you collect a heart for every Catty you carry.
    • Get a Piece of Mind. It's a somewhat rare drop from Angel Feather. You can't use it on a Flying Garden.
    • Free up 200 capacity and weight. You receive two quest items of 100 each.

    Walkthrough

    1. The pot on Tonka Island

    • Go to Uptown and talk to the Seamstress. Choose the second answer twice.

      Seamstress

    • Go to Tonka Island and talk to Miranda the Flower Lover in the south of the island, then enter her house.

      Miranda the Flower Lover

    • Talk to the pot inside and choose to take it. You turn into Catty (Anzu). Your stats and equipment stay the same.

    2. To Titania

    • Go back to the Seamstress in Uptown. She gives you an apricot-coloured triangle bandage.

    • Go to the 5th floor of the Guild Palace and talk to the Titanian Committee President.

      Titanian Committee President

    • Use the Piece of Mind. Talk to Titus and Tita.

    • Talk to Titus again to leave. He gives you a Titus Crystal(Event).
    • Talk to Emil, then go inside the house and talk to Marcia. Talk to Emil again: he sends you to North Mountain.

    3. Icy Island

    • Go to Eternal North Boundary and take the northwest exit toward Icy Island. Use the Titus Crystal(Event) to pass the invisible wall.

    • Talk to the Icy Chief. He gives you an Icy Prism(Event).

      Icy Chief

    4. Collect the Catty hearts

    • Collect a heart for each Catty you carry. Carry that Catty and talk to:

      Catty you carry Talk to
      Catty (Momo) Any Trash Can
      Catty (Ai) The Broken Electell in Fruit Forest
      Catty (Yamabuki) The Senior Merchant on Battleship Island
      Catty (Sumire) Ghost Buster Gadoo at Ghost Shelter
      Catty (Akane) Rita in the Rita's Marionette Craftshop on Tonka Island

    • If you wear Catty (Midori), you don't need any hearts.

    • Rita is also on Tonka Island, so collect her heart last.

    5. Back to your body

    • Go back to Miranda's house on Tonka Island. Talk to your own body and enter it.
    • Talk to the pot again. The hearts gather around it, and you receive Catty (Anzu).

    Rewards

    • Catty (Anzu): the first boy Catty. Skills and details on Catties

    Good to know

    • Logging out keeps your progress. You are still Catty (Anzu) and still on the quest when you log back in.
    • Check the pot before you finish. It tells you which Catties are missing if you don't carry them all.
