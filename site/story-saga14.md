# Gate of Emerald Forest

Source page: [story-saga14.html](story-saga14.html)

A bonus Main Story from SAGA 14: you follow **Titus** and **Tita** from [El Ciel (Upper Layer)](maps.html#map:11023000) to [Mai Mai Island](maps.html#map:10059000).

## Story

Your journey begins at Titus's lab in [El Ciel (Upper Layer)](maps.html#map:11023000), where you meet **Titus** and **Tita**. When **Tita** runs off, you follow her trail down through [El Ciel (Lower Layer)](maps.html#map:11024000) and **Emil's house** in [Downtown](maps.html#map:10024000) all the way to [Tonka Island](maps.html#map:10062000). After asking the [Shy Boy](maps.html#map:10062000) and [Talkative Housewife](maps.html#map:10062000) for information, you catch up with **Tita** on [Mai Mai Island](maps.html#map:10059000). From there, you venture into [Baobab Forest](maps.html#map:10059100), catch a [Horse Mackerel](materials.html#item:10108700) for the **Man Betting on Fishing**, and push deep into [Oracle Wind Cave 2F](maps.html#map:20073000) to defeat an unnamed enemy before returning to [Downtown](maps.html#map:10024000) to visit **Demise**.

Later, you return to Titus's lab in [El Ciel (Upper Layer)](maps.html#map:11023000) only to watch **Titus** leave as well. You track him down while he gathers information in [Downtown](maps.html#map:10024000), and together you travel by airship from [Uptown](maps.html#map:10023000) to [Tonka Island](maps.html#map:10062000), speaking with the [Dreaming Craftsman](maps.html#map:10062000) and the [Shy Boy](maps.html#map:10062000). Your airship journey continues to [Mai Mai Island](maps.html#map:10059000), where you follow **Titus** into [Baobab Forest](maps.html#map:10059100) and defeat the monster [Matan Kikuru](monsters.html#monster:15620003) before heading back to [Downtown](maps.html#map:10024000) to look for **Titus** and the others.

## Characters

**Tita**

You meet her at Titus's lab in [El Ciel (Upper Layer)](maps.html#map:11023000), then chase her to the exit of [El Ciel (Lower Layer)](maps.html#map:11024000).

**Titus**

Leads the second part, from his lab to [Baobab Forest](maps.html#map:10059100).

**Demise**

You visit him in [Downtown](maps.html#map:10024000) at the end of the first part.

**Man Betting on Fishing**

Asks you to catch him a fish in [Baobab Forest](maps.html#map:10059100).

[Shy Boy](maps.html#map:10062000)

One of the people you ask for clues in [Tonka Island](maps.html#map:10062000).

[Talkative Housewife](maps.html#map:10062000)

Another person you ask for clues in [Tonka Island](maps.html#map:10062000).

[Dreaming Craftsman](maps.html#map:10062000)

A craftsman in [Tonka Island](maps.html#map:10062000) you question in the second part.
