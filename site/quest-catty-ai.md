# Catty (Ai) Quest

Source page: [quest-catty-ai.html](quest-catty-ai.html)

A cat spirit is trapped in a broken robot. Rebuild the robot to set her free.

## Before you start

- Finish the [Catty (Momo) Quest](quest-catty-momo.md) and equip **Catty (Momo)** for the whole quest.
- Collect all of these:
   | Item | How to get it |
   |---|---|
   | [Critical Crystal](upgrade-crystals.md#item:90000046) | See [Upgrade Crystals](upgrade-crystals.md) |
   | [Strength Crystal](upgrade-crystals.md#item:90000044) | See [Upgrade Crystals](upgrade-crystals.md) |
   | [Magic Crystal](materials.html#item:90000045) | See [Upgrade Crystals](upgrade-crystals.md) |
   | [Life Crystal](upgrade-crystals.md#item:90000043) | See [Upgrade Crystals](upgrade-crystals.md) |
   | [Heart Ruby](materials.html#item:10012100) | From [wooden boxes](boxes.md), or made at the [Jewelry Shop](maps.html#map:10023000) in [Uptown](maps.html#map:10023000) from a [Red Crystal 1](materials.html#item:10046100) and 4 × [Abradant](materials.html#item:10045800) ([recipe](crafting.html#recipe:761)) |
   | [Galvanized Iron Body](materials.html#item:10030003) | Made at the [Junk Shop](maps.html#map:10024000) in [Downtown](maps.html#map:10024000) from a [Motor](materials.html#item:10030100), an [Electell Model](materials.html#item:10030002) and a [Pocket Computer](materials.html#item:10030200). The parts drop from robots |
   | [Cotton Ribbon](materials.html#item:10043700) [Cloth](materials.html#item:10021300) [Thread](materials.html#item:10019800) | For a new **Seamstress Triangle Bandage** at the end |
- The **Junk Shopkeeper** gives you the **Gear** for the [Electell Model](materials.html#item:10030002) for free.
- Don't carry a **marionette** when you talk to the **Junk Shopkeeper**; he won't talk to you.

## Walkthrough

### 1. Find the broken robot

- Go to [Fruit Forest](maps.html#map:10030000) and talk to the [Broken Electell](maps.html#map:10030000) on the east side.
   **Broken Electell**
- Go to [Uptown](maps.html#map:10023000) and talk to the **Seamstress**. She tells you to ask someone from Tonka.
   **Seamstress**
- Leave by the south gate and talk to [Niccolo](maps.html#map:10031000) just outside, on the west side of the road. He lists the parts the robot needs.
   **Niccolo**

### 2. Make the heart

- Go to [Downtown](maps.html#map:10024000) and talk to the **Junk Shopkeeper**. Choose **yes** to both questions. He turns your [Heart Ruby](materials.html#item:10012100) into an **Empty Heart**.
   **Junk Shopkeeper**
- Go to the [White Church](maps.html#map:10023000) in [Uptown](maps.html#map:10023000) and talk to the **White Church Vates**. Choose to **give your vitality** (the first answer). He turns the **Empty Heart** into a [Tin Heart](materials.html#item:10047200).
   **White Church Vates**
- **Don't combine** the [Galvanized Iron Body](materials.html#item:10030003) with the [Tin Heart](materials.html#item:10047200). That makes an [Electell Marionette](materials.html#item:10030001) and uses up both.

### 3. Repair the robot

- With all the parts, talk to the [Broken Electell](maps.html#map:10030000) again. It takes everything except the bandage materials.
- Talk to [Niccolo](maps.html#map:10031000) again.
- Talk to the **Seamstress** again and have her make a new **Seamstress Triangle Bandage** from your [Cotton Ribbon](materials.html#item:10043700), [Cloth](materials.html#item:10021300) and [Thread](materials.html#item:10019800).
- With the bandage in your bag, talk to the [Broken Electell](maps.html#map:10030000) once more and choose **yes** to pull the plug (the first answer). You receive **Catty (Ai)**.

## Rewards

- **Catty (Ai)**: skills and details on [Catties](catties.md#ai)

## Good to know

- **Make the bandage before the last talk.** Without it the robot explodes and you have to start over.
- **Only ask the **Seamstress** for the bandage during the quest.** If you ask her to cut without the quest, she makes pink shorts and uses up your materials.
