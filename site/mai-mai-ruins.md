## How to enter

- Enter the [Mai Mai Ruins 1st Deck](maps.html#map:20164000) from [Mai Mai Island](maps.html#map:10059000). To reach the island, see [Airships & Rides](airships-and-rides.md).
   [Mai Mai Island](maps.html#map:10059000)
- [Machinery Representative](maps.html#map:20164000) on the [Mai Mai Ruins 1st Deck](maps.html#map:20164000) gives the [Lv 90 Weapon Quest](quest-lv90-weapon.md).
   **Machinery Representative**
