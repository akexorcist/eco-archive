# Dual Job

Source page: [dual-job.html](dual-job.html)

A **Dual Job** lets you use skills from a second job line while you keep your own job.

- Each skill unlocks one level at a time as your Dual Job Level goes up. In the game's Dual Job window, skills whose maximum level differs from the original job's are shown in red.

## Requirements

- Your character must be **reborn** (High Emil, High Titania or High Dominion) and have a **Chronicle job** (third job).
- **Level 110** and **Job Level 50**.

## How to unlock

1. Go to [Endless Corridor B41F](maps.html#map:10042000) and talk to **Fraise**.
   **Fraise**
2. Go with her to the [Endless Corridor (Back Layer)](maps.html#map:10042000). Talk to **Fraise** again next to the **Insmouse Statue** at the entrance and read the statue.
   **Insmouse Statue**
3. Explore the floor to its exit. The level 110 monsters there drop four colours of jewel. Collect **10 of each colour**. Fraise gives you 1 of each when you meet her at the exit.
   - **Red Jewel**
   - **Blue Jewel**
   - **Yellow Jewel**
   - **Green Jewel**
4. Take the warp to the [Endless Corridor (Arcane Art)](maps.html#map:10042000). Talk to the **Mysterious Object** in the middle of the room: it trades 10 Jewels of one colour for 1 Large Jewel.
   **Mysterious Object**
5. Set each Large Jewel in the statue of the same colour:
   **Salamander Statue**
   **Mandragora Statue**
   **Electell Statue**
   **Insmouse Statue**
   - **Large Red Jewel** → **Salamander Statue**
   - **Large Green Jewel** → **Mandragora Statue**
   - **Large Yellow Jewel** → **Electell Statue**
   - **Large Blue Jewel** → **Insmouse Statue**
6. Go back to the middle. **Orthos** appears. Accept his quest: he sends you to a private area to fight him. He uses both Swordsman and Warlock skills.
   **Orthos**
7. After you win, report to **Orthos** and **choose your Dual Job system**.

## After you unlock it

- Your Job Level is shown as the Dual Job's Job Level, starting at **1**, with a cap of **110**.
- You also get the job bonus of the system you chose, and you can wear the equipment of that system's Chronicle job.
- Set up to **10 skills** in the **Selected Skills** box (drag or double-click). They appear in the **Dual** tab of the skill window. You can change them as often as you like.
- If your main job and Dual Job share a skill, the higher skill level is used.
- To change which skills you can use, talk to **Orthos** on [Endless Corridor B41F](maps.html#map:10042000), or use a **Book of Conversion**. After an event, an NPC in [Uptown](maps.html#map:10023000) gives the book.

**Orthos**

- You can only have **one** system at a time. Each system keeps its own Dual Job EXP, so changing systems doesn't lose progress.
- The Joker line can't be taken as a Dual Job. A [Joker](job-joker.md) can still take another system.
- You can take your own job's system, for example [Swordsman](job-swordsman.md) as a [Gladiator](job-gladiator.md).
- A [Merchant](job-merchant.md) Dual Job doesn't let you open a stall without a golem.

## More systems to switch to

You can unlock more systems and then switch between them with **Orthos** or the **Book of Conversion**. The current one is marked with a ☆.

- To start the event, you need all of these:
   - You can enter the Abyss floors of the [Endless Corridor](dungeons.md).
   - A **Book of Conversion** from the NPC in [Uptown](maps.html#map:10023000).
   - A Dual Job.
- Start it at the NPC who gives the **Book of Conversion**.

## Systems

- There are **12** systems. Each system gives you skills from its 4 jobs and a bonus to 3 stats.

The full list (12 systems) is in [data/dual-jobs.json](data/dual-jobs.json). The interactive list with filters is on [dual-job.html](dual-job.html).
