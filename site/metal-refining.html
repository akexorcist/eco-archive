    Metal Refining

    Advanced Metal Refining smelts ore into metal, makes metal parts and melts old parts back down.

    On this page

    • Refine with an NPC
    • Tools

    • Metal parts

    How it works

    • Advanced Metal Refining is a Miner (Tatarabe) skill, learned at Job Level 13, with 3 levels.
    • Each skill level opens the recipes of that level. The Crafting page lists every Advanced Metal Refining recipe.
    • Most metal recipes succeed 99% of the time. A failed try gives an Iron Fragment.
    • Most recipes past the ore stage need a Miner Furnace.

    Refine with an NPC

    Talk to one of these NPCs with the materials and the tool in your inventory. Each makes every Advanced Metal Refining recipe, and also weapon and armor recipes.

    The fee depends on the recipe level:

    Recipe level Fee
    Lv 1 100 G
    Lv 2 5,000 G
    Lv 3 10,000 G

    A few special recipes cost more, such as the True Heart Stone from a True Rainbow Stone (40,000 G).

    They stand in these places:

    • Weaponsmith in the Weaponsmith, Downtown.

    • Weaponsmith in the Iron City Weaponsmith, Lower Part of Iron City.

      Weaponsmith

    • Weaponsmith in the Fortress Weaponsmith, Enigma Group Fortress.

      Weaponsmith

    • Ironworks Receptionist in the Forge Lobby, Lower Part of Iron City.

    Enhanced crystals

    At Lv 3, a Miner Furnace fuses one Magic Crystal with a summonstone into an enhanced crystal. Each try succeeds 90%; a failed try gives 10 × Iron Fragment. See Upgrade Crystals.
