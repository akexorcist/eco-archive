    Base job · Backpacker

    A gathering job that gets more drops from monsters and chests and opens locked boxes.

    Job change

    Requirement: You are a Novice. There is no level requirement.

    1. Enter Uptown. You need an Acropolis City Pass.
    2. Go to the Guild Palace Lobby 1F in Uptown.
    3. Go inside and take the elevator to the Guild Palace Lobby 4F.
    4. Enter the room on the south side of the 4th floor. This is the Ranger Guild.

    5. Talk to the Ranger Master and choose Ranger.

      Ranger Master

    6. Accept his test: bring 2 × Bone.

    7. Hunt Bawoo for the Bone. Bawoo live on Lake Utena, Snov Mountain Road and Lake Utena Estuary.

    8. Return to the Ranger Master in the Ranger Guild (Guild Palace Lobby 4F, south room) and hand over the Bone.

    9. When asked, take off your armor.
    10. Talk to the master again. You are now a Ranger and receive Neck Goggles. You also learn Bivouac.
