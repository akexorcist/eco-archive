    Job change

    Requirement: MAG 30 or higher, as an Elementalist at any Job Level.

    1. Go to the Master Sage's House in Uptown.

    2. Talk to the Sage Master and choose to browse the items on top of the shelves.

      Sage Master

    3. Buy a Forbidden Book for 100,000 G.

    4. Talk to the Sage Master again and choose to change your job.
    5. When asked, take off all your equipment.
    6. Choose the skills to take with you: 1 skill, plus 1 more for every 10 Job Levels. See Job Switching.
    7. You are now an Enchanter. You can switch back to Elementalist the same way.
