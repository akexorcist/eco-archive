# Status Points

Source page: [stat-points.html](stat-points.html)

The **status points** you get each level, spent to raise your stats.

## Points per level

- You start with **2** points at Lv 1.
- Each level-up gives this many points: points = ⌊(new level − 1) ÷ 3⌋ + 3 The amount grows by 1 every 3 levels.
- The highest level is **110**. At Lv 110 you have **2,291** points in total.
- DEM characters get no status points. They raise their stats with DEMIC chips instead. See [DEMIC Chips](demic-chips.md).

| Level | Points per level-up | Total points at the top of the range |
|---|---|---|
| Lv 2–10 | 3–6 | 41 |
| Lv 11–20 | 6–9 | 116 |
| Lv 21–30 | 9–12 | 224 |
| Lv 31–40 | 13–16 | 366 |
| Lv 41–50 | 16–19 | 541 |
| Lv 51–60 | 19–22 | 749 |
| Lv 61–70 | 23–26 | 991 |
| Lv 71–80 | 26–29 | 1,266 |
| Lv 81–90 | 29–32 | 1,574 |
| Lv 91–99 | 33–35 | 1,880 |
| Lv 100–110 | 36–39 | 2,291 |

## Cost to raise a stat

Raising a stat by 1 costs:

cost = ⌊current value ÷ 6⌋ + 1

Only the value you raised yourself counts, not bonuses from your job or equipment.

| Current value | Cost for +1 |
|---|---|
| 1–5 | 1 |
| 6–11 | 2 |
| 12–17 | 3 |
| 18–23 | 4 |
| 24–29 | 5 |
| 30–35 | 6 |
| 36–41 | 7 |
| 42–47 | 8 |
| 48–53 | 9 |
| 54–59 | 10 |
| 60–65 | 11 |
| 66–71 | 12 |
| 72–77 | 13 |
| 78–83 | 14 |
| 84–89 | 15 |
| 90–95 | 16 |
| 96–99 | 17 |

- Example: raising a stat from 1 to 50 costs **233** points; from 1 to 100 costs **883**.
- A stat you raise with points stops at **100**.
- DEM can raise a stat to **120** with [DEMIC Chips](demic-chips.md), not counting Limitation chips and Parts.
- Job and equipment bonuses add on top. The total in the status window has no fixed cap.
- With 2,291 points at Lv 110, you can raise at most two stats to 100. About **529** points are left for the rest.
- Your starting values depend on your race. See [Races](races.md).
- A stat reset returns every point you spent. See [Game Basics](game-basics.md#resetting-status-and-skills).

## Job bonus

The status window shows each stat as two numbers, for example **80+13**. The first is what you raised with points. The second is the bonus from your job and equipment.

- The job bonus grows with your job level: bonus = ⌊job level × job rate⌋
- Expert, Technical and Chronicle jobs add **30** to their job level in this formula.
- Every stat formula below uses your total, bonus included.
- A [Dual Job](dual-job.md) adds its own bonus to 3 stats.

## Stat formulas

Each value below comes from your total stats. ⌊ ⌋ means "round down". The squared ⌊ ⌋ terms are why a stat gives an extra jump at certain values.

| Value | Formula | Extra jump at |
|---|---|---|
| Min ATK | (STR + ⌊STR ÷ 9⌋²) × (1 + DEX × 1.5 ÷ 160) With bows, guns, throwing weapons and cards, INT replaces DEX. | STR 9, 18, 27… |
| Max ATK | STR + ⌊(STR + 14) ÷ 5⌋² | STR 6, 11, 16… |
| Min MATK | (MAG + ⌊(MAG + 9) ÷ 8⌋²) × (1 + INT × 1.2 ÷ 320) | MAG 7, 15, 23… |
| Max MATK | MAG + ⌊(MAG + 17) ÷ 6⌋² | MAG 7, 13, 19… |
| Short Range Accuracy | DEX + ⌊DEX ÷ 10⌋ × 11 + level + 3 At most 500. | DEX 10, 20, 30… |
| Long Range Accuracy | INT + ⌊INT ÷ 10⌋ × 11 + level + 3 At most 500. | INT 10, 20, 30… |
| Short Range Dodge | AGI + ⌊(AGI + 18) ÷ 9⌋² + ⌊level ÷ 3⌋ − 1 At most 500. | AGI 9, 18, 27… |
| Long Range Dodge | ⌊INT × 5 ÷ 3⌋ + AGI + ⌊level ÷ 3⌋ + 3 At most 500. | — |
| ATK Speed | AGI × 3 + ⌊(AGI + 63) ÷ 9⌋² + 129 At most 800, reached at total AGI 108. | AGI 9, 18, 27… |
| Cast Speed | DEX × 3 + ⌊(DEX + 63) ÷ 9⌋² + 129 At most 800, reached at total DEX 108. | DEX 9, 18, 27… |
| % DEF | ⌊VIT ÷ 3⌋ + ⌊VIT ÷ 9⌋ × 2 At most 50. VIT 120 adds 2 more and VIT 150 another 3. | VIT 3, 6, 9… |
| % MDEF | ⌊INT ÷ 3⌋ + ⌊VIT ÷ 4⌋ At most 50. INT 120 adds 2 more and INT 150 another 3. | — |
| Critical rate | ⌊(DEX + 1) ÷ 8⌋ | DEX 7, 15, 23… |
| Critical dodge | ⌊(AGI + 1) ÷ 8⌋ | AGI 7, 15, 23… |
| Max HP | (VIT × 3 + ⌊VIT ÷ 5⌋² + level × 2 + ⌊level ÷ 5⌋² + 50) × job factor | VIT 5, 10, 15… |
| Max MP | (MAG × 3 + level + ⌊level ÷ 9⌋² + 30) × job factor | — |
| Max SP | (INT + VIT + level + ⌊level ÷ 9⌋² + 20) × job factor | — |

- The job factor is a fixed multiplier per job. Later jobs have higher factors.
- Equipment, skills and Iris cards add to these values after the formula.
- For how DEF reduces damage, see [Game Basics](game-basics.md#damage-and-def).
