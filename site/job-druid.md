## Job change

**Requirement:** MAG 30 or higher and Job Level 30 or higher as a Vates.

1. Go to the [White Church](maps.html#map:10023000) in [Uptown](maps.html#map:10023000).
2. Talk to the **White Church Vates** and choose to change jobs. Listen until the conversation ends and accept his **challenge**.
   **White Church Vates**
3. Get a **Piece of Mind**. It drops from [Angel Feather](monsters.html#monster:10270000) and [Skeleton Archer](monsters.html#monster:10350000). [Angel Feather](monsters.html#monster:10270000) lives on [North Plateau](maps.html#map:10049000), [North Mountain](maps.html#map:10050000) and [Eternal North Boundary](maps.html#map:10051000).
4. Use the **Piece of Mind**. It warps you to the [Titania Transmission Room](maps.html#map:10023000).
5. Talk to **Titus**. He sees the Druid mark on you and sends you back to the **White Church Vates**.
   **Titus**
6. Return to the **White Church Vates** in the [White Church](maps.html#map:10023000) ([Uptown](maps.html#map:10023000)). The teleporter in the [Titania Transmission Room](maps.html#map:10023000) takes you to the [White Church](maps.html#map:10023000).
7. Choose to become a Druid. When asked, **take off all your equipment**. You are now a Druid.
