# Tutorial Quests

Source page: [quest-tutorial.html](quest-tutorial.html)

Lessons from **Amis** on her airship, which show new characters around the game.

## How it works

- Every lesson starts and ends with **Amis** in [Amis' Airship](maps.html#map:10023000). Talk to her to start the next lesson.
   **Amis**
- To board the airship, talk to **Amis** in [Uptown](maps.html#map:10023000).
   **Amis**

## Walkthrough

### 1. First Time Job

- Use the steering wheel on the airship to go down to [Uptown](maps.html#map:10023000).
- Find **Dark Feather Alma** on [Acropolis East Bridge](maps.html#map:10023100). Take his quest, **Dark Feather Request**, finish it and bring him what he asks for.
   **Dark Feather Alma**
- Take the stairs at any bridge down to [Downtown](maps.html#map:10024000) and talk to **Dark Feather Alma**.
   **Dark Feather Alma**
- Go to the [Cafe](maps.html#map:10024000), [Downtown](maps.html#map:10024000) and help **Dark Feather Alma**.
   **Dark Feather Alma**
- Return to **Amis**.

### 2. Nice Gift

- Go east to [Acronia Forest](maps.html#map:10015000) and find **Momo Alma**.
   **Momo Alma**
- Collect the [Milk](materials.html#item:10002200) that **Bawoo Alma** asks for and give it to **Momo Alma**.
- Talk to **Bawoo Alma** on [Fareast Main Street](maps.html#map:10017000).
   **Bawoo Alma**
- Go to the [Airship Engineer](maps.html#map:10017000) on the same street and talk to **Bawoo Alma**. Use the **Bawoo Stamp** there.
   **Bawoo Alma**
- Go to the [Cafe Stamp Manager](maps.html#map:10023000) in [Uptown](maps.html#map:10023000) and listen to **Bawoo Alma** about stamps.
   **Bawoo Alma**
- Return to **Amis**.

### 3. A Big Deal

- Follow **Shabotan Alma** and **Necro Armor Alma** to the front of the [Guild Palace Lobby 1F](maps.html#map:10023000), [Uptown](maps.html#map:10023000). They teach you about jobs.
   **Shabotan Alma**
- Return to **Amis**.
- Talk to the **Beginner Quest Counter** in the school, then talk to her again.
   **Beginner Quest Counter**
- Take a quest from her, finish it and report back to her.

### 4. Fun Friend

- Go to the [Soul Storage](maps.html#map:10024000) in north [Downtown](maps.html#map:10024000), where **Loki Alma** waits.
   **Loki Alma**
- Inside, talk to **Loki Alma** and borrow a soul.
   **Loki Alma**
- Back on the airship, take the quest **Get My Bag!**
- Talk to the **Troubled West Faction Soldier** in west [Downtown](maps.html#map:10024000) and go with him to [Bee Village](maps.html#map:20004002).
   **Troubled West Faction Soldier**
- Return to **Amis**.

### 5. Exciting Trip

- Follow **Cockko Alma** and **Balulu Alma** to [Acronia Eastern Plains](maps.html#map:10025000).
   **Cockko Alma**
- Take the lost child to the village at [East Cape](maps.html#map:10018000).
   **Balulu Alma**
- Meet the [Airship Engineer](maps.html#map:10017000) on [Fareast Main Street](maps.html#map:10017000), where **Balulu Alma** waits.
   **Balulu Alma**
- Talk to the **Big Collector Merchant** in [Acronia Eastern Plains](maps.html#map:10025000) and do what he asks.
   **Big Collector Merchant**
- Return to **Amis**.

### 6. Wishes & Feelings

- Talk to **Seahorse Alma** in west [Downtown](maps.html#map:10024000).
   **Seahorse Alma**
- In the [Card Institute](maps.html#map:10024000), talk to **Seahorse Alma** or **Tarantula Alma** to buy a picture frame.
   **Seahorse Alma**
- Return to the school and talk to **Amis** to decorate the frame.

### 7. Pleasant Souvenir

- Visit **Pefang Alma** at each of these shops in turn:
   **Pefang Alma**
   | Stop | Place |
   |---|---|
   | Weapons | [Weapon Shop](maps.html#map:10024000), south [Downtown](maps.html#map:10024000) |
   | Armor | The stall on the south side of central [Downtown](maps.html#map:10024000) |
   | Adventure supplies | The stall on the north side of central [Downtown](maps.html#map:10024000) |
   | Accessories | [Jewelry Shop](maps.html#map:10023000), north [Uptown](maps.html#map:10023000) |
   | Tailoring | [Seamstress' House](maps.html#map:10023000), north [Uptown](maps.html#map:10023000) |
- Return to **Amis**.

### 8. Gloomy Adventure

- From [Acronia Eastern Plains](maps.html#map:10025000), go to [Acronia Eastern Beach](maps.html#map:10034000) and talk to **Salamander Alma**.
   **Salamander Alma**
- Split up with **Salamander Alma** and **Flyfish Alma** and find the **Wounded Bawoo**.
   **Wounded Bawoo**
- Return to **Amis**.
- Use a **Warp Key E** to go back to [Continental Cave B1F](maps.html#map:20000000) and follow the **Wounded Bawoo**.
   **Wounded Bawoo**
- Return to **Amis**.

### 9. Fashionable

- Get a Lv 35 job armor and show it to **Minidoo Alma** in the school. See the [Lv 35 Armor Quest](quest-lv35-armor.md).
   **Minidoo Alma**
- Go to the area next to the [Seamstress' House](maps.html#map:10023000) in northwest [Uptown](maps.html#map:10023000).
   Uptown — Seamstress' House
- Return to **Amis**.

### 10. Conveyed Warmth

- Talk to **Weapon Enhancement Instructor** in the school.
   **Weapon Enhancement Instructor**
- Talk to **Mummy Alma** in [Uptown](maps.html#map:10023000), next to the **ECO Ticket Collector**, to get enhancement crystals.
   **Mummy Alma**
- Return to **Amis**.

### 11. Where's The Heart

- Talk to **? ? ?**, the picture story show, in **...In a Dream...?**.
   **? ? ?**
- Return to **Amis**.
- Talk to [Crasher Bakia](maps.html#map:10024000) in west [Downtown](maps.html#map:10024000) and go to the [Card Institute](maps.html#map:10024000).
   **Crasher Bakia**
- Put an IRIS card into the slot, then talk to **Automedic Alma** or **Draki Alma**. See [Iris Cards](iris-cards.md).
   **Automedic Alma**
- Return to **Amis**.

### 12. Time of Departure

- Ask **Amis** about Alma.
- Raise your **Tiny Zero** to **Lv 50** or higher, then talk to **Amis** with it.
