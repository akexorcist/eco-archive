    Accessory Crafting

    Accessory Construction, the craft that makes hair ornaments, glasses, pendants and cut gems.

    On this page
    • How it works
    • Tools and common parts
    • Cutting gems
    • Glasses
    • Notable accessories
    • Related

    How it works

    Talk to the Jewelry Shop in the Jewelry Shop, Uptown, with the materials and the tool in your inventory. She makes every recipe up to Lv 5.

    Jewelry Shop
    • No job learns Accessory Construction.
    • Almost every recipe is free. Only the Mark of the Familiar recipes cost 5,000 G.
    • Most accessories come out as the normal item 91% of the time. The other 9% come out better:
      • 5%: the +1 version, such as Red Round-Rimmed Glasses +1
      • 3%: the +2 version
      • 1%: the +3 version
    • Cut gems always succeed.
    • The Crafting page lists every Accessory Construction recipe.

    Tools and common parts

    Jewelry Tools

    Use
    Tool for most accessories
    Where to get it
    Jewelry shops:
    • Iron City Jewelry Shop, Upper Iron City
    • Fareast Jewelry Shop, Fareast City
    • Morg Jewelry Shop, Morg City

    Pliers

    Use
    Tool for the Glass Frame and the Tiara of Protection
    Where to get it
    Jewelry shops (same as Jewelry Tools), plus:
    • Northern Jewelry Shop, Northern Promenade

    Diamond File

    Use
    Tool for talismans and some pendants
    Where to get it
    Crafted in 2 steps:
    • Make Diamond Dust (Lv 5):
      • 1 × Diamond
      • Iron Hammer (tool)
    • Make Diamond File (Lv 5):
      • 1 × Diamond Dust
      • 1 × Leather
      • 1 × Glue

    Pin

    Use
    Part in most brooches and pendants
    Where to get it
    Made with Weapon Construction (recipe):
    • 1 × Needle
    • Pliers (tool)

    Glue

    Use
    Part in most accessories
    Where to get it
    Made with Chemical Preparation (recipe):
    • 1 × Snakehead Fish Sweat
    • 1 × Jerico

    Abradant

    Use
    Used to cut gems and lenses
    Where to get it
    Made with Chemical Preparation (recipe):
    • 4 × Pebble
    • Iron Hammer (tool)

    Cutting gems

    At Lv 4, one crystal and 4 × Abradant make one gem. The crystal's colour and number decide the gem.

    Red crystal

    • Red Crystal 1→Heart Ruby
    • Red Crystal 2→Garnet
    • Red Crystal 3→Melody Stone
    • Red Crystal 4→Flame Summonstone

    Yellow crystal

    • Yellow Crystal 1→Imperial Topaz
    • Yellow Crystal 2→Rutile
    • Yellow Crystal 3→Electric Stone
    • Yellow Crystal 4→Wind Summonstone

    Blue crystal

    • Blue Crystal 1→Aquamarine
    • Blue Crystal 2→Lapis Lazuli
    • Blue Crystal 3→Cheater Rock
    • Blue Crystal 4→Water Summonstone

    Green crystal

    • Green Crystal 1→Emerald
    • Green Crystal 2→Peridot
    • Green Crystal 3→Jade
    • Green Crystal 4→Earth Summonstone

    Milky crystal

    • Milky Crystal 1→Diamond
    • Milky Crystal 2→Cubic Zirconia
    • Milky Crystal 3→Amethyst
    • Milky Crystal 4→Falcon Eye
    • Milky Crystal 5→Light Summonstone

    Glasses

    Each recipe in the table lists the parts it needs. Most glasses use a lens and a frame; the Snorkel uses only a lens.

    Glass Lenses

    • Make Glass Lenses (Lv 3):
      • 1 × Crystal
      • 4 × Abradant

    Glass Frame

    1. Make Titanium Wire (Lv 3):
      • 1 × Titanium Rod, made with Advanced Metal Refining
    2. Make Glass Frame (Lv 3):
      • 1 × Titanium Wire
      • Pliers (tool)
