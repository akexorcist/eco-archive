    Ancient Ruins

    The Ancient Ruins are private quest dungeons: each party gets its own copy, and the map is random every time.

    How to enter

    1. Go to the north side of Downtown, next to the fortune teller's shop.

    2. Talk to the West Faction Member in Downtown about the ruins. He sends you to Ruins Square.

      West Faction Member

    3. Talk to the East Faction Member in the middle of Ruins Square and choose a quest. Each quest costs 3 quest points.

      East Faction Member
    • Everyone in the party takes the quest, then one run clears it for the whole party.
    • Each quest has a time limit, 2 to 3 hours depending on the quest.
    • Quests come in levels from 10 to 80. The level 10 quest gives 1,500 EXP, 1,000 Job EXP, 1,000 gold and a treasure chest.
    • Many treasure chests on the Boxes page are found only in these quests, for example "Labyrinth Search" and "Demon Horde".

    Endless Corridor

    The Endless Corridor is a deep dungeon from Endless Corridor B1F down to Endless Corridor B99F.

    • Talk to the D Master in Staff Desert, at the entrance.

      D Master

    Dimension dungeons

    How to enter

    Each player needs one Dimensional Stone per entry, so a party needs one for every member.

    1. Talk to Dr. Dim in Uptown. He explains how to make the stone.

      Dr. Dim

    2. Make the Dimensional Stone with Item Refining (90% success), from either recipe:

      • Recipe 1:
        • 1 × Mana Water
        • 1 × Solid Pebble
        • 1 × Crest Paper
      • Recipe 2:
        • 1 × Mana Water
        • 1 dimension material, such as Dimension Coal or Dimension Ice Stone

    3. Carry the stone and walk into the normal dungeon's entrance. The game asks if you want to use it.

    4. Choose Use to spend one stone and go to the dimension version. Choose Don't use to enter the normal dungeon and keep the stone.

    Private dungeons

    A PD- dungeon (Private Dungeon) is a private copy of a dimension dungeon.

    • Use a Private Dungeon Generator (cash shop item) before you enter the dungeon. Every party member needs one.
    • Only you and your party (up to 8 players) can enter.
    • Monsters are as strong as in the D- dungeon, but give 1.5 × EXP and Job EXP.
    • No bosses or treasure chests appear.
    • It closes when the party breaks up, or when the last member leaves or logs out.

    Recommendations

    • Ancient Ruins, level 10 quest: the boss Jumbo Pururu has a lot of HP. Go with at least 2 players, or you may run out of time.
    • Demon Horde quest: recommended from level 60. It always has bosses that drop the same rare items as dungeon bosses.
