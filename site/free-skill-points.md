# Free Skill Points

Source page: [free-skill-points.html](free-skill-points.html)

Extra skill points you earn from quests and errands, on top of levelling up.

## How it works

- There are **21** extra skill points in all.
- They all go to your **first job**, even if you collect them as an Expert or Technical job.

## Acropolis

1 point

Open all four shops of the [Lv 35 Armor Quest](quest-lv35-armor.md), then talk to the [Elegant Lady](maps.html#map:10024000) in [Downtown](maps.html#map:10024000)

1 point

Bring a second fairy-filled Crystal to **Talented Astrologer Remia**. See [Lv 55 Armor Quest](quest-lv55-armor.md)

8 points

Finish the 8 Endless Corridor quests from [D Master](maps.html#map:10042000) and give each ring to the [Old Man](maps.html#map:10023000) at the east gate of [Uptown](maps.html#map:10023000): 1 point per ring

## Northern Kingdom

1 point

Go to the top of [Northern Promenade](maps.html#map:10065000) and talk to the queen, **Beldegard**

1 point

In the Spell User Guild, go to the skill/stat reset room, [Sorcerer Master Room](maps.html#map:10065000), and talk to the **Sorcerer** at the top. Only Spell Users can enter; others can possess a Spell User to get in

1 point

Do the document delivery quest twice: carry the Compendium of Law from the **Sage Master** in [Uptown](maps.html#map:10023000) to **Miriaria** in the north within 15 minutes. The first time gives a **Text Book**, the second a skill point

## Fareast

1 point

Talk to [Farmer Sam](maps.html#map:10057000) about the weeds, then talk to him again. You don't have to pull the weeds

1 point

Find the 7 lost chicks around the village for **Charles** in the top-left barn and try to catch each one, then report back

1 point

Carry a lunch box from the [Mother](maps.html#map:10057000) to the [Water Keeper](maps.html#map:10057000) at the water wheel, bottom right of town, and choose the skill point

## Iron South

1 point

Do the Fighter delivery of the [Lv 55 Armor Quest](quest-lv55-armor.md) for [Ghost Buster Gadoo](maps.html#map:10061000)

1 point

Do Mother's Medicine twice for [Novice Adventurer Porol](maps.html#map:10066000). See [Lv 55 Armor Quest](quest-lv55-armor.md)

1 point

Climb [Iron Volcano](maps.html#map:10064000) and talk to the [Salamander](maps.html#map:10064000) in the middle of the lava. Go with a party

1 point

Ask the fortune teller, [Iron South's Mother](maps.html#map:10066000), in the top-left of [Lower Part of Iron City](maps.html#map:10066000) to read your soul's light

1 point

Buy a 1 G flower from the [Flower Girl](maps.html#map:10063100) in the bottom-left of [Upper Iron City](maps.html#map:10063100)
