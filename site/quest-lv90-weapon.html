    Lv 90 Weapon Quest

    Clear the arsenal in Mai Mai Ruins 1st Deck for a random Lv 90 weapon.

    Items needed
    Arsenal Pass Card
    Quest points
    No points:
    • Gate to The Arsenal
    Reward
    One random Lv 90 weapon
    On this page
    • Before you start
    • Walkthrough
    • Rewards
    • Related

    Before you start

    • Get an Arsenal Pass Card. It opens the armory room. It drops from these monsters in Mai Mai Ruins 1st Deck:
      • Holograph
      • Holograf CD
      • Rebooted Guttinger

    Walkthrough

    1. Accept the mission

    • Go to Mai Mai Ruins 1st Deck and talk to Machinery Representative. Accept the mission Gate to The Arsenal. It has a 120-minute limit.

      Machinery Representative

    2. Defeat the Arsenal Keeper

    • Enter the armory room with your Arsenal Pass Card.
    • Defeat the Arsenal Keeper (Lv 105). It drops a Vacuum Pack (Private).

    3. Report back

    • Return to Machinery Representative in Mai Mai Ruins 1st Deck and report.
