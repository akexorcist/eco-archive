## Job change

**Requirement:** Job Level 30 or higher as a Ranger.

1. Go to the [Guild Palace Lobby 1F](maps.html#map:10023000) in [Uptown](maps.html#map:10023000).
2. Go inside and take the **elevator** to the [Guild Palace Lobby 4F](maps.html#map:10023000).
3. Enter the room on the **south side** of the 4th floor. This is the [Ranger Guild](maps.html#map:10023000).
4. Talk to the **Ranger Master** and choose to change your job. He asks you to bring an **Ice Flower(Event)**.
   **Ranger Master**
5. Get the **Ice Flower(Event)** from an Icy. **Transform into a Marionette** before you talk to them. Ice Flowers melt in human hands, so they won't hand it over otherwise.
   **Icy Chief**
   **Walking Icy**
   - On [Icy Island](maps.html#map:10051100), talk to the [Icy Chief](maps.html#map:10051100). You need a **Mysterious Crystal** to enter the island.
   - On [North Mountain](maps.html#map:10050000), bring **1** × [Happy Flower](equipment.html#item:50035600) and talk to the [Walking Icy](maps.html#map:10050000). She freezes the flower into an **Ice Flower(Event)**.
   - Only a plain [Happy Flower](equipment.html#item:50035600) works, not an elemental one. It is [crafted](crafting.html#recipe:708) from a [Flower Seed](materials.html#item:10005603) and [Mana Water](materials.html#item:10002202).
6. Return to the **Ranger Master** in the [Ranger Guild](maps.html#map:10023000) ([Guild Palace Lobby 4F](maps.html#map:10023000), south room) and hand over the **Ice Flower(Event)**.
7. Choose to become an **Explorer**. When asked, **take off your armor**.
8. You are now an Explorer. Your Job Level goes back to **1**.
