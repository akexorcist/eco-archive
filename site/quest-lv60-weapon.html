    Lv 60 Weapon Quest

    Help the ghost of the Princess of the Fallen Kingdom find her mother's lost earrings.

    Items needed
    4 × Crest Paper
    Repeatable
    Yes
    Reward
    The right to buy Lv 60 weapons and shields (26,000–70,000 G) from one random shop
    On this page
    • Before you start
    • Walkthrough
    • Rewards
    • Good to know
    • Related

    Before you start

    • Bring 4 × Crest Paper. Four of the search spots are behind sealed portals, and each portal burns one.
      • Crest Paper drops from these monsters, all inside the castle:
        • Spector
        • Evil Dead
        • Revenant
        • Skeleton Raphael
    • Keep your inventory light. The Princess of the Fallen Kingdom won't sell to you if you are overweight, or if the weapon would make you overweight.

    Walkthrough

    1. Go to Undead Castle

    • Go to Undead Castle.

    • Enter the castle through the nearest door. The front entrance puts you on the 2nd floor.

    • Climb to the 4th floor, the Audience Room.
    • In the Audience Room, step into the portal next to the two red chairs. It takes you to the King's Bedroom.

    • Talk to the Princess of the Fallen Kingdom. She asks you to find her mother's earrings: Empress Earring(Left) and Empress Earring(Right).

      Princess of the Fallen Kingdom

    2. Search for the earrings

    There are 6 search spots in the castle, but only 2 hold an earring. Which two is random every time you take the quest.

    • A spot shows as a sparkle on the floor and a star on the minimap. Walk onto it to search.
    • An empty spot gives you a worthless item or nothing at all. Move on to the next spot.
    • Visit the spots in any order. Once you have both earrings, stop searching and go back to the Princess of the Fallen Kingdom.

    3. Return to the Princess

    • Go back to the Princess of the Fallen Kingdom in the King's Bedroom (Audience Room, 4F → portal next to the two red chairs).
    • Choose Give her the earrings. She opens one of three shops at random.
    • Buy the weapon you want.
      • Choosing buy or cancel ends the quest straight away. To shop again, start the quest over.

    Rewards

    If the shop lacks the weapon you want, take the quest again from the start. The same shop can come up again.

    Good to know

    • The earrings must be your own. The Princess of the Fallen Kingdom won't accept earrings that another player gives you or sells you.
    • Shop 1 is the hardest to get. It comes up least often.
    • Search the nearest spots first, and leave the rooftop spots for last.
    • This quest gives no quest points.
