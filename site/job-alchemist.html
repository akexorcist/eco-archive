    Job change

    Requirement: Job Level 30 or higher as a Farmer.

    1. Go to the Guild Palace Lobby 1F in Uptown.
    2. Go inside and take the elevator to the Guild Palace Lobby 3F.
    3. Enter the room on the north side of the 3rd floor. This is the Farmer Guild.

    4. Talk to the Farmer Master and choose to change jobs. He sends you to the Alchemist Master.

      Farmer Master

    5. Take the elevator to the Guild Palace Lobby 4F. Enter the room on the east side. This is the Alchemist Guild.

    6. Talk to the Alchemist Master and choose Take me as your apprentice (the 3rd choice). Then choose to take the test.

      Alchemist Master

    7. Talk to him again with the same choice to start the Chemical Synthesis Skill Test. He asks 10 questions. You answer each one by bringing him the item it asks for. There is no time limit.

      • If you don't have the right item, he repeats the question.
    8. Questions 1 to 5 are always the same. Bring 1 of each item:
      • Heal Potion: Marika Herb and Abori Herb, from Herb
      • Magic Potion: Ril Herb and Regna Herb, from Herb
      • Stamina Potion: Gikogiko Herb and Rune Herb, from Herb and Herb
      • Multi Condition Drink: Miti Herb, from Herb
      • Resurrection Potion: Tamtam Herb, from Herb
      • All four kinds of Herb plants grow on Momo Plains and Breadbasket.
    9. Questions 6 to 10 come from one of three sets, chosen at random. Bring 1 of each item for your set:
      • Set 1
        • Leather Conditioner: Ether, sold by the North Vending Shopkeeper in Downtown
        • Sleeping Potion: Wild Rosebush Thorn, from elemental flowers such as Fire Flower and Earth Flower
        • Hair Color: Carrier Oil, from Crash Ice on Cape Northerin and Snov Fork
        • Light Perfume: White Petal, from Light Flower
        • Molten Gold: Poison Potion, made from Spider Wine and Distilled Water
      • Set 2
        • Paint Remover: Colorless Powder, made from 4 × Jerico and 1 × Dry Powder
        • Mute Potion: Snow Drop, from Pefang and Proon
        • Jerico Potion: Jerico, from Pururu and other Pururus
        • Water Perfume: Blue Petal, from Ice Flower
        • Poison Potion: Spider Wine, from Earth Spider on Lake Utena and Acronia Eastern Beach
      • Set 3
        • Abradant: Pebble, from Rock
        • Stone Curse Potion: Cockatrice Eye, from Cockatrice on Acronia Eastern Beach
        • Hair Loss Treatment: Cuticle Extract, from Fallen Tree and Paper Tree
        • Flame Perfume: Red Petal, from Fire Flower in Staff Desert
        • Glue: Snakehead Fish Sweat, from Snakehead Fish in Continental Cave B5F
    10. After the last right answer, the master offers the job change. Choose to become an Alchemist.
    11. Take off all your equipment first. The master gives you the Alchemist mark, and you are now an Alchemist.
