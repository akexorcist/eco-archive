# Light Tower

Source page: [light-tower.html](light-tower.html)

A tower of many floors, reached by airship from [Morg City](maps.html#map:10060000).

## How to enter

1. Talk to **Billionaire Diermant** in the [Merchant Guild Headquarters](maps.html#map:10060000), [Morg City](maps.html#map:10060000), and choose to **buy an airship ticket**. The ticket costs Lv × 100 G (8,000 G at Lv 80).
   **Billionaire Diermant**
2. Board the Light Tower airship in [Morg City](maps.html#map:10060000). It lands on the [Light Tower Bottom Floor](maps.html#map:20146000).
   [Morg City](maps.html#map:10060000)

- Other airship routes are on [Airships & Rides](airships-and-rides.md).
- To fly back to [Morg City](maps.html#map:10060000), talk to [Airship Staff](maps.html#map:20146000) on the [Light Tower Bottom Floor](maps.html#map:20146000) and board the airship next to him.
   **Airship Staff**
