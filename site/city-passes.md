# City Passes

Source page: [city-passes.html](city-passes.html)

Passes that get you past the guards at the borders and at the gates of [Uptown](maps.html#map:10023000).

## Uptown

- The guards at the gates of [Uptown](maps.html#map:10023000) want an **Acropolis City Pass**.
- Finish **one quest** of any kind, then talk to the [Elegant Lady](maps.html#map:10024000) in [Downtown](maps.html#map:10024000). She gives you an **Acropolis City Pass**.
   **Elegant Lady**
- The way down to [Downtown](maps.html#map:10024000) is in the small passages beside each of the four [Uptown](maps.html#map:10023000) gates.
- A guard turns you away while someone is possessing you.

## Good to know

- **Each crossing uses up one pass,** except the **Fareast Republic Pass**, which you only show once.
- **The first delivery quest to [Upper Iron City](maps.html#map:10063100) lets you through for free.** Take it at the Quest Counter (**Cafe Master** in the [Cafe](maps.html#map:10024000), [Downtown](maps.html#map:10024000)) instead of buying a pass.
