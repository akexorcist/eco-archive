# Knight

Source page: [job-knight.html](job-knight.html)

Expert job · Fighter

A main tank job that guards against every attack type and heals and revives allies with light skills.

## Job change

**Requirement:** STR 30 or higher and Job Level 30 or higher as a Fencer.

1. Go to the [Guild Palace Lobby 1F](maps.html#map:10023000) in [Uptown](maps.html#map:10023000).
2. Go inside and take the **elevator** to the [Guild Palace Lobby 2F](maps.html#map:10023000).
3. Enter the room on the **west side** of the 2nd floor. This is the [Fencer Guild](maps.html#map:10023000).
4. Talk to the **Fencer Master** and ask to change jobs. He asks you to get a certificate from **Mr. Bong** in the [Iron City Cafe](maps.html#map:10066000), [Lower Part of Iron City](maps.html#map:10066000).
   **Fencer Master**
5. Travel to the [Lower Part of Iron City](maps.html#map:10066000).
6. Enter the [Iron City Cafe](maps.html#map:10066000) and talk to **Mr. Bong**. He asks you to bring **1** × [Canned Ice](materials.html#item:10033904).
   **Mr. Bong**
7. Hunt [Ogre](monsters.html#monster:10340300) in the [Northern Dungeon](maps.html#map:20014000) or [Fury](monsters.html#monster:26010900) in the [Eastern Dungeon](maps.html#map:20090000) for the [Canned Ice](materials.html#item:10033904).
8. Return to **Mr. Bong** in the [Iron City Cafe](maps.html#map:10066000) ([Lower Part of Iron City](maps.html#map:10066000)) and hand over the [Canned Ice](materials.html#item:10033904). Talk to him again to get the quest **Like the Wind (Party)**.
   - Party members who take the same quest share the kill count.
9. Travel to [Iron Volcano](maps.html#map:10064000). Defeat [Lava Pururu](monsters.html#monster:10000801) **×5** and [Gigo](monsters.html#monster:10140000) **×3** within **10 minutes**.
10. Return to **Mr. Bong** in the [Iron City Cafe](maps.html#map:10066000) and talk to him. He gives you a **Certificate(Quest)**.
11. Return to the **Fencer Master** in the [Fencer Guild](maps.html#map:10023000) ([Guild Palace Lobby 2F](maps.html#map:10023000), west room).
12. **Take off all your equipment** and talk to the **Fencer Master**. He hands you the Knight mark, and you are now a Knight.
