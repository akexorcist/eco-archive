# Everlasting Union

Source page: [story-saga15.html](story-saga15.html)

A bonus Main Story from SAGA 15: you hunt for **Hastur** and rescue [Zanaf](maps.html#map:10031000).

## Story

Your adventure takes you to the [Acronia Western Plains](maps.html#map:10022000) to meet **Lux**, followed by a stop at **Emil's house** in [Downtown](maps.html#map:10024000) to look for your companions. Returning to the plains to search for clues, your trail soon points toward **Hastur**.

You head to [Acronia Hill](maps.html#map:10013000) to ask the **Caravan Merchant** for leads, which eventually guide you across the water to the [Island of the Undead](maps.html#map:10019000). There, you track down **Hastur** and rescue [Zanaf](maps.html#map:10031000), who had fallen under **Hastur**'s possession.

## Characters

**Lux**

The first person you go to see, in [Acronia Western Plains](maps.html#map:10022000) at the start of the story.

**Hastur**

The one you hunt from [Acronia Hill](maps.html#map:10013000) to [Island of the Undead](maps.html#map:10019000).

[Zanaf](maps.html#map:10031000)

Possessed by **Hastur**; you come to the rescue on [Island of the Undead](maps.html#map:10019000).

**Caravan Merchant**

You ask about **Hastur** in [Acronia Hill](maps.html#map:10013000).
