# Champion Battle

Source page: [champion-battle.html](champion-battle.html)

Player-versus-player fighting on some Dominion World maps.

## How to join

1. Talk to a [Battle Tuner](maps.html#map:12021000) and choose to **join the Champion Battle**. One stands in each of these places:
   - [Battle Tuner](maps.html#map:12021000) in [West Fort City](maps.html#map:12021000)
      **Battle Tuner**
   - [Battle Tuner](maps.html#map:12058000) in [Sky Tower Island](maps.html#map:12058000)
      **Battle Tuner**
2. Go to one of the [battle maps](#battle-maps) and fight other participants.

## Rules

- You can possess only other participants. Only participants can pick up a possession item.
- Dying in the battle costs EXP as usual.
- Some skills work differently against players: see [PvP Skill Changes](pvp-skill-changes.md).

## WRP

WRP (War Record Point) is your Champion Battle score, shown in the status window. You earn it by defeating:

- Monsters in the Dominion World
- Other participants:
   - A higher-level player: the level difference + 10 WRP
   - A player of the same or a lower level: 5 WRP

In a party, the WRP is split between the members on the same map near the fight, at least 1 each. The defeated player loses half the amount.

Check your rank on a **WRP Ranking** board. One stands next to each [Battle Tuner](maps.html#map:12021000):

- **WRP Ranking** in [West Fort City](maps.html#map:12021000)
   **WRP Ranking**
- **WRP Ranking** in [Sky Tower Island](maps.html#map:12058000)
   **WRP Ranking**

## Field Champions

- The top 10 of the WRP ranking are **Field Champions**.
- A Field Champion gets a **Champion Mark** from either [Battle Tuner](maps.html#map:12021000). If you lose it, talk to a [Battle Tuner](maps.html#map:12021000) again.
- Wear the **Champion Mark** to use its champion skill. It gives every player around you **Champion Monster Killer** for 3 minutes: full damage against Champ DEM.
- In the [City Defense Battle](city-defense-battle.md), Field Champions earn 1.5 × the CP.
