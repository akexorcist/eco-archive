    Job change

    Requirement: Job Level 30 or higher as a Merchant.

    1. Go to the Guild Palace Lobby 1F in Uptown.
    2. Go inside and take the elevator to the Guild Palace Lobby 4F.
    3. Enter the room on the north side of the 4th floor. This is the Merchant Guild.

    4. Talk to the Merchant Master, choose to change your job and accept the trial. He asks you to bring 10 × Crest Paper to Gordo, the guild's top trader.

      Merchant Master

    5. Make the five deliveries in this order. Each delivery tells you where the next one goes.

      • If you talk to Gordo out of order, he only acts as a shop.

    6. Bring 10 × Crest Paper to Busy Gordo in the Northern Cafe in Northern Promenade.

      • Crest Paper drops from undead monsters such as Skeleton Archer and Skeleton.
      Busy Gordo

    7. Bring 10 × Antidote Fruit to Shivering Gordo in the Igloo in Eternal North Boundary.

      • Antidote Fruit drops from Working Bee and Green Pururu.
      Shivering Gordo

    8. Bring 10 × Giant Barley Flour to Kind Gordo in the Commoner's House in East Cape.

      • The Grocery Shop Old Lady sells Giant Barley Flour. You can also make it from Giant Barley with Item Refining (recipe).
      Kind Gordo

    9. Bring 10 × Bottled Oil to Franz in the Power Control Room in the Lower Part of Iron City. Serious Gordo waits there too.

      • Bottled Oil drops from Coal Tar and Oily.
      Franz

    10. Bring 10 × Bouquet to the Guild Palace Guide in the Guild Palace Lobby 4F. Abetting Gordo waits beside her.

      • Make each Bouquet from 4 × Flower with Item Refining (recipe). Flowers come from Flower gathering points.
      Guild Palace Guide

    11. Return to the Merchant Master in the Merchant Guild (Guild Palace Lobby 4F, north room) and choose to become a Trader.

    12. When asked, take off all your equipment. The master gives you the Trader mark, and you are now a Trader.
