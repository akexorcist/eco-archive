# Tailoring

Source page: [tailoring.html](tailoring.html)

[Tailoring](job-farmer.md#skill:2054) sews thread, cloth and leather into clothes, hats and bags.

## How it works

- [Tailoring](job-farmer.md#skill:2054) is a [Farmer](job-farmer.md) skill, learned at Job Level 25, with 5 levels.
- Each skill level opens the recipes of that level. The [Crafting](crafting.md) page lists every [Tailoring](job-farmer.md#skill:2054) recipe.
- Most clothes, hats and bags succeed 100% of the time. Materials such as thread and fabric succeed 99%.

## Sew with an NPC

Talk to the **Seamstress** in the [Seamstress' House](maps.html#map:10023000), [Uptown](maps.html#map:10023000), with the materials and the tool in your inventory. She sews every recipe up to Lv 5, and she also cooks.

The fee depends on the recipe level:

| Recipe level | Fee |
|---|---|
| Lv 1 | 100 G |
| Lv 2 | 1,000 G |
| Lv 3 | 2,000 G |
| Lv 4 | 4,000 G |
| Lv 5 | 5,000 G |

**Seamstress**
