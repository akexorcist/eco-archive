## Job change

**Requirement:** Novice, with no stat or level requirement.

1. Enter [Uptown](maps.html#map:10023000). You need an **Acropolis City Pass**.
2. Go to the [Guild Palace Lobby 1F](maps.html#map:10023000) in [Uptown](maps.html#map:10023000).
3. Go inside and take the **elevator** to the [Guild Palace Lobby 3F](maps.html#map:10023000).
4. Enter the room on the **west (left-hand) side** of the 3rd floor. This is the [Blacksmith Guild](maps.html#map:10023000).
5. Talk to the **Miner Master** and choose to become a **Miner (Tatarabe)**.
   **Miner Master**
6. Accept his request: bring **3** × [Iron Lump](materials.html#item:10015600).
7. Hunt [Iron Ore Rock](monsters.html#monster:30010000) for **3** × [Metallic Stone](materials.html#item:10014800). [Iron Ore Rock](monsters.html#monster:30010000) is found on [Acronia Southern Plains](maps.html#map:10031000), [Acronia Northern Plains](maps.html#map:10014000), [Acronia Western Plains](maps.html#map:10022000) and [Acronia Hill](maps.html#map:10013000).
8. Take the [Metallic Stone](materials.html#item:10014800) to the **Weaponsmith** in [Downtown](maps.html#map:10024000) and choose to have metal refined. Each refine costs **100 G** and turns 1 × [Metallic Stone](materials.html#item:10014800) into 1 × [Iron Lump](materials.html#item:10015600).
   **Weaponsmith**
9. Return to the **Miner Master** in the [Blacksmith Guild](maps.html#map:10023000) ([Guild Palace Lobby 3F](maps.html#map:10023000), west room) and hand over 3 × [Iron Lump](materials.html#item:10015600).
10. When asked, **take off your armor**.
11. Talk to him again. You are now a Miner (Tatarabe) and receive a [Miner Bandana](equipment.html#item:50021001) and a [Miner Furnace](materials.html#item:10033100). You also learn [Ore Knowledge](job-miner.md#skill:800).
