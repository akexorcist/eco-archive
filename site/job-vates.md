## Job change

**Requirement:** MAG 10 or higher. Titania characters meet this from the start.

1. Enter [Uptown](maps.html#map:10023000). You need an **Acropolis City Pass**.
2. Go to the [White Church](maps.html#map:10023000) in [Uptown](maps.html#map:10023000).
3. Talk to the **White Church Vates** and choose to become a **Vates**.
   **White Church Vates**
4. **Emil and Dominion only:** travel to [Acronia Eastern Beach](maps.html#map:10034000) and talk to the [Light Fairy](maps.html#map:10034000). Then return to the **White Church Vates**. Titania characters skip this step.
   **Light Fairy**
5. The **White Church Vates** sends you to [Sailor](maps.html#map:10024000) in [Downtown](maps.html#map:10024000). Talk to her and choose to hear about healing magic.
   **Sailor**
6. Return to the **White Church Vates** in the [White Church](maps.html#map:10023000) ([Uptown](maps.html#map:10023000)). Answer his three questions: **I want to heal as many people as possible** → **Light Fairy** → **Yes**.
   - If you give a wrong answer, talk to [Sailor](maps.html#map:10024000) again before you retry.
7. Choose to become a Vates. When asked, **take off your armor**.
8. Talk to him again. You are now a Vates and receive a [Holy Hat](equipment.html#item:50021300). You also learn [Heal](job-vates.md#skill:3054).
