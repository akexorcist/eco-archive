# Races

Source page: [races.html](races.html)

The four races you can choose when you create a character. The race mostly changes your starting stats.

## Starting stats

### Emil

Fighter, Backpacker

STR **8**

DEX **3**

INT **3**

VIT **10**

AGI **4**

MAG **3**

- The highest **VIT** and **HP** at the start, which makes Emil the easiest race for beginners.
- The only race that can wear **[backpacks](equipment.html#backpack)**. Other races use waist bags, which hold less.

### Titania

Spell User

STR **6**

DEX **3**

INT **6**

VIT **4**

AGI **2**

MAG **10**

- Starts with **MAG 10**, so a Titania can become a [Wizard](job-wizard.md) or [Vates](job-vates.md) at level 1.
- Carries less because of its wings.

### Dominion

Fighter, Spell User

STR **10**

DEX **5**

INT **1**

VIT **1**

AGI **5**

MAG **8**

- Starts with **STR 10** and good **MAG**, the best attack stats of any race.
- Starts with only **VIT 1** and **INT 1**, so it has the least HP at the start.

### DEM

Uses DEMIC chips instead of jobs

STR **5**

DEX **5**

INT **5**

VIT **5**

AGI **5**

MAG **5**

- DEM has no jobs. It learns skills from all jobs by setting [DEMIC chips](demic-chips.md).
- All six stats start at **5**.

## Reincarnation

Emil, Titania and Dominion characters can reincarnate into a high race and get a Chronicle job.

- **Requirements:**
   - Level 100 or higher
   - Job Level 50
   - Finish the [Reincarnation Quest](quest-reincarnation.md) in [Northern Promenade](maps.html#map:10065000)
- **Bonus:** your choices depend on your highest level, Lv 100–110. Switch effects, wings, halos and tails in the **Rebirth window**, opened from the equipment window.

### High Emil

**Bonus options**

- A new effect
- A Catty, Medamas or Cait Sith pet or marionette

**In game**

- Backpack 1–3: Catty Medama Keeper, Assault, General
- Marionette 1–3: Cat Doll in purple, blue, orange
- Partner 1: [Black Cat](partners.html#item:10084400)
- Partner 2: Cait Sith, also as a ride
- Not shown: Grace, Aureola, Ankle Feather (particle effects)
