# Ride Crawler Quest

Source page: [quest-ride-crawler.html](quest-ride-crawler.html)

Raise a **Petite Crawler** into a **Ride Crawler** you can ride.

## Before you start

- [Pierre](maps.html#map:10057000) in [Fareast City](maps.html#map:10057000) asks for one particular pet. Where to find them:
   | Pet | Where |
   |---|---|
   | **Puppy** | The pet shop at [East Cape](maps.html#map:10018000) |
   | **Bawoo Bawoo** | A very rare drop from [Crimson Bawoo](monsters.html#monster:10120100) |
   | **Roque Bird** | A drop from [Thunderbird](monsters.html#monster:10140400) |
   | **Pegasus** | A drop from [Vega](monsters.html#monster:10640000) |
   | **Momo** | From its quest |

## Walkthrough

### 1. Meet the Riding Crawler

- Go to [Momo Plains](maps.html#map:10056000) and talk to the [Riding Crawler](maps.html#map:10056000), next to the [Pet Breeder](maps.html#map:10056000).
   **Riding Crawler**

### 2. Trade a pet with Pierre

- Go to [Fareast City](maps.html#map:10057000) and talk to [Pierre](maps.html#map:10057000), who stands with a group of small crawlers. He asks for one pet.
   **Pierre**
- If his pet is hard to find, answer that you **promise to bring it**, then talk to him again. He offers to change his request **once**. This doesn't work if he first asked for the **Puppy** or **Momo**.
- Give him the pet. He gives you a **Petite Crawler**.

### 3. Raise your crawler

- Raise the **Petite Crawler** as your partner. See [Pet Raising](pet-raising.md) for how partners grow.
- Its Base Lv, Partner Rank and Trust carry over to the Ride Crawler.

### 4. Turn it into a Ride Crawler

- Leave the **Petite Crawler** with the [Pet Breeder](maps.html#map:10056000) on [Momo Plains](maps.html#map:10056000). It comes back as a **Ride Crawler**. The colour pattern is random.
   **Pet Breeder**

## Good to know

- **Riding hides you from most monsters.** Monsters that attack first ignore you, except bosses and monsters in dungeons.
- **It carries a lot but isn't fast.** A Ride Crawler is slower than a dragon.
