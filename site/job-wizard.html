    Job change

    Requirement: MAG 10 or higher. Titania characters meet this from the start.

    1. Enter Uptown. You need an Acropolis City Pass.
    2. Go to the Guild Palace Lobby 1F in Uptown.
    3. Go inside and take the elevator to the Guild Palace Lobby 3F.
    4. Enter the room on the east side of the 3rd floor. This is the Spell User Guild.

    5. Talk to the Spell User Master and choose Wizard.

      Spell User Master

    6. When asked, take off your armor.

    7. You are now a Wizard and receive an Opal Pendant.
