# Soul Taker

Source page: [job-soultaker.html](job-soultaker.html)

Chronicle job · Spell User

A job with very strong dark area spells that also raises the party's MATK and can summon Fenrir.

## Job change

**Requirement:** Lv 100 or higher, Job Level 50 in either [Cabalist](job-cabalist.md) or [Necromancer](job-necromancer.md), and at least one win over the [Emil Dragon](monsters.html#monster:14520000).

1. Travel to [Northern Promenade](maps.html#map:10065000). When you meet the requirements, a **mysterious creature** (a cat) appears there for you to talk to. If it doesn't appear, you don't meet them yet.
2. Talk to the creature until it runs away.
   **? ? ?**
3. Go to the [Northern Cafe](maps.html#map:10065000) in [Northern Promenade](maps.html#map:10065000) and talk to **Valeria**.
   **Valeria**
4. Chase the creature: find it again in [Northern Promenade](maps.html#map:10065000) and follow it into the [Queen Audience Room](maps.html#map:10065000).
5. Follow the creature into the **magic circle**.
6. In the [White World](maps.html#map:10065000), walk straight ahead to the creature sleeping there. It asks you 3 questions.
   **? ? ?**
   - Answers: choose **not to wake it**, then answer that **it is real**. Any answer works for the third question.
   - A wrong answer sends you back. Walk to the creature again.
7. In the room with the book ([White World](maps.html#map:10065000)), talk to **Mucha**.
   **Mucha**
8. Look inside the **White Book**. It sends you to face **Renette**, the opponent of the Warlock line. Then return to the room with the book.
   **White Book**
9. Talk to **Mucha** again.
10. Look inside the **White Book** and choose **Moment of Emptiness (Time)**. There you earn two proofs of strength: the **Hero Proof** and the **Soul Proof**.
11. Defeat the monsters in that room for **small fire orbs** and **large fire orbs**.
12. Combine **50** small fire orbs or **1** large fire orb into **1 talisman**. A golem there also sells talismans.
   - The talisman comes out **red** or **blue** at random.
   - **Warlock line:** you need the **red** one, like every Spell User line.
13. Use the talisman to fight a **copy of yourself**. You have **10 minutes**.
   - **Warlock line:** you fight the copy.
   - Each talisman works once. Everyone who enters needs their own talisman and the quest.
14. Fight **Renette**, the opponent of your line.
   **Renette**
   - HP regenerates on its own on this map.
15. Look inside the **White Book** again. **Take off your equipment** first. You become a Soul Taker and reincarnate into the higher form of your race (see [Races](races.md)). Your level and Job Level go back to **1**.
   - You keep your skills, but you raise them again.

**Job Lv 45 Summon Fenrir Active physical attack · Max Lv 3**

- Changes behavior by skill level.
- Lv 1 attempts to instantly defeat a non-boss target and stores that target's level for 5 minutes if it succeeds.
- The success chance is caster Lv - target Lv + 30% , clamped between 10% and 100%; against player targets, the chance is capped at 45%.
- Lv 2 consumes the stored Fenrir mark to heal the caster.
- Lv 3 consumes the stored Fenrir mark to deal unavoidable physical damage in a 5x5 ground area.

| Lv | MP | Cast time | Cooldown | Target/Area | Effect |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | 30 | 1 sec | 1 sec | Single target | Instant defeat attempt on non-boss targets; success stores Fenrir mark for 5 minutes |
| 2 | 75 | 0.5 sec | 0.5 sec | Self | Restores HP, MP, and SP; stronger when consuming a stored Fenrir mark |
| 3 | 120 | 1.5 sec | 2 sec | 5x5 ground area | Unavoidable physical damage, 1,100%-2,000% based on the stored target level difference |
