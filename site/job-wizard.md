# Wizard

Source page: [job-wizard.html](job-wizard.html)

Base job · Spell User

A job whose neutral magic deals steady damage to every monster, with low physical defense.

2. [Sorcerer Expert](job-sorcerer.md)

## Job change

**Requirement:** MAG 10 or higher. Titania characters meet this from the start.

1. Enter [Uptown](maps.html#map:10023000). You need an **Acropolis City Pass**.
2. Go to the [Guild Palace Lobby 1F](maps.html#map:10023000) in [Uptown](maps.html#map:10023000).
3. Go inside and take the **elevator** to the [Guild Palace Lobby 3F](maps.html#map:10023000).
4. Enter the room on the **east side** of the 3rd floor. This is the [Spell User Guild](maps.html#map:10023000).
5. Talk to the **Spell User Master** and choose **Wizard**.
   **Spell User Master**
6. When asked, **take off your armor**.
7. You are now a Wizard and receive an [Opal Pendant](equipment.html#item:50050800).
