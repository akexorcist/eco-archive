# Catty (Sumire) Quest

Source page: [quest-catty-sumire.html](quest-catty-sumire.html)

Bring a runaway boy safely home.

## Before you start

- Finish the [Catty (Momo) Quest](quest-catty-momo.md) and **equip a Catty** for the whole quest.

## Walkthrough

### 1. Hear about the missing boy

- Go to [Downtown](maps.html#map:10024000) and talk to the [Elegant Lady](maps.html#map:10024000). She tells you about a child hiding in the east.
   **Elegant Lady**
- Go to [Acronia Eastern Plains](maps.html#map:10025000) and enter the [East Beginner's School](maps.html#map:10025000). You can enter it for this quest. Talk to the **Teacher**.
   **Teacher**

### 2. Visit Marcia

- Go to [East Cape](maps.html#map:10018000) and enter the [Merchant Shop](maps.html#map:10018000). Talk to the **Guild Merchant** and to **Marcia**. She invites you to her airship.
- In the **Airship Garden**, talk to **Marcia**, then to her pet **Plum**. You get a knife. **Marcia** sends you to [Ghost Buster Gadoo](maps.html#map:10061000).

### 3. Ask Gadoo for help

- Go south past [Cape Southerin](maps.html#map:10046000) to [Ghost Shelter](maps.html#map:10061000) and talk to [Ghost Buster Gadoo](maps.html#map:10061000). He tells you about a specialist in [Downtown](maps.html#map:10024000) who can open a secret path.
   **Ghost Buster Gadoo**

### 4. Into the underground room

- Go to the **northwest house** in [Downtown](maps.html#map:10024000) and talk to **Mr. Specialist**. Choose the **first answer**. He sends you to an underground room full of robots.
   **Mr. Specialist**
- Walk to the end and talk to the boy, **Relo**. A giant robot, **Giganto**, attacks. Defeat it.
   - The room heals you over time, like a town.
   - If you lose, talk to **Mr. Specialist** again and retry.
- Pick up what it drops, break the barrier at the door with it, and talk to **Relo** again.

### 5. Back to Gadoo

- You arrive in the [South Faction Commander's Office](maps.html#map:10023300). Talk to the soldier at the desk, then to **Ghost Buster Gadoo**. He gives you **Catty (Sumire)**.

## Rewards

- **Catty (Sumire)**: the most motherly of the cat spirits. Skills and details on [Catties](catties.md#sumire)

## Good to know

- **Carry all your Catties.** Wear one and keep the others in your bag: they add extra lines to the story.
