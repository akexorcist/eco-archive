    Catty (Ai) Quest

    A cat spirit is trapped in a broken robot. Rebuild the robot to set her free.

    Level
    30 or higher, with 20 Fame
    Items needed
    • The four crystals
    • Heart Ruby
    • Galvanized Iron Body
    • For a new bandage:
      • Cotton Ribbon
      • Cloth
      • Thread
    Reward
    Catty (Ai)
    On this page
    • Before you start
    • Walkthrough
    • Rewards
    • Good to know
    • Related

    Before you start

    • Finish the Catty (Momo) Quest and equip Catty (Momo) for the whole quest.

    • Collect all of these:

      Item How to get it
      Critical Crystal See Upgrade Crystals
      Strength Crystal See Upgrade Crystals
      Magic Crystal See Upgrade Crystals
      Life Crystal See Upgrade Crystals
      Heart Ruby From wooden boxes, or made at the Jewelry Shop in Uptown from a Red Crystal 1 and 4 × Abradant (recipe)
      Galvanized Iron Body Made at the Junk Shop in Downtown from a Motor, an Electell Model and a Pocket Computer. The parts drop from robots
      • Cotton Ribbon
      • Cloth
      • Thread
      		 For a new Seamstress Triangle Bandage at the end

    • The Junk Shopkeeper gives you the Gear for the Electell Model for free.

    • Don't carry a marionette when you talk to the Junk Shopkeeper; he won't talk to you.

    Walkthrough

    1. Find the broken robot

    • Go to Fruit Forest and talk to the Broken Electell on the east side.

      Broken Electell

    • Go to Uptown and talk to the Seamstress. She tells you to ask someone from Tonka.

      Seamstress

    • Leave by the south gate and talk to Niccolo just outside, on the west side of the road. He lists the parts the robot needs.

      Niccolo

    2. Make the heart

    • Go to Downtown and talk to the Junk Shopkeeper. Choose yes to both questions. He turns your Heart Ruby into an Empty Heart.

      Junk Shopkeeper

    • Go to the White Church in Uptown and talk to the White Church Vates. Choose to give your vitality (the first answer). He turns the Empty Heart into a Tin Heart.

      White Church Vates

    • Don't combine the Galvanized Iron Body with the Tin Heart. That makes an Electell Marionette and uses up both.

    3. Repair the robot

    • With all the parts, talk to the Broken Electell again. It takes everything except the bandage materials.
    • Talk to Niccolo again.
    • Talk to the Seamstress again and have her make a new Seamstress Triangle Bandage from your Cotton Ribbon, Cloth and Thread.
    • With the bandage in your bag, talk to the Broken Electell once more and choose yes to pull the plug (the first answer). You receive Catty (Ai).

    Rewards

    • Catty (Ai): skills and details on Catties

    Good to know

    • Make the bandage before the last talk. Without it the robot explodes and you have to start over.
    • Only ask the Seamstress for the bandage during the quest. If you ask her to cut without the quest, she makes pink shorts and uses up your materials.
