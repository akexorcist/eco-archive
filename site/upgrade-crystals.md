# Upgrade Crystals

Source page: [upgrade-crystals.html](upgrade-crystals.html)

Crystals that raise equipment from **+1** up to **+10** through [reinforcement](equipment-upgrades.md#reinforcement).

## Where to find them

| Crystal | Where to get it |
|---|---|
| **Life Crystal** | Drops from [Bawoo](monsters.html#monster:10120000) in [Killer Bee Hill](maps.html#map:10020000) and [Lake Utena](maps.html#map:10032000) Drops from [Amber Jelly](monsters.html#monster:10000800) in [Acronia Western Plains](maps.html#map:10022000) and [Acronia Southern Plains](maps.html#map:10031000) Drops from [Pefang](monsters.html#monster:10440000) in [North Plateau](maps.html#map:10049000) and [North Mountain](maps.html#map:10050000) Found in [Wooden Boxes, Treasure Chests and Containers](boxes.md) One free from the **Ironworks Receptionist** in the [Forge Lobby](maps.html#map:10066000) the first time you ask about reinforcement, once you have 10 Fame |
| **Strength Crystal** | Drops from [ChockKo](monsters.html#monster:10010000) in [Fruit Forest](maps.html#map:10030000) and [Lake Utena](maps.html#map:10032000) Drops from [Draki](monsters.html#monster:10020000) in [Continental Cave B1F](maps.html#map:20000000) and [Continental Cave B2F](maps.html#map:20001000) Drops from [Tarantula](monsters.html#monster:10210002) in [Dead Mine B1F](maps.html#map:20030000) and [Dead Mine B2F](maps.html#map:20031000) Drops from [Cyclops](monsters.html#monster:10340000) in [North Plateau](maps.html#map:10049000) and [North Mountain](maps.html#map:10050000) Found in [Wooden Boxes, Treasure Chests and Containers](boxes.md) |
| [Magic Crystal](materials.html#item:90000045) | Drops from [Milk Pitcher](monsters.html#monster:10000400) in [Acronia Forest](maps.html#map:10015000) and [East Cape](maps.html#map:10018000), the easiest source Drops from [Green Pururu](monsters.html#monster:10000500) in [East Cape](maps.html#map:10018000) and [Acronia Eastern Beach](maps.html#map:10034000) Drops from [Angel Feather](monsters.html#monster:10270000) in [North Plateau](maps.html#map:10049000) and [North Mountain](maps.html#map:10050000) Drops from [Roper](monsters.html#monster:10080000) in [Fruit Forest](maps.html#map:10030000) and [Staff Desert](maps.html#map:10042000) |
| **Critical Crystal** | Drops from [Killer Bee](monsters.html#monster:10040000) in [Killer Bee Hill](maps.html#map:10020000) and [Fruit Forest](maps.html#map:10030000) Drops from [Wasp](monsters.html#monster:10040100) in [Bee Village](maps.html#map:20004002) and [Mothgue Cliff](maps.html#map:10028000) Drops from [Queen Bee](monsters.html#monster:10040200) in [Killer Bee Hill](maps.html#map:10020000) and [Fruit Forest](maps.html#map:10030000) Drops from [Rock Eater](monsters.html#monster:10050102) in [Iron South Street](maps.html#map:10063000) and [Iron Volcano](maps.html#map:10064000) Found in [Wooden Boxes, Treasure Chests and Containers](boxes.md) |
| [Enhanced Fire Crystal](crafting.html#recipe:183) | Crafted from a [Magic Crystal](materials.html#item:90000045) and a [Flame Summonstone](materials.html#item:10011201) with the [Miner (Tatarabe)](job-miner.md) skill [Advanced Metal Refining](job-miner.md#skill:2051) Lv 3 The **Ironworks Receptionist** in the [Forge Lobby](maps.html#map:10066000) can do this refining for you |
| [Enhanced Water Crystal](crafting.html#recipe:184) | Crafted from a [Magic Crystal](materials.html#item:90000045) and a [Water Summonstone](materials.html#item:10011203) the same way |
| [Enhanced Wind Crystal](crafting.html#recipe:185) | Crafted from a [Magic Crystal](materials.html#item:90000045) and a [Wind Summonstone](materials.html#item:10011207) the same way |
| [Enhanced Earth Crystal](crafting.html#recipe:186) | Crafted from a [Magic Crystal](materials.html#item:90000045) and a [Earth Summonstone](materials.html#item:10011205) the same way |
| [Enhanced Light Crystal](crafting.html#recipe:187) | Crafted from a [Magic Crystal](materials.html#item:90000045) and a [Light Summonstone](materials.html#item:10011210) the same way |
| [Enhanced Darkness Crystal](crafting.html#recipe:188) | Crafted from a [Magic Crystal](materials.html#item:90000045) and a [Dark Summonstone](materials.html#item:10011209) the same way Drops from [Catoblepas](monsters.html#monster:16820000) in [Carmine Brick Ruins 1F Center](maps.html#map:20220000) |

The four basic crystals are rare drops, and no NPC shop sells them.
