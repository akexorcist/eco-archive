# Lv 55 Backpacker Hat & Shoes Quest

Source page: [quest-lv55-hat-shoes.html](quest-lv55-hat-shoes.html)

Errands that earn each Backpacker job line its Lv 55 **hat or shoes**.

## Before you start

- Each quest is only for its own job line, including the Expert and Technical jobs of that line.
- The Farmer and Ranger NPCs take **1** of each item.

## Walkthrough

### 1. Farmer line: the Iron City cafe

- Go to the [Iron City Cafe](maps.html#map:10063100) in [Upper Iron City](maps.html#map:10063100) and talk to the **Cafe Employee**. She needs ingredients for her menu.
   **Cafe Employee**
- Bring 1 of each:
   - [Fruit Juice](crafting.html#recipe:431)
   - [Bacon](materials.html#item:10004500)
   - [Boiled Egg](materials.html#item:10007750)
   - [Mushroom Soup](crafting.html#recipe:439)
   - [Pea Pottage](crafting.html#recipe:473)
   - [Mushroom Bread](crafting.html#recipe:440)
   - [Organic Breadfruit Nut](materials.html#item:10006200)
   - [Jam](crafting.html#recipe:448)
   - [Grape Jam](crafting.html#recipe:449)
   - [Organic Fruit Juice](crafting.html#recipe:432)
   - [Delicious Kebab](crafting.html#recipe:442)
   - [Special Stew](crafting.html#recipe:466)
   - [Delicious Curry](crafting.html#recipe:464)
   - [Carrot Stick](crafting.html#recipe:408)
   - [Potato Salad](crafting.html#recipe:447)
   - [Lettuce](materials.html#item:10006800)
   - [Cheese](materials.html#item:10008451)
   - **Pine Mushroom**
   - [Chocolate Chip Biscuit](materials.html#item:10009450)
   - [Calcium Biscuit](crafting.html#recipe:421)
   - [Sweet Coffee](crafting.html#recipe:472)
   - [Marshmallow](crafting.html#recipe:401)
- She gives you ****Farm Lady Rubber Band Hat**** (female) or ****Lumberjack Boots(M)**** (male).

### 2. Merchant line: Ewan's golems

- Go to the [Igloo](maps.html#map:10051000) in [Eternal North Boundary](maps.html#map:10051000) and talk to **Ewan**. He wants to see your golems.
   **Ewan**
- Bring 1 of each golem. He doesn't take them.
   - [Veril Golem](crafting.html#recipe:1307)
   - [Mandragora Golem](crafting.html#recipe:1310)
   - [Ice Golem](crafting.html#recipe:1311)
   - [Insmouse Golem](crafting.html#recipe:1312)
   - [Tiny Golem](crafting.html#recipe:1316)
   - [Electell Golem](crafting.html#recipe:1318)
- He gives you ****Business Shoes(F)**** (female) or ****Business Beret**** (male).

### 3. Ranger line: Hot-Blooded Red's Jericos

- Go to [Battleship Island](maps.html#map:10035000) and talk to [Hot-Blooded Red](maps.html#map:10035000). He collects Jericos.
   **Hot-Blooded Red**
- Bring 1 of each Jerico:
   - [Jerico](materials.html#item:10032800)
   - [Red Jerico](materials.html#item:10032801)
   - [Purple Jerico](materials.html#item:10032802)
   - [Blue Jerico](materials.html#item:10032803)
   - [Sky Blue Jerico](materials.html#item:10032804)
   - [Green Jerico](materials.html#item:10032805)
   - [Lime Green Jerico](materials.html#item:10032806)
   - [Yellow Jerico](materials.html#item:10032807)
   - [Orange Jerico](materials.html#item:10032808)
   - [Black Jerico](materials.html#item:10032809)
   - [Heavy Jerico](crafting.html#recipe:58)
   - [White Jerico](materials.html#item:10032811)
- He gives you ****Safari Boots(F)**** (female) or ****Safari Boots(M)**** (male).
