    Warlock

    Base job · Spell User

    A dark magic job that, unlike other Spell Users, fights well up close and can use shields.

    Job change

    Requirement: MAG 10 or higher.

    1. Enter Uptown. You need an Acropolis City Pass.
    2. Go to the Black Church in Uptown.

    3. Talk to the Black Church Vates and choose to become a Warlock.

      Black Church Vates

    4. Emil and Titania only: travel to the Island of the Undead and talk to Guglielmo. He sends you across to the island. Dominion characters skip steps 4 and 5.

      Guglielmo

    5. Emil and Titania only: talk to the Dark Fairy, then return to the Black Church Vates.

      Dark Fairy

    6. The Black Church Vates sends you to Mr. Black in Downtown. Talk to him and choose to hear about dark magic.

      Mr. Black

    7. Return to the Black Church Vates in the Black Church (Uptown). Answer his three questions: There's nothing like that → Light property → Dagger.

      • If you give a wrong answer, talk to Mr. Black again before you retry.
    8. Choose to become a Warlock. When asked, take off your armor.
    9. Talk to him again. You are now a Warlock and receive a Brooch of the Darkness. You also learn Black Widow.
