# Accessory Crafting

Source page: [accessory-crafting.html](accessory-crafting.html)

Accessory Construction, the craft that makes hair ornaments, glasses, pendants and cut gems.

## How it works

Talk to the **Jewelry Shop** in the [Jewelry Shop](maps.html#map:10023000), [Uptown](maps.html#map:10023000), with the materials and the tool in your inventory. She makes every recipe up to Lv 5.

**Jewelry Shop**

- No job learns Accessory Construction.
- Almost every recipe is free. Only the [Mark of the Familiar](crafting.html#recipe:2540) recipes cost 5,000 G.
- Most accessories come out as the normal item 91% of the time. The other 9% come out better:
   - 5%: the +1 version, such as **Red Round-Rimmed Glasses +1**
   - 3%: the +2 version
   - 1%: the +3 version
- Cut gems always succeed.
- The [Crafting](crafting.md) page lists every Accessory Construction recipe.

## Glasses

Each recipe in the table lists the parts it needs. Most glasses use a lens and a frame; the [Snorkel](equipment.html#item:50041300) uses only a lens.

### Glass Lenses

- Make [Glass Lenses](materials.html#item:10046800) ([Lv 3](crafting.html#recipe:749)):
   - 1 × [Crystal](materials.html#item:10014300)
   - 4 × [Abradant](materials.html#item:10045800)

### Glass Frame

1. Make [Titanium Wire](materials.html#item:10045200) ([Lv 3](crafting.html#recipe:752)):
   - 1 × [Titanium Rod](materials.html#item:10040001), made with [Advanced Metal Refining](job-miner.md#skill:2051)
2. Make [Glass Frame](materials.html#item:10041100) ([Lv 3](crafting.html#recipe:753)):
   - 1 × [Titanium Wire](materials.html#item:10045200)
   - [Pliers](materials.html#item:10023700) (tool)
