# Endless Corridor Quests

Source page: [quest-endless-corridor.html](quest-endless-corridor.html)

Party quests in the [Endless Corridor](endless-corridor.md) that reward skill points.

## How the corridor works

- Talk to the [D Master](maps.html#map:10042000) at the entrance to take a quest and choose the floor to start from.
   **D Master**
- Starting deeper costs gold: B1F is free, B11F costs 5,000 G, B21F 10,000 G and B31F 15,000 G.
- To go down a floor, defeat a monster with **Alcana** in its name and pick up its Alcana Ring. Each floor's ring only opens the next floor. Everyone in your party needs their own ring.
- To leave, step back into the portal you came down and choose to go out. You return to the entrance and can't go back to that floor.
- Everyone in a party with the same quest shares the kill: whoever defeats the target, the quest counts for all.
- Alcana Rings vanish when you go down a floor, even if you carry several.

## Below B40F

- Once you have reached **B41F**, the [D Master](maps.html#map:10042000) can send you straight there.
- The B41F lobby leads to the **Middle floors** (B42F to B71F) and the **Lower floors** (B72F and below).
- Only Chronicle jobs can enter the Lower floors.
- The **Lower Class Warrior** gives the Middle floor quests and the **Senior Warrior** the Lower floor quests. They pay **Mission Ticket**.

## Collect the skill points

- Go to the east side of [Uptown](maps.html#map:10023000) and give each ring to the [Old Man](maps.html#map:10023000). He gives you **1 skill point per ring**.
   **Old Man**
- Giving him the same ring again gives you an item instead.
