    PvP Skill Changes

    How some skills work differently when you fight other players.

    On this page
    • Related

    These changes apply in Arena PvP, the Champion Battle and Knight Training.

    Skill In PvP
    • God Speed Slash
    • Zillion Blade
    • Light of Darkness
    • Setsuna
    • Tempest Shot
    • Destruction Glare
    • Damnation
    • Railgun
    • Mistletoe Shot
    • Time is Money
    		 Half damage.
    • Kensei
    • Quick Slash Combo 1
    • Quick Slash Combo 2
    • Quick Slash Combo 3
    • Head Crusher
    • Lumberjack Slash
    • Eagle Strike
    • Swallow Snatcher
    • Beast Jawbreaker
    • Specter Sword
    • Brandish
    • Omnislash
    		 With Kensei, these skills get extra damage. Against players, that extra damage is halved. Their normal damage is unchanged.
    • ATK Communion
    • DEF Communion
    • MATK Communion
    • MDEF Communion
    • HIT Communion
    • AVOID Communion
    • C.SPD Communion
    		 A smaller bonus for the SP spent.
    Elemental Rain Each elemental hit does 75% of its damage.
    Plant Edge 75% damage and no Stiffness.
    Black Rose Thorn Harvest Master doesn't raise the reflected damage.
    Divine Breaker Uses the normal DEF calculation.
    • Supportive Blaze Arrow
    • Supportive Aqua Arrow
    • Supportive Terra Arrow
    • Supportive Zephyr Arrow
    		 The element DEF lasts twice as long.
    Vital Shot The stat-down lasts 30 seconds instead of 15.
    Closeout Lasts 60 seconds at Lv 1 down to 5 seconds at Lv 5 (normally 180 down to 15).
    Shadow Stitch Stiffness 25% to 45%, lasting 6 seconds at Lv 1 down to 2 seconds at Lv 5.
    Venom Blast The Stun lasts 5 seconds instead of 15.
    Sweep Attack Stiffness chance 15% to 35% (normally 50% to 90%).
    Bumrush An extra 13% miss chance before the Stiffness.
    Material Bind Half the chance to take effect.
    Artful Traps Triggers at 15% instead of 40%. The Lv 4 Stun lasts 3 seconds instead of 10.
    False Money Each debuff lands at 15% instead of 50%.
    Expansion Arm Clears buffs at 30% instead of 70%.
    Weakness Shot Takes effect at 30% instead of 50%.
    Freak Blast Mystic Silence, Mystic Poison and Medusa Eyes add +5% per level instead of +12%.
    Evaporate Success chance 5% + 4% × Lv (normally 10% + 10% × Lv).
    Hate Steal Also cancels the target's cast.
    Reflect Shield Triggers at most once every 3 seconds.
    Blocking Can't be used while you are inside a possession.
    Pressure All your Pressure fields share one limit of 3.
    Increase Human Property Dodge Lowers your dodge against players instead (-3 at Lv 1 to -15 at Lv 5).
    Engraving The waiting mark doesn't hold players with Stiffness.
