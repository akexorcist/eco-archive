    Lv 65 Armor Quest

    The Queen of the North, Beldegard, lets trusted adventurers take Lv 65 armor from the palace treasury.

    Level
    60 or higher
    Quest points
    1 point each:
    • Escaped Rabbit (Party)
    Repeatable
    Yes. Do the hunt once for every piece you want
    Reward
    One Lv 65 armor piece for your job per Queen Key
    On this page
    • Before you start
    • Walkthrough
    • Good to know
    • Related

    Before you start

    • Bring a party. The giant Minidoo is a party fight.
    • Most jobs need 2 to 4 hunts for the full set.

    Walkthrough

    1. Get your first Queen Key

    • Go to Northern Promenade and climb to the top of the town, to the Queen Audience Room. Talk to the queen, Beldegard. She gives you a Queen Key. You only get this free key once.

      Beldegard

    2. Open the treasury

    • Go back down to the Northern Palace Ballroom, the room before the Queen's. Talk to the Guardswoman in the bottom-right corner and choose to enter the treasury.

      Guardswoman

    • In the Repositorium, open any chest with your first key. It gives you a Resurrection Potion.

    3. Hunt the giant Minidoo

    • Talk to the Guardswoman again and take her hunt quest, Escaped Rabbit (Party). It costs 1 quest point and has no time limit. A party can take it together.
    • Go to the bottom-left area of Northern Dungeon and defeat the giant Minidoo.
      • It doesn't attack first.
      • It grows bigger and hits harder the more you hit it.
      • Near death it blows itself up, hitting everyone nearby for about 2,000 damage. If at least one party member survives, the quest still counts.
    • Report back to the Guardswoman. She gives you another Queen Key.

    4. Open a chest

    • Enter the Repositorium and open the chest for the piece you want. Each key opens one chest and gives you the piece for your job.

    • Party members don't all need the quest. Friends below level 60 can still join the fight.
