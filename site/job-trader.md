## Job change

**Requirement:** Job Level 30 or higher as a Merchant.

1. Go to the [Guild Palace Lobby 1F](maps.html#map:10023000) in [Uptown](maps.html#map:10023000).
2. Go inside and take the **elevator** to the [Guild Palace Lobby 4F](maps.html#map:10023000).
3. Enter the room on the **north side** of the 4th floor. This is the [Merchant Guild](maps.html#map:10023000).
4. Talk to the **Merchant Master**, choose to change your job and accept the trial. He asks you to bring **10** × [Crest Paper](materials.html#item:10024900) to **Gordo**, the guild's top trader.
   **Merchant Master**
5. Make the five deliveries **in this order**. Each delivery tells you where the next one goes.
   - If you talk to **Gordo** out of order, he only acts as a shop.
6. Bring **10** × [Crest Paper](materials.html#item:10024900) to **Busy Gordo** in the [Northern Cafe](maps.html#map:10065000) in [Northern Promenade](maps.html#map:10065000).
   **Busy Gordo**
   - [Crest Paper](materials.html#item:10024900) drops from undead monsters such as [Skeleton Archer](monsters.html#monster:10350000) and [Skeleton](monsters.html#monster:10190000).
7. Bring **10** × **Antidote Fruit** to **Shivering Gordo** in the [Igloo](maps.html#map:10051000) in [Eternal North Boundary](maps.html#map:10051000).
   **Shivering Gordo**
   - **Antidote Fruit** drops from [Working Bee](monsters.html#monster:10040003) and [Green Pururu](monsters.html#monster:10000500).
8. Bring **10** × [Giant Barley Flour](materials.html#item:10001100) to **Kind Gordo** in the [Commoner's House](maps.html#map:10018000) in [East Cape](maps.html#map:10018000).
   **Kind Gordo**
   - The [Grocery Shop Old Lady](maps.html#map:10025000) sells [Giant Barley Flour](materials.html#item:10001100). You can also make it from [Giant Barley](materials.html#item:10005900) with [Item Refining](job-miner.md#skill:2009) ([recipe](crafting.html#recipe:5)).
9. Bring **10** × [Bottled Oil](materials.html#item:10000701) to **Franz** in the [Power Control Room](maps.html#map:10066000) in the [Lower Part of Iron City](maps.html#map:10066000). **Serious Gordo** waits there too.
   **Franz**
   - [Bottled Oil](materials.html#item:10000701) drops from [Coal Tar](monsters.html#monster:10000900) and [Oily](monsters.html#monster:10000901).
10. Bring **10** × [Bouquet](materials.html#item:10005400) to the **Guild Palace Guide** in the [Guild Palace Lobby 4F](maps.html#map:10023000). **Abetting Gordo** waits beside her.
   **Guild Palace Guide**
   - Make each [Bouquet](materials.html#item:10005400) from **4** × [Flower](materials.html#item:10005300) with [Item Refining](job-miner.md#skill:2009) ([recipe](crafting.html#recipe:41)). Flowers come from [Flower](monsters.html#monster:30210000) gathering points.
11. Return to the **Merchant Master** in the [Merchant Guild](maps.html#map:10023000) ([Guild Palace Lobby 4F](maps.html#map:10023000), north room) and choose to become a **Trader**.
12. When asked, **take off all your equipment**. The master gives you the Trader mark, and you are now a Trader.
