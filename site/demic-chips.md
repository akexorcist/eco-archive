# DEMIC Chips

Source page: [demic-chips.html](demic-chips.html)

Chips that [DEM](races.md) characters set to learn skills, since they have no jobs.

## Where to buy

- Talk to the **Lab Guide** in [Downtown](maps.html#map:10024000) and choose **DEM Customize Room**.
   **Lab Guide**
- Or talk to [Snub](maps.html#map:12021000) in [West Fort City](maps.html#map:12021000).
   **Snub**
- In the [DEM Customize Room](maps.html#map:10024000), buy chips from **Dr. Quark**.
   **Dr. Quark**

## How DEM grow

- A DEM's level alone doesn't raise its stats. Spend **EP** to raise the **Cost Limit**, which lets you set more chips.
- Setting an **Engage Task** on the panel costs 1 EP. It doubles the chips next to it.
- Chips work only in **Machina Form**. In Normal Form you wear normal equipment instead.
