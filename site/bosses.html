    Good to know

    • Party drops go to everyone. Some boss drops are party drops: each party member gets one.
    • Stay within 20 levels of the boss. Party drops are lost when your level is 20 or more away from the boss, except for bosses of Lv 130 and higher.
    • Bosses come back. A defeated boss appears again after a while.
