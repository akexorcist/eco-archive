# Factions

Source page: [factions.html](factions.html)

The four armies of Acropolis you can join.

## Joining a faction

Join a faction by talking to its commander. You need at least 1 [Fame](fame.md). Choose to **join** the faction, then **sign** the form.

- **East Faction:** the **East Faction Commander** in the [East Faction Commander's Office](maps.html#map:10023100), through the [East Faction Command Center Entrance](maps.html#map:10023100) on the [Acropolis East Bridge](maps.html#map:10023100).
   **East Faction Commander**
- **West Faction:** the **West Faction Commander** in the [West Faction Commander's Office](maps.html#map:10023200), through the [West Faction Command Center Entrance](maps.html#map:10023200) on the [Acropolis West Bridge](maps.html#map:10023200).
   **West Faction Commander**
- **South Faction:** the **South Faction Commander** in the [South Faction Commander's Office](maps.html#map:10023300), through the [South Faction Command Center Entrance](maps.html#map:10023300) on the [Acropolis South Bridge](maps.html#map:10023300).
   **South Faction Commander**
- **North Faction:** the **North Faction Commander** in the [North Faction Commander's Office](maps.html#map:10023400), through the [North Faction Command Center Entrance](maps.html#map:10023400) on the [Acropolis North Bridge](maps.html#map:10023400).
   **North Faction Commander**

When you join, the commander gives you your faction's Knight Card and an **Acropolis City Pass**. Once you have **30 Fame**, talk to your commander again for your faction's leather armor.

| Faction | Knight Card | Armor at 30 Fame |
|---|---|---|
| East Faction | **Fareast Knight Card** | **East Faction Leather Armor** |
| West Faction | **Morg Knight Card** | **Western Faction Leather Armor** |
| South Faction | **Iron South Knight Card** | **South Faction Leather Armor** |
| North Faction | **Northern Knight Card** | **North Faction Leather Armor** |

- You can't leave your faction or change to another one.
- You don't need to have joined a faction to take part in [Knight Training](knight-training.md). The side you fight for in a battle doesn't have to be your own faction.

## Gatling Cannon License

Only **South Faction** members can get the **Gatling Cannon License(Event)**, which lets you buy a [Gatling Cannon](partners.html#item:10018900).

1. Talk to the **Receptionist** in the [Joint Committee Office Lobby](maps.html#map:10063100), [Upper Iron City](maps.html#map:10063100). Choose the **Gatling Cannon sales license** and show your **Iron South Knight Card**. Pay **9,200 G** for the license.
   **Receptionist**
2. Talk to the **Factory Receptionist** in the [Factory Lobby](maps.html#map:10066000), [Lower Part of Iron City](maps.html#map:10066000). Choose **buy a Gatling Cannon** and show the license.
   **Factory Receptionist**
   - Pay **300,000 G** for the [Gatling Cannon](partners.html#item:10018900).
   - Or say it is too expensive. He offers a quest that rewards a [Gatling Cannon](partners.html#item:10018900) instead. It costs **3 quest points**. The quests are on [How to Get Pets](getting-pets.md#quest-pets).

## Faction Power

- The **Faction Power** window shows each faction's gauge and your **Contribution Points**.
- The leading faction becomes the **Faction of the Week**. Its members get the bonus while it is in effect. The window also shows next week's bonus.
- Faction bonuses:
   - EXP Increased
   - Trust Increased
   - Quest Reward x1.25
- Training bonuses:
   - Airship Flight to [Fareast City](maps.html#map:10057000)
   - Airship Flight to [Morg City](maps.html#map:10060000)
   - Airship Flight to [Upper Iron City](maps.html#map:10063100)
   - Airship Flight to [Northern Promenade](maps.html#map:10065000)
