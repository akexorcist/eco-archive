# Element Fairies

Source page: [element-fairies.html](element-fairies.html)

The six **element fairies** of the Acronia continent, one for each element.

## The fairies

| Fairy | Where | Arrows |
|---|---|---|
| [Light Fairy](maps.html#map:10034000) | [Acronia Eastern Beach](maps.html#map:10034000) | [Light Summonstone](materials.html#item:10011210) → all your [Wooden Arrow](materials.html#item:10026400) become [Light Arrow](materials.html#item:10026510) |
| [Dark Fairy](maps.html#map:10019000) | [Island of the Undead](maps.html#map:10019000) | — |
| [Fire Fairy](maps.html#map:10042000) | [Staff Desert](maps.html#map:10042000) | [Flame Summonstone](materials.html#item:10011201) → all your [Wooden Arrow](materials.html#item:10026400) become [Fire Arrow](materials.html#item:10026500) |
| [Water Fairy](maps.html#map:10002000) | [Snov Snowfield](maps.html#map:10002000) | [Water Summonstone](materials.html#item:10011203) → all your [Wooden Arrow](materials.html#item:10026400) become [Water Arrow](materials.html#item:10026504) |
| [Earth Fairy](maps.html#map:10018000) | [East Cape](maps.html#map:10018000) | [Earth Summonstone](materials.html#item:10011205) → all your [Wooden Arrow](materials.html#item:10026400) become [Earth Arrow](materials.html#item:10026505) |
| [Wind Fairy](maps.html#map:10035000) | [Battleship Island](maps.html#map:10035000) | [Wind Summonstone](materials.html#item:10011207) → all your [Wooden Arrow](materials.html#item:10026400) become [Wind Arrow](materials.html#item:10026507) |

## Element arrows

- Bring the fairy a **Summonstone of its element** and some [Wooden Arrow](materials.html#item:10026400). Choose to put its power in.
- The [Dark Fairy](maps.html#map:10019000) doesn't make arrows.

## Filling a Crystal

- Bring a [Crystal](materials.html#item:10014300) to any fairy during the Spell User part of the [Lv 55 Armor Quest](quest-lv55-armor.md). It fills the Crystal with its element, and the element decides which staff you get.
