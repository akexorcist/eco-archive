    Hairstyles

    How to change your hairstyle at the salons in Uptown.

    The two salons

    • Nibelungen Beautician in Uptown serves women only.

      Nibelungen Beautician

    • Helheim Beautician in Uptown serves men only.

      Helheim Beautician

    Before you talk to either salon:

    • Take off your hat. The salon can't work on hair hidden under a hat.
    • End any Marionette transformation first.

    Basic styles

    Without a letter you can only cut or restyle your current hair.

    Service Cost What it does
    Cut 3,000 G Makes your hair shorter. Men can go down to a buzz cut and then a shaved head.
    Styling 10,000 G Changes your hair into another style of about the same length.

    The first time you talk to a salon, you get one random Hair Color as a welcome gift.

    Growing your hair longer

    Hair Loss Treatment makes your hair one step longer. Double-click it to use it. If your hair is already as long as it gets, nothing changes and you keep the item.

    Make it with Chemical Preparation Lv 1 (99% success):

    • 1 × Cuticle Extract
    • 1 × Honey
    • 1 × Resurrection Potion

    Cuticle Extract drops from Fallen Tree, Paper Tree and Driftwood.
