# Joker

Source page: [job-joker.html](job-joker.html)

Chronicle job · Novice

A job that can use every weapon and wear any job's armor, within its level, race and gender limits.

1. [Novice Starting](job-novice.md)
2. Joker Chronicle

## Job change

**Requirement:** Lv 100 or higher, Job Level 50 as a [Novice](job-novice.md), and at least one win over the [Emil Dragon](monsters.html#monster:14520000). You must never have changed jobs.

1. Travel to [Northern Promenade](maps.html#map:10065000). When you meet the requirements, a **mysterious creature** (a cat) appears there for you to talk to. If it doesn't appear, you don't meet them yet.
2. Talk to the creature until it runs away.
   **? ? ?**
3. Go to the [Northern Cafe](maps.html#map:10065000) in [Northern Promenade](maps.html#map:10065000) and talk to **Valeria**.
   **Valeria**
4. Chase the creature: find it again in [Northern Promenade](maps.html#map:10065000) and follow it into the [Queen Audience Room](maps.html#map:10065000).
5. Follow the creature into the **magic circle**.
6. In the [White World](maps.html#map:10065000), walk straight ahead to the creature sleeping there. It asks you 3 questions.
   **? ? ?**
   - Answers: choose **not to wake it**, then answer that **it is real**. Any answer works for the third question.
   - A wrong answer sends you back. Walk to the creature again.
7. In the room with the book ([White World](maps.html#map:10065000)), talk to **Mucha**, then look inside the **White Book**.
   **Mucha**
   **White Book**
8. You receive the **Book of Rebirth: A Journey to Find Yourself**. Travel to each spot below and **use the book** there.
   1 [Northern Promenade](maps.html#map:10065000) Middle of the town 2 [Uptown](maps.html#map:10023000) In front of the [Guild Palace Lobby 1F](maps.html#map:10023000) 3 [Morg City](maps.html#map:10060000) In the pit in the middle of the city where logs appear 4 [Tonka Island](maps.html#map:10062000) In front of the wind-up doll (Pino) statue 5 [Mai Mai Island](maps.html#map:10059000) Next to the [Wizard Pilgrim](maps.html#map:10059000), near the shop 6 [Upper Iron City](maps.html#map:10063100) Top left of the map, in front of the [Air Force Camp Guard](maps.html#map:10063100) 7 [Fareast City](maps.html#map:10057000) On the road near [Michelle](maps.html#map:10057000), who is plowing the field
9. When the book is complete, return to the [White World](maps.html#map:10065000) and show it to **Mucha**.
10. Look inside the **White Book** again. **Take off your equipment** first. You become a Joker and reincarnate into the higher form of your race (see [Races](races.md)). Your level and Job Level go back to **1**.
   - You keep your skills, but you raise them again.

**Job Lv 35 Joker Style Active physical attack · Max Lv 3**

- Changes behavior by level.
- Level 1 is a single heavy hit that lowers STR by 10 for 5 seconds after damage.
- Level 2 hits the target 3 times and has a 45% chance to Slow non-boss targets for 5 seconds.
- Level 3 hits enemies in a 3x3 area around the target, lowers AGI by 15 for 5 seconds after damage, and knocks targets back 2 cells.

| Lv | SP | Cast time | Cooldown | Target pattern | Damage rate | Additional effect |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | 35 | 1 sec | 1 sec | Single target | 1,050% | STR -10 for 5 sec |
| 2 | 43 | 1 sec | 1 sec | Single target, 3 hits | 300% per hit | 45% Slow for 5 sec; bosses are immune |
| 3 | 50 | 1 sec | 1 sec | 3x3 area around target | 1,050% | AGI -15 for 5 sec; knockback 2 cells |
