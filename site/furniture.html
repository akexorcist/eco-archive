    Furniture

    Furniture decorates your Flying Garden, in the garden or inside its house.

    On this page

    What furniture is for

    Kind What it changes
    Furniture A single piece you place: tables, chairs, lamps, beds, fences and more
    House The look of the house on your Flying Garden
    Room wall The walls inside the house
    Room floor The floor inside the house
    Garden floor The ground of the garden
    Sail The sails of your Flying Garden
    • You can place up to 100 pieces in your Flying Garden, counting the house, floors and walls.
    • How to place and turn pieces is on the Flying Garden page.

    Storage furniture

    Some pieces hold items, like a small warehouse in your garden:

    Furniture Slots
    Drawer 10
    Dresser 20
    Closet 20

    Two storage pieces of the same kind share their contents.

    House styles

    The Furniture Carpenter in Uptown can rebuild your Small House in a town style. You pay with ECO Ticket, which Cleaning Quests give.

    Acropolis House

    You need
    1 × Small House
    15 × ECO Ticket
    Who can build it
    Everyone

    Morg House

    You need
    1 × Small House
    30 × ECO Ticket
    Who can build it
    Members of the Western Army

    Iron South House

    You need
    1 × Small House
    30 × ECO Ticket
    Who can build it
    Members of the Southern Army

    Northern Kingdom House

    You need
    1 × Small House
    30 × ECO Ticket
    Who can build it
    Members of the Northern Army
