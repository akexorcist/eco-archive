    Job change

    Requirement: MAG 10 or higher. Titania characters meet this from the start.

    1. Enter Uptown. You need an Acropolis City Pass.
    2. Go to the White Church in Uptown.

    3. Talk to the White Church Vates and choose to become a Vates.

      White Church Vates

    4. Emil and Dominion only: travel to Acronia Eastern Beach and talk to the Light Fairy. Then return to the White Church Vates. Titania characters skip this step.

      Light Fairy

    5. The White Church Vates sends you to Sailor in Downtown. Talk to her and choose to hear about healing magic.

      Sailor

    6. Return to the White Church Vates in the White Church (Uptown). Answer his three questions: I want to heal as many people as possible → Light Fairy → Yes.

      • If you give a wrong answer, talk to Sailor again before you retry.
    7. Choose to become a Vates. When asked, take off your armor.
    8. Talk to him again. You are now a Vates and receive a Holy Hat. You also learn Heal.
