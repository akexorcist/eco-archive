    Base job · Backpacker

    Job change

    Requirement: Novice, with no stat or level requirement.

    1. Enter Uptown. You need an Acropolis City Pass.
    2. Go to the Guild Palace Lobby 1F in Uptown.
    3. Go inside and take the elevator to the Guild Palace Lobby 3F.
    4. Enter the room on the west (left-hand) side of the 3rd floor. This is the Blacksmith Guild.

    5. Talk to the Miner Master and choose to become a Miner (Tatarabe).

      Miner Master

    6. Accept his request: bring 3 × Iron Lump.

    7. Hunt Iron Ore Rock for 3 × Metallic Stone. Iron Ore Rock is found on Acronia Southern Plains, Acronia Northern Plains, Acronia Western Plains and Acronia Hill.

    8. Take the Metallic Stone to the Weaponsmith in Downtown and choose to have metal refined. Each refine costs 100 G and turns 1 × Metallic Stone into 1 × Iron Lump.

      Weaponsmith

    9. Return to the Miner Master in the Blacksmith Guild (Guild Palace Lobby 3F, west room) and hand over 3 × Iron Lump.

    10. When asked, take off your armor.
    11. Talk to him again. You are now a Miner (Tatarabe) and receive a Miner Bandana and a Miner Furnace. You also learn Ore Knowledge.
