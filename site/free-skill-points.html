    Free Skill Points

    Extra skill points you earn from quests and errands, on top of levelling up.

    On this page
    • How it works
    • Acropolis
    • Northern Kingdom
    • Fareast
    • Iron South
    • Related

    How it works

    • There are 21 extra skill points in all.
    • They all go to your first job, even if you collect them as an Expert or Technical job.

    Acropolis

    1 point

    Open all four shops of the Lv 35 Armor Quest, then talk to the Elegant Lady in Downtown

    1 point

    Bring a second fairy-filled Crystal to Talented Astrologer Remia. See Lv 55 Armor Quest

    8 points

    Finish the 8 Endless Corridor quests from D Master and give each ring to the Old Man at the east gate of Uptown: 1 point per ring

    Northern Kingdom

    1 point

    Go to the top of Northern Promenade and talk to the queen, Beldegard

    1 point

    In the Spell User Guild, go to the skill/stat reset room, Sorcerer Master Room, and talk to the Sorcerer at the top. Only Spell Users can enter; others can possess a Spell User to get in

    1 point

    Do the document delivery quest twice: carry the Compendium of Law from the Sage Master in Uptown to Miriaria in the north within 15 minutes. The first time gives a Text Book, the second a skill point

    Fareast

    1 point

    Talk to Farmer Sam about the weeds, then talk to him again. You don't have to pull the weeds

    1 point

    Find the 7 lost chicks around the village for Charles in the top-left barn and try to catch each one, then report back

    1 point

    Carry a lunch box from the Mother to the Water Keeper at the water wheel, bottom right of town, and choose the skill point

    Iron South

    1 point

    Do the Fighter delivery of the Lv 55 Armor Quest for Ghost Buster Gadoo

    1 point

    Do Mother's Medicine twice for Novice Adventurer Porol. See Lv 55 Armor Quest

    1 point

    Climb Iron Volcano and talk to the Salamander in the middle of the lava. Go with a party

    1 point

    Ask the fortune teller, Iron South's Mother, in the top-left of Lower Part of Iron City to read your soul's light

    1 point

    Buy a 1 G flower from the Flower Girl in the bottom-left of Upper Iron City
