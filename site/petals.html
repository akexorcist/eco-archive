    Flower Petals

    Six coloured petals, one for each element.

    On this page
    • How to get petals
    • Where to find each petal
    • What uses petals
    • Related

    How to get petals

    • Bring a Farmer who has learned Plant Knowledge. The skill is learned at Job Lv 7 and has only one level.
    • The Farmer must defeat the flower. Other players may get flower seeds from it instead.
    • The petal colour matches the flower's element.

    Where to find each petal

    • No shop sells petals, and they can't be crafted.
    • Momo Plains has both the Light Flower and the Earth Flower, so you can get White Petal and Orange Petal there.
    • Flowers grow on the same maps as the element crystals of their element.

    What uses petals

    Use Petals needed
    Lv 55 Armor Quest, Backpacker part 1 of each colour per run; 2 runs need 12 petals
    Perfumes with Chemical Preparation Lv 1
    1 × petal + 1 × Distilled Water + 1 × Ether makes:
    • Flame Perfume (Red)
    • Water Perfume (Blue)
    • Light Perfume (White)
    • Wind Perfume (Yellow)
    • Earth Perfume (Orange)
    Dandelion One-Piece (F) with Tailoring Lv 3 5 × Yellow Petal + 5 × Alchemy Fruit (Armor) + 5 × Noble Fabric
    Alchemist job test A Red Petal, White Petal or Blue Petal, depending on your question set
    Quest Counter quest "Collect the flowers that bloom at midnight!" (Lv 19+) 5 × Black Petal. The reward is a Purple Flower Seed
