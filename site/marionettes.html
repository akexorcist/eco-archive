    Marionettes & Golems

    On this page
    • Making golems
    • Free marionette
    • All marionettes and golems

    Making golems

    A golem is made with Marionette Construction, a Marionettist skill learned at Job Level 2, from:

    • 1 × Golem Goblet
    • 1 × its marionette

    Marionette Construction also makes the parts of the Electell Marionette:

    • Motor
    • Electell Model
    • Pocket Computer
    • Galvanized Iron Body

    Every recipe is on the Crafting page.

    Make with an NPC

    These NPCs make golems for 1,000 G.

    • Marionettist Master in the Marionettist Guild, Uptown. Also sells the Golem Goblet.

      Marionettist Master

    • Rita in the Rita's Marionette Craftshop, Tonka Island.

      Rita

    Free marionette

    Elina in Uptown gives you 1 free marionette.

    • You must be Lv 8 or higher.
    • Once per account.
    • You choose one of:
      • Salamander Marionette
      • Insmouse Marionette
      • Mandragora Marionette
      • Electell Marionette
