# Food and Healing Items

Source page: [food-items.html](food-items.html)

Items that restore HP, MP and SP, or revive a fallen character.

## How they work

- **Potions and drinks work at once.** Most food takes 0.5 to 1.5 seconds to eat.
- **Some food restores a share of your maximum.** For example, [Curry](materials.html#item:10008900) restores 35% of your max HP.
