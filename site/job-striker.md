# Striker

Source page: [job-striker.html](job-striker.html)

Expert job · Fighter

A job that adds elements to its arrows and trains and commands a wolf or bird pet.

## Job change

**Requirement:** STR 30 or higher and Job Level 30 or higher as an Archer.

1. Go to the [Guild Palace Lobby 1F](maps.html#map:10023000) in [Uptown](maps.html#map:10023000).
2. Go inside and take the **elevator** to the [Guild Palace Lobby 2F](maps.html#map:10023000).
3. Enter the room on the **north side** of the 2nd floor. This is the [Archer Guild](maps.html#map:10023000).
4. Talk to the **Archer Master** and choose to change jobs. The master sends you to [Lady Pamela](maps.html#map:10034000) in [Acronia Eastern Beach](maps.html#map:10034000).
   **Archer Master**
5. Travel to [Acronia Eastern Beach](maps.html#map:10034000).
6. Talk to [Lady Pamela](maps.html#map:10034000) and agree to take the test. She asks you to bring **100** × [Fire Arrow](materials.html#item:10026500).
   **Lady Pamela**
7. Go to the [Fire Fairy](maps.html#map:10042000) in [Staff Desert](maps.html#map:10042000). Bring **100** × [Wooden Arrow](materials.html#item:10026400) and **1** × [Flame Summonstone](materials.html#item:10011201). The fairy turns them into 100 × [Fire Arrow](materials.html#item:10026500).
   **Fire Fairy**
   - The **Archer Master** sells [Wooden Arrow](materials.html#item:10026400).
   - [Flame Summonstone](materials.html#item:10011201) drops from [Fire Crystal](monsters.html#monster:30030000) in [Staff Desert](maps.html#map:10042000).
   - 1 × [Flame Summonstone](materials.html#item:10011201) converts up to 200 × [Wooden Arrow](materials.html#item:10026400) at a time.
8. Return to [Lady Pamela](maps.html#map:10034000) in [Acronia Eastern Beach](maps.html#map:10034000) and hand over 100 × [Fire Arrow](materials.html#item:10026500). She tells you to prepare potions for the trial.
9. Talk to [Lady Pamela](maps.html#map:10034000) again and accept the **Striker Trial (Party)** quest.
   - The quest needs **1** quest point and has no time limit.
10. Go to [Continental Cave B5F](maps.html#map:20004000).
11. Defeat **1** [Sandfish](monsters.html#monster:26100003).
12. Return to [Lady Pamela](maps.html#map:10034000) in [Acronia Eastern Beach](maps.html#map:10034000). She gives you a **Striker License(Event)**.
13. Return to the **Archer Master** in the [Archer Guild](maps.html#map:10023000) ([Guild Palace Lobby 2F](maps.html#map:10023000), north room) and choose to become a **Striker**.
14. When asked, **take off your armor**. You are now a Striker.
