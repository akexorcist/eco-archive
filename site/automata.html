    How Automata work

    • Automata fight with the act cubes listed below.
    • Each Automata's full data is on the Partners page.
    • For the Backpack Familiar: Automata (Sapphire) and Backpack Familiar: Automata (Diamond) quests, talk to the quest NPC in your normal form while wearing an Automata.

    Amethyst

    • Item: Automata (Amethyst)
    • How to get: The DEM story quest Heart of the Machine. Needs Lv 20 and 21 Fame.
    • Attack: Ranged
    • Act cubes:
      • Defrag
      • Optimization
      • Concentration
      • Attack Burst
      • High Guard
      • Revive

    Ruby

    • Item: Automata (Ruby)
    • How to get: After Automata (Amethyst). Needs Lv 45.
    • Attack: Melee
    • Act cubes:
      • Defrag
      • Optimization
      • Avoid Burst
      • Attack Burst
      • Critical
      • Rouse Body
      • Assault
      • Stun Blow
      • Charge

    Topaz

    • Item: Automata (Topaz)
    • How to get: After Automata (Ruby). Needs Lv 60.
    • Attack: Melee
    • Act cubes:
      • Defrag
      • Optimization
      • Solid Aura
      • Magic Shield
      • Revive
      • Flip It!
      • Stop!

    Sapphire

    • Item: Backpack Familiar: Automata (Sapphire)
    • How to get: After Automata (Topaz). Needs Lv 80.
    • Attack: Ranged
    • Act cubes:
      • Defrag
      • Optimization
      • Dust Explosion
      • Lullaby
      • Continuous Throw
      • Revive
      • Zen

    Diamond

    • Item: Backpack Familiar: Automata (Diamond)
    • How to get: After Backpack Familiar: Automata (Sapphire). Needs Lv 90.
    • Attack: Ranged
    • Act cubes:
      • Defrag
      • Optimization
      • Lullaby
      • Magic Barrier
      • Revive
      • Luminary Nova

    Where each quest goes

    Automata Places you visit
    Automata (Amethyst)
    • Downtown
    • Electell Laboratory
    Automata (Ruby)
    • DEM Customized Room
    • West Fort City, with Snub, Resistance Investigator and Instructor Gunbal
    Automata (Topaz)
    • DEM Customized Room
    • Light Tower Grounds
    • Hell Desert, with Mysterious Woman
    • Factory Lobby, Lower Part of Iron City, with Worker Jose
    Backpack Familiar: Automata (Sapphire)
    • DEM Customized Room
    • Between Dimensions (Northern Underground), with Ice Pick ＤＥＭ
    Backpack Familiar: Automata (Diamond)
    • DEM Customized Room
    • Abyss of the Depth, with A Call from Someone
    • The Automata (Ruby) quest takes place in the Dominion world.
    • The Backpack Familiar: Automata (Diamond) quest asks for 9 × Fear Butterfly Wings and other dimension materials before the final fight.

    Combining Automata

    Once you have all five, they can join into DEM parts.

    You get What it is What it takes
    Set Automata Night Form One set of DEM parts made from all five Automata
    • You must be Lv 99 to finish.
    • Bring:
      • All five Automata
      • The five Optimization Modules, one from each Automata's quest
    • Pay Dr. Disciple in DEM Customize Room:
      • 10,000,000 base EXP
      • 10,000,000 job EXP
    Automata Boost Back part that turns the five Automata into a fast form; the Ignition skill raises your movement speed
    • Bring to Snub in West Fort City:
      • 5 × Broken Flight Unit
      • 10 × Power Unit
      • 20 × Heat Exhaust Unit

    The five Optimization Modules:

    • Optimization Module (Amethyst)
    • Optimization Module (Ruby)
    • Optimization Module (Topaz)
    • Optimization Module (Sapphire)
    • Optimization Module (Diamond)
