## Job change

**Requirement:** Job Level 10 or higher as a Novice.

1. Enter [Uptown](maps.html#map:10023000). You need an **Acropolis City Pass**.
2. Go to the [Guild Palace Lobby 1F](maps.html#map:10023000) in [Uptown](maps.html#map:10023000).
3. Go inside and take the **elevator** to the [Guild Palace Lobby 3F](maps.html#map:10023000).
4. Enter the room on the **north side** of the 3rd floor. This is the [Farmer Guild](maps.html#map:10023000).
5. Talk to the **Farmer Master** and choose **Farmer**.
   **Farmer Master**
6. Accept his request: bring **2** × [Giant Barley Straw](materials.html#item:10007900).
7. Hunt [Giant Barley](monsters.html#monster:30130000) for the straw. It grows on [Fareast Main Street](maps.html#map:10017000), [Acronia Forest](maps.html#map:10015000) and [East Cape](maps.html#map:10018000).
8. Return to the **Farmer Master** in the [Farmer Guild](maps.html#map:10023000) ([Guild Palace Lobby 3F](maps.html#map:10023000), north room) and hand over the straw.
9. When asked, **take off your armor**.
10. You are now a Farmer and receive a [Flower Brooch](equipment.html#item:50051400) and a [Cotton Ribbon](materials.html#item:10043700). You also learn [Cultivate Plant](job-farmer.md#skill:3128).
