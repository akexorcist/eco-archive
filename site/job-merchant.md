# Merchant

Source page: [job-merchant.html](job-merchant.html)

Base job · Backpacker

A trading job that carries more weight, gets better shop prices and can open a shop without a golem.

## Job change

**Requirement:** Novice. There is no level requirement.

1. Enter [Uptown](maps.html#map:10023000). You need an **Acropolis City Pass**.
2. Go to the [Guild Palace Lobby 1F](maps.html#map:10023000) in [Uptown](maps.html#map:10023000).
3. Go inside and take the **elevator** to the [Guild Palace Lobby 4F](maps.html#map:10023000).
4. Enter the room on the **north side** of the 4th floor. This is the [Merchant Guild](maps.html#map:10023000).
5. Talk to the **Merchant Master** and choose to become a **Merchant**.
   **Merchant Master**
6. Accept his request. He gives you a **Letter(Quest)** for the [Forgetful Old Man](maps.html#map:10024000) in [Downtown](maps.html#map:10024000).
7. Talk to the [Forgetful Old Man](maps.html#map:10024000) in [Downtown](maps.html#map:10024000) and give him the letter. He forgot his wallet.
   **Forgetful Old Man**
8. Fetch the old man's things one at a time. Bring each one back to him before you fetch the next.
   **Old Man's Child**
   **Old Man's Grandson**
   **Old Man's Friend**
   **Old Man's Subordinate**
   **Old Man's Daughter-in-Law**
   - **Grandfather Wallet** from [Old Man's Child](maps.html#map:10023000) in [Uptown](maps.html#map:10023000)
   - **Grandfather Handkerchief** from [Old Man's Grandson](maps.html#map:10024000) in [Downtown](maps.html#map:10024000)
   - **Grandfather Cane** from **Old Man's Friend** in [Uptown](maps.html#map:10023000)
   - **Grandfather Glasses** from **Old Man's Subordinate** in [Downtown](maps.html#map:10024000)
   - **Grandfather Pants** from **Old Man's Daughter-in-Law** in [Uptown](maps.html#map:10023000)
9. After the pants, the old man only mumbles. Talk to the [Crybaby](maps.html#map:10024000) in [Downtown](maps.html#map:10024000). He gives you the **Grandfather Denture**.
   **Crybaby**
10. Give the dentures to the [Forgetful Old Man](maps.html#map:10024000). He gives you the **Merchant Master Stamp**.
11. Return to the **Merchant Master** in the [Merchant Guild](maps.html#map:10023000) ([Guild Palace Lobby 4F](maps.html#map:10023000), north room), hand over the stamp and choose to become a **Merchant**.
12. When asked, **take off all your equipment**.
13. Talk to the master again. You are now a Merchant and receive a **Left-Hand Tote Bag**. You also learn [Packing](job-merchant.md#skill:702).
