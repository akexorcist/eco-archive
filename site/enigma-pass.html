    Enigma Group Pass

    The pass that gets you into the Enigma Group Fortress.

    On this page
    • Get the pass
    • Enter the fortress
    • Related

    Get the pass

    • Go to Fareast City and enter the Fareast Hut, the house with the hidden portal.

    • Talk to the Enigma Group Communicator. When he asks for the password, type one of these:

      Language Password
      English Friend of Enigma Group
      Japanese フシギだんとおともだち
      Chinese 謎語團和朋友
      Enigma Group Communicator

    • Say it right and he gives you an Enigma Group Pass. Say it wrong and he throws you out into Fareast City.

    • You can get a pass this way as often as you need.

    Enter the fortress

    • Go to the Enigma Group Fortress and talk to an Enigma Group Member at the gate. He takes the pass and lets you in.
