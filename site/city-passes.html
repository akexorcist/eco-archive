    City Passes

    Passes that get you past the guards at the borders and at the gates of Uptown.

    On this page
    • Uptown
    • Border passes
    • Where to buy them
    • Good to know
    • Related

    Uptown

    • The guards at the gates of Uptown want an Acropolis City Pass.

    • Finish one quest of any kind, then talk to the Elegant Lady in Downtown. She gives you an Acropolis City Pass.

      Elegant Lady

    • The way down to Downtown is in the small passages beside each of the four Uptown gates.

    • A guard turns you away while someone is possessing you.

    Border passes

    Pass Checked at Price
    Northern Kingdom Pass Northern Border Guard at the north gate of Cape Northerin, on the road to Northern Promenade 500 G / 1,000 G / 5,000 G
    United Iron South Pass Iron South Border Guard at the south gate of Cape Southerin, on the road to Upper Iron City 500 G / 1,000 G / 5,000 G
    Fareast Republic Pass East Cape Border Guard at the east gate of East Cape, on the road to Fareast City 3,000 G / 6,000 G
    Morg Land Pass Morg Border Guard at the airship dock on Battleship Island, for the airship to Morg City 500 G / 1,000 G / 5,000 G

    • Northern Border Guard checks the Northern Kingdom Pass at the north gate of Cape Northerin.

      Northern Border Guard

    • Iron South Border Guard checks the United Iron South Pass at the south gate of Cape Southerin.

      Iron South Border Guard

    • East Cape Border Guard checks the Fareast Republic Pass at the east gate of East Cape.

      East Cape Border Guard

    • Morg Border Guard checks the Morg Land Pass before you board the airship to Morg City on Battleship Island.

      Morg Border Guard

    Where to buy them

    Pass Sold by
    Northern Kingdom Pass
    • Spell User Master in the Spell User Guild, Guild Palace Lobby 3F, Uptown, 500 G
    • White Church Vates in the White Church, Uptown, 500 G
    • Black Church Vates in the Black Church, Uptown, 500 G
    • Black Marketeer on Cape Northerin, 5,000 G
    • Mr. Specialist in his shop, Downtown, 1,000 G
    United Iron South Pass
    • Swordsman Master in the Swordsman Guild, Guild Palace Lobby 2F, Uptown, 500 G
    • Fencer Master in the Fencer Guild, Guild Palace Lobby 2F, Uptown, 500 G
    • Scout Master in the Scout Guild, Guild Palace Lobby 2F, Uptown, 500 G
    • Archer Master in the Archer Guild, Guild Palace Lobby 2F, Uptown, 500 G
    • Black Marketeer on Cape Southerin, 5,000 G
    • Mr. Specialist in his shop, Downtown, 1,000 G
    Fareast Republic Pass
    • Miner Master in the Blacksmith Guild, Guild Palace Lobby 3F, Uptown, 3,000 G
    • Farmer Master in the Farmer Guild, Guild Palace Lobby 3F, Uptown, 3,000 G
    • Ranger Master in the Ranger Guild, Guild Palace Lobby 4F, Uptown, 3,000 G
    • Merchant Master in the Merchant Guild, Guild Palace Lobby 4F, Uptown, 3,000 G
    • Mr. Specialist in his shop, Downtown, 6,000 G
    Morg Land Pass
    • Merchant Master in the Merchant Guild, Guild Palace Lobby 4F, Uptown, 500 G
    • Black Marketeer on Battleship Island, 5,000 G
    • Mr. Specialist in his shop, Downtown, 1,000 G
    • The guild masters are in the guild rooms off Guild Palace Lobby 2F, Guild Palace Lobby 3F and Guild Palace Lobby 4F in Uptown, so each job group can buy the pass to its own home country there.

    Good to know

    • Each crossing uses up one pass, except the Fareast Republic Pass, which you only show once.
    • The first delivery quest to Upper Iron City lets you through for free. Take it at the Quest Counter (Cafe Master in the Cafe, Downtown) instead of buying a pass.
