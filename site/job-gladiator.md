## Job change

**Requirement:** Lv 100 or higher, Job Level 50 in either [Blade Master](job-blade-master.md) or [Bounty Hunter](job-bounty-hunter.md), and at least one win over the [Emil Dragon](monsters.html#monster:14520000).

1. Travel to [Northern Promenade](maps.html#map:10065000). When you meet the requirements, a **mysterious creature** (a cat) appears there for you to talk to. If it doesn't appear, you don't meet them yet.
2. Talk to the creature until it runs away.
   **? ? ?**
3. Go to the [Northern Cafe](maps.html#map:10065000) in [Northern Promenade](maps.html#map:10065000) and talk to **Valeria**.
   **Valeria**
4. Chase the creature: find it again in [Northern Promenade](maps.html#map:10065000) and follow it into the [Queen Audience Room](maps.html#map:10065000).
5. Follow the creature into the **magic circle**.
6. In the [White World](maps.html#map:10065000), walk straight ahead to the creature sleeping there. It asks you 3 questions.
   **? ? ?**
   - Answers: choose **not to wake it**, then answer that **it is real**. Any answer works for the third question.
   - A wrong answer sends you back. Walk to the creature again.
7. In the room with the book ([White World](maps.html#map:10065000)), talk to **Mucha**.
   **Mucha**
8. Look inside the **White Book**. It sends you to face **Kamui**, the opponent of the Swordsman line. Then return to the room with the book.
   **White Book**
9. Talk to **Mucha** again.
10. Look inside the **White Book** and choose **Moment of Emptiness (Time)**. There you earn two proofs of strength: the **Hero Proof** and the **Soul Proof**.
11. Defeat the monsters in that room for **small fire orbs** and **large fire orbs**.
12. Combine **50** small fire orbs or **1** large fire orb into **1 talisman**. A golem there also sells talismans.
   - The talisman comes out **red** or **blue** at random.
   - **Swordsman line:** you need the **red** one, like every Fighter line.
13. Use the talisman to fight a **copy of yourself**. You have **10 minutes**.
   - **Swordsman line:** you fight the copy.
   - Each talisman works once. Everyone who enters needs their own talisman and the quest.
14. Fight **Kamui**, the opponent of your line.
   **Kamui**
   - HP regenerates on its own on this map.
15. Look inside the **White Book** again. **Take off your equipment** first. You become a Gladiator and reincarnate into the higher form of your race (see [Races](races.md)). Your level and Job Level go back to **1**.
   - You keep your skills, but you raise them again.

**Job Lv 47 Kensei Passive · Max Lv 5**

- Enhances sword combo routes and adds bonus damage to specific slash skills.
- The Kensei bonus for Quick Slash combos, behead-style skills, Omnislash , Brandish , and Specter Sword is halved in PvP; God Speed Slash applies its PvP reduction after its Kensei bonus is added.

| Affected skill group | Kensei bonus | Extra combo routes enabled |
|---|---|---|
| Quick Slash Combo 1 , Quick Slash Combo 2 , Quick Slash Combo 3 | +300% + 100% x Kensei Lv | Omnislash, God Speed Slash, Zillion Blade |
| Head Crusher, Lumberjack Slash, Eagle Strike, Swallow Snatcher, Beast Jawbreaker | +300% + 100% x Kensei Lv | Maximize Spirit Sword |
| Specter Sword | +30% + 10% x Kensei Lv | Zillion Blade |
| Omnislash | +10% + 10% x Kensei Lv | God Speed Slash |
| Brandish | +700% + 100% x Kensei Lv | Quick Slash Combo 1, Quick Slash Combo 2, Quick Slash Combo 3 |

| Zillion Blade | No direct damage bonus | Brandish, Specter Sword, God Speed Slash, Quick Slash Combo 1, Quick Slash Combo 2, Quick Slash Combo 3 |

- If Kensei is learned, Zillion Blade opens combo routes to Brandish, Specter Sword, God Speed Slash, and Quick Slash Combo 1-3.
