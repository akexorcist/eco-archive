# Between the Worlds

Source page: [between-the-worlds.html](between-the-worlds.html)

The three worlds meet at [Sky Tower Island](maps.html#map:10058000). Each world has its own [Sky Tower Island](maps.html#map:10058000), and the [Elevator Room](maps.html#map:10058000) in the tower links them.

1. Go to [Sky Tower Island](maps.html#map:10058000) (Emil World) from [Morg City](maps.html#map:10060000).
2. Walk into the **Sky Tower Door** in the middle of the island and choose to **go in**. You arrive in the [Elevator Room](maps.html#map:10058000).
   [Sky Tower Island](maps.html#map:10058000)
3. Use the control panel in the [Elevator Room](maps.html#map:10058000):
   - **Upper button**: [Sky Tower Island](maps.html#map:11058000) in the Titania World.
   - **Lower button**: [Sky Tower Island](maps.html#map:12058000) in the Dominion World. You can't be possessing anyone or have a quest in progress.

## Back to the Emil World

Use the keyhole on the island and choose to **return**:

- **Sky Tower Keyhole** on [Sky Tower Island](maps.html#map:11058000) (Titania World).
   **Sky Tower Keyhole**
- **Sky Tower Keyhole** on [Sky Tower Island](maps.html#map:12058000) (Dominion World). You can't be possessing anyone or have a quest in progress.
   **Sky Tower Keyhole**

## First trip to each world

- **Titania World:** bring the **Sky Tower Key** to the **Sky Tower Keyhole** on [Sky Tower Island](maps.html#map:10058000) (Emil World). The key is found in the [Mai Mai Ruins 1st Deck](maps.html#map:20164000).
   **Sky Tower Keyhole**
- After the keyhole, the trip goes on in the Titania World:
   - Talk to the [Guardian Mermaid](maps.html#map:11058000) on [Sky Tower Island](maps.html#map:11058000).
      **Guardian Mermaid**
   - Talk to [Tea Tree](maps.html#map:21180001) in the [Submarine Cave](maps.html#map:21180001).
      **Tea Tree**
   - Talk to [Skuld](maps.html#map:11053000) in the [Water Layer](maps.html#map:11053000).
      **Skuld**
   - Bring a present to the [Guidance Mermaid](maps.html#map:11053000) in the [Water Layer](maps.html#map:11053000).
      **Guidance Mermaid**
- **Dominion World:** the Main Story chapter [The World Begins to Move](story-the-world-begins-to-move.md) takes you there from [Sky Tower Island](maps.html#map:10058000).
- **Dominion level:** in the Dominion World you play at your separate Dominion level and job level. Equipment above your Dominion level comes off when you arrive.
