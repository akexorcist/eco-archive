# Woodcraft

Source page: [woodcraft.html](woodcraft.html)

[Woodcraft](job-farmer.md#skill:2020) turns timber and branches into rods, tools, fishing rods and furniture.

## How it works

- [Woodcraft](job-farmer.md#skill:2020) is a [Farmer](job-farmer.md) skill, learned at Job Level 15, with 3 levels.
- Each skill level opens the recipes of that level. The [Crafting](crafting.md) page lists every [Woodcraft](job-farmer.md#skill:2020) recipe.
- Its level also raises the quality of woodwork repair kits made with [Woodwork Repair Kit Craft](job-farmer.md#skill:2086). See [Item Repair](item-repair.md).

## Make with an NPC

Talk to one of these NPCs with the materials and the tool in your inventory. They make every recipe up to Lv 3.

The fee depends on the recipe level:

|---|---|

| Lv 2 | 5,000 G |
| Lv 3 | 10,000 G |
