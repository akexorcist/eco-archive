    Expert job · Spell User

    A job with strong support spells and neutral magic stronger than the Wizard's, at a higher MP cost.

    Job change

    Requirement: MAG 30 or higher and Job Level 30 or higher as a Wizard.

    1. Go to the Guild Palace Lobby 1F in Uptown.
    2. Go inside and take the elevator to the Guild Palace Lobby 3F.
    3. Enter the room on the east side of the 3rd floor. This is the Spell User Guild.

    4. Talk to the Spell User Master. The master sends you to the Sorcerer in Northern Promenade.

      Spell User Master
      • You need a Northern Kingdom Pass to enter Northern Promenade. The Spell User Master sells it.

    5. Travel to Northern Promenade.

    6. Enter the Spell User Guild Headquarters' Lobby, then go to the Sorcerer Master Room.

    7. Talk to the Sorcerer. He asks you to bring five items.

      Sorcerer

    8. Collect 1 of each item. All five drop from monsters.

      • Crystal from Cyclops
      • Snow Drop from Pefang
      • Willydoo Ball from Willydoo
      • Lapis Lazuli Piece from Mini Demon
      • Crest Paper from Skeleton Archer
    9. Return to the Sorcerer in the Sorcerer Master Room (Spell User Guild Headquarters' Lobby, Northern Promenade) and hand over the items.
    10. When asked, take off your armor. The Sorcerer gives you the Sorcerer mark, and you are now a Sorcerer.
