    Endless Corridor Quests

    Party quests in the Endless Corridor that reward skill points.

    Where
    The Staff Desert entrance of the Endless Corridor
    Repeatable
    The quests can be repeated; the skill point is once per ring
    Reward
    8 rings, and 1 skill point for each ring
    On this page
    • How the corridor works
    • Below B40F
    • The 8 quests
    • Collect the skill points
    • Related

    How the corridor works

    • Talk to the D Master at the entrance to take a quest and choose the floor to start from.

      D Master

    • Starting deeper costs gold: B1F is free, B11F costs 5,000 G, B21F 10,000 G and B31F 15,000 G.

    • To go down a floor, defeat a monster with Alcana in its name and pick up its Alcana Ring. Each floor's ring only opens the next floor. Everyone in your party needs their own ring.
    • To leave, step back into the portal you came down and choose to go out. You return to the entrance and can't go back to that floor.
    • Everyone in a party with the same quest shares the kill: whoever defeats the target, the quest counts for all.
    • Alcana Rings vanish when you go down a floor, even if you carry several.

    Below B40F

    • Once you have reached B41F, the D Master can send you straight there.
    • The B41F lobby leads to the Middle floors (B42F to B71F) and the Lower floors (B72F and below).
    • Only Chronicle jobs can enter the Lower floors.
    • The Lower Class Warrior gives the Middle floor quests and the Senior Warrior the Lower floor quests. They pay Mission Ticket.

    The 8 quests

    Quest Floor Defeat Time limit Reward
    The Beginning (Party) B1F Sand Crawler 60 min Keura
    A Long way (Party) B10F Killer Bee 120 min Gala
    Fire Flower (Party) B15F Tentacle 150 min Huira
    The Guards and Poison Swamp (Party) B20F Gaz 180 min Ella
    Subsequent Corridor (Party) B25F Living Armor 210 min Kuira
    Bones (Party) B30F Skeleton Archer 240 min Aura
    Shining Eyes in the dark (Party) B35F Cyclops 270 min Lila
    Deep (Party) B40F Houou 300 min Mila

    Collect the skill points

    • Go to the east side of Uptown and give each ring to the Old Man. He gives you 1 skill point per ring.

      Old Man

    • Giving him the same ring again gives you an item instead.
