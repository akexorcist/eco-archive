# Breeder

Source page: [job-breeder.html](job-breeder.html)

Joint job

A Joint Job for raising and commanding pets, which any character can take on and leave again.

## Job change

**Requirement:** Job Level 30 or higher in the job you have now (base, Expert or Technical).

1. Travel to [Fareast City](maps.html#map:10057000). The [Breeder Guild](maps.html#map:10057000) is the first building on the south side of the city, at **(41, 154)**.
2. Go inside and talk to the **Breeder Guild Receptionist** behind the counter. Choose the **first option** to say you want to change jobs.
   **Breeder Guild Receptionist**
   - She explains the job. When she asks if you have questions, choose the **first option** again.
3. Talk to the **Breeder Guild Member** in front of the door to enter the [Interview Room](maps.html#map:10057000).
   **Breeder Guild Member**
4. In the [Interview Room](maps.html#map:10057000), talk to the **Breeder Guild Master** near the table.
   **Breeder Guild Master**
5. Answer his 7 questions: **2 → 1 → 1 → 2 → 2 → 3 → 1**.
   - If you fail, you are sent back to the guild. Talk to the receptionist and choose the **third option** to take the interview again.
6. You return to the [Breeder Guild](maps.html#map:10057000). Talk to the **Breeder Guild Receptionist** behind the counter. Choose the **first option**, then the **second option**. This sets your save point to the [Breeder Guild](maps.html#map:10057000).
   **Breeder Guild Receptionist**
7. Talk to her again. She gives you a [Dark Dragon (Event)](partners.html#item:10052310) to ride.
8. Ride the dragon and enter the room at the back left. You have **3 minutes** to ride through the maze ([2nd Examination Grounds](maps.html#map:10057000)) to **Plum**. Talk to her while still riding.
   **Plum**
   - Don't talk to the other NPCs in the maze.
   - If you are too late, you fail and return to the guild. Talk to the receptionist for a new try.
9. Return to the **Breeder Guild Receptionist** behind the counter. She explains the final test and gives you the [Dark Dragon (Event)](partners.html#item:10052310) again.
10. Ride the dragon into the room at the back left again. Ride through the second maze ([2nd Examination Grounds](maps.html#map:10057000)) to the **Breeder Guild Master** within the time limit and talk to him while riding.
   **Breeder Guild Master**
11. The master takes the dragon back and gives you the **Breeder Brooch**. Wear it to become a Breeder.
   - The receptionist sells a new brooch if you lose yours.

## Skills

Breeder skills cost no skill points. You learn each one automatically when you reach its Job Level.

**Job Lv 1 Go! Active skill · Max Lv 1**

- Orders all current partners to attack the selected target.
- The target becomes their priority attack target when it is valid.

| Lv | Cast time | Cooldown |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | 0 sec | 0 sec |

**Job Lv 3 Come Back! Active skill · Max Lv 1**

Orders all current partners to stop attacking and clear their current attack target.

| Lv | Cast time | Cooldown |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | 0 sec | 1 sec |

**Job Lv 5 Grow Up Passive · Max Lv 1**

Increases partner EXP gained by 2%.

| Lv | Partner EXP gain |
|---|---|
| 1 | +2% |

**Job Lv 8 Belief Proof Active partner utility · Max Lv 1**

Opens a partner pose selection and makes the active partner perform the chosen linked motion.

| Lv | Cast time | Cooldown |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | 0 sec | 0 sec |

**Job Lv 10 Heal Fire Active field support · Max Lv 1**

- Places a healing fire for 60 seconds.
- Allies in the 5x5 area recover HP every 3 seconds; partners receive a larger heal than players.

| Lv | SP | Cast time | Cooldown | Duration | Area | Player heal | Partner heal |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | 25 | 0 sec | 0 sec | 60 sec | 5x5 | 20 HP every 3 sec | 50 HP every 3 sec |

**Job Lv 13 Reins Passive · Max Lv 1**

- Unlock-style passive for using supported ride partners.
- It adds no combat stats by itself.

| Lv | Effect |
|---|---|
| 1 | Unlock/marker passive; no direct combat stat bonus |

**Job Lv 14 Encouragement Active buff · Max Lv 1**

Raises the target's minimum and maximum ATK for 60 seconds.

| Lv | SP | Cast time | Cooldown | Duration | Min ATK | Max ATK |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | 12 | 0 sec | 0 sec | 60 sec | +6 | +12 |

**Job Lv 15 High Grow Up Passive · Max Lv 1**

Increases partner EXP gained by 3%.

| Lv | Partner EXP gain |
|---|---|
| 1 | +3% |

**Job Lv 20 Metamorphose Active self-buff · Max Lv 1**

- Transforms the character into the appearance of their equipped partner.
- Casting it again while transformed removes the transformation.

| Lv | SP | Cast time | Cooldown |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | 25 | 0 sec | 0 sec |

**Job Lv 23 Lion Power Passive · Max Lv 1**

Improves the active partner or supported ride partner's maximum HP, MP, and SP.

| Lv | Partner Max HP | Partner Max MP | Partner Max SP |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | +40% | +30% | +30% |

**Job Lv 27 Acrobat Evasion Active partner utility · Max Lv 1**

Adds aggro to enemies in a 5x5 area around the partner, making them target the partner.

| Lv | SP | Cast time | Cooldown | Area | Aggro |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | 25 | 0 sec | 0 sec | 5x5 around partner | +2,500 to partner |

**Job Lv 30 Pet Delay Cancel Active partner buff · Max Lv 1**

Applies Delay Cancel to the active partner for 20 seconds, increasing partner attack speed.

| Lv | SP | Cast time | Cooldown | Duration | Partner ASPD |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | 36 | 0 sec | 1 sec | 20 sec | +50% |
