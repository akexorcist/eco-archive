# Memories of the Ice Queen

Source page: [story-saga16.html](story-saga16.html)

A bonus Main Story from SAGA 16: you gather permission to go beyond [Northern Underground (Ice Cave)](maps.html#map:20211000).

## Story

Starting deep inside the back of [Northern Underground (Ice Cave)](maps.html#map:20211000), you head into town to hear what the **Northern Priest** at the **Northern Inn Main Building (Guest Room)** and **Tiru** at the **Northern Shop** have to say. To pass beyond the ice cave, you must first earn permission from three people: **Fuller** at the **Northern Inn Annex (Guest Room)**, **Forseti** in a **Private House (Attic)** who requests an Unmelting Ice first, and **Gney** at the **Barracks**.

Once all three grant their approval, you report back to **Tiru** and return to the back of [Northern Underground (Ice Cave)](maps.html#map:20211000). Your journey concludes further within at [Northern Underground (World Tree)](maps.html#map:20212000), where you listen to what **Tiru** has to share.

## Characters

**Tiru**

First met at the **Northern Shop**; the story ends with a talk at [Northern Underground (World Tree)](maps.html#map:20212000).

**Northern Priest**

The first person you talk to, in the **Northern Inn Main Building (Guest Room)**.

**Fuller**

Gives you permission to go past the ice cave, in the **Northern Inn Annex (Guest Room)**.

**Forseti**

Asks for an Unmelting Ice before giving permission, in the **Private House (Attic)**.

**Gney**

The last one to give permission, at the **Barracks**.
