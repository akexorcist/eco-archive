## Job change

**Requirement:** STR 30 or higher and Job Level 30 or higher as a Swordsman.

1. Go to the [Guild Palace Lobby 1F](maps.html#map:10023000) in [Uptown](maps.html#map:10023000).
2. Go inside and take the **elevator** to the [Guild Palace Lobby 2F](maps.html#map:10023000).
3. Enter the room on the **east side** of the 2nd floor. This is the [Swordsman Guild](maps.html#map:10023000).
4. Talk to the **Swordsman Master** and ask to change jobs. He asks you to get a certificate from **Mr. Bong** in the [Iron City Cafe](maps.html#map:10066000), [Lower Part of Iron City](maps.html#map:10066000).
   **Swordsman Master**
5. Travel to the [Lower Part of Iron City](maps.html#map:10066000).
6. Enter the [Iron City Cafe](maps.html#map:10066000) and talk to **Mr. Bong**. Choose to become a Blade Master. He asks you to bring **1** × [Black Vinegar](materials.html#item:10033910).
   **Mr. Bong**
7. Hunt [South Pole Pefang](monsters.html#monster:10440100) in [Ghost Shelter](maps.html#map:10061000) or [CockKo Baby](monsters.html#monster:10620100) on [Momo Plains](maps.html#map:10056000) for the [Black Vinegar](materials.html#item:10033910).
8. Return to **Mr. Bong** in the [Iron City Cafe](maps.html#map:10066000) ([Lower Part of Iron City](maps.html#map:10066000)) and hand over the [Black Vinegar](materials.html#item:10033910). Talk to him again to get the quest **Like the Wind (Party)**.
   - Party members who take the same quest share the kill count.
9. Travel to [Iron Volcano](maps.html#map:10064000). Defeat [Lava Pururu](monsters.html#monster:10000801) **×5** and [Gigo](monsters.html#monster:10140000) **×3** within **10 minutes**.
10. Return to **Mr. Bong** in the [Iron City Cafe](maps.html#map:10066000) and talk to him. He gives you a **Certificate(Quest)**.
11. Return to the **Swordsman Master** in the [Swordsman Guild](maps.html#map:10023000) ([Guild Palace Lobby 2F](maps.html#map:10023000), east room).
12. **Take off all your equipment** and talk to the **Swordsman Master**. Choose to become a Blade Master. You are now a Blade Master.
   - Your Job Level goes back to 1. You keep your Swordsman skills.

- Berserk doubles minimum and maximum ATK by adding the character's current ATK values again, but the character becomes uncontrollable unless Cold-Blooded Berserk succeeds.
- While Berserk is active, incoming physical attacks ignore the right-side DEF calculation.
