# Flower Petals

Source page: [petals.html](petals.html)

Six coloured petals, one for each element.

## How to get petals

- Bring a [Farmer](job-farmer.md) who has learned [Plant Knowledge](job-farmer.md#skill:807). The skill is learned at Job Lv 7 and has only one level.
- The [Farmer](job-farmer.md) must defeat the flower. Other players may get flower seeds from it instead.
- The petal colour matches the flower's element.

- No shop sells petals, and they can't be crafted.
- [Momo Plains](maps.html#map:10056000) has both the [Light Flower](monsters.html#monster:30211000) and the [Earth Flower](monsters.html#monster:30210800), so you can get [White Petal](materials.html#item:10043204) and [Orange Petal](materials.html#item:10043208) there.
- Flowers grow on the same maps as the element crystals of their element.
