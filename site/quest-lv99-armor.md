# Lv 99 Armor Quest

Source page: [quest-lv99-armor.html](quest-lv99-armor.html)

Earn a **Jewel of Hope** from the **Emil Dragon** and trade it for a **Lv 99 job outfit**.

## Before you start

- Defeat the **Emil Dragon** once first, in the [Lv 99 Weapon Quest](quest-lv99-weapon.md) (**Strongest Dragon End (Party)**).
- Bring **3 quest points** for the book and **1** for the dragon fight.
- Bring a **party** for the dragon fight.

## Walkthrough

### 1. Talk to the Strong Inspiration Man

- Go to [Abyss of the Depth](maps.html#map:11027000) and talk to **Strong Inspiration Man**.
   **Strong Inspiration Man**

### 2. Collect the Fragments of Courage

- In [Abyss of the Depth](maps.html#map:11027000), enter the house at **(134, 207)**. Read the book on the round table in the middle of the room. It costs **3 quest points** and takes you into the book world.
- You go in alone. Your HP, MP and SP recover all the time, as in a town.
- Defeat the **Unknown** monsters there. They look like your own character. Each can drop a **Fragment of Courage**.
- Collect **100** × **Fragment of Courage**.

### 3. Defeat the Emil Dragon's human form

- Bring the fragments to the **Emil Dragon** in [Abyss of the Depth](maps.html#map:11027000).
   **Emil Dragon**
- He gives you the quest **Strongest Dragon's End (Party)**: a party hunt with a **60-minute** limit.
- Enter the dungeon behind him and defeat the [Emil Dragon - Arma](monsters.html#monster:14520002) (Lv 250).
- Report back to the dragon. He takes the 100 fragments and gives you a **Jewel of Hope**.

### 4. Trade the Jewel of Hope

- Return to **Strong Inspiration Man** in [Abyss of the Depth](maps.html#map:11027000).
- Choose **Male** or **Female**, then **Fighter**, **Spell User** or **Backpacker**. He takes the **Jewel of Hope** and gives you the full set of one random job line from that group.
   - Make room in your inventory first.

The set is one of these.

Names with (M) are for men and (F) for women, but [Sword Emperors Armor: Black Dragon (Bottom)](equipment.html#item:50204100) and [Athenas Crown](equipment.html#item:50109600) are for men, [Sword Emperors Shoes: White Dragon Run](equipment.html#item:50204300) and [White Dragons Overknee](equipment.html#item:50204200) are for women, and [Bow Emperors Quiver](equipment.html#item:50074600), [Blacsmith Emperors Tools](equipment.html#item:50074700), [Farmer Emperors Rucksack](equipment.html#item:50074800) and [Explorer Emperors Helm](equipment.html#item:50110500) are for both.

## Good to know

- **Losing the dragon fight doesn't cost the fragments.** The dragon takes them only when he gives you the **Jewel of Hope**.
- **The **Fragment of Courage** can't be traded.**
