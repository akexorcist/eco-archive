# Lv 85 Armor Quest

Source page: [quest-lv85-armor.html](quest-lv85-armor.html)

Lift the curse on a zombie blacksmith's **cursed Lv 85 armor**.

## Before you start

- Finish the **metal armor** part of the [Lv 35 Armor Quest](quest-lv35-armor.md) first. [Apprentice Blacksmith Arus](maps.html#map:10023000) only joins in once his shop is open.
- Bring at least **3** reinforcement crystals of any kind, for example 3 × [Life Crystal](upgrade-crystals.md#item:90000043) ([where to find it](upgrade-crystals.md)), and at least **15,000 G** for the fees.

### 2. Ask Arus for help

- While wearing the cursed armor, go to [Uptown](maps.html#map:10023000) and talk to [Apprentice Blacksmith Arus](maps.html#map:10023000). He sends you to his partner in [Lower Part of Iron City](maps.html#map:10066000).
   **Apprentice Blacksmith Arus**

### 3. Visit Arus's partner

- Go to [Lower Part of Iron City](maps.html#map:10066000) and enter the **house with the blue roof** at the top middle of the map. Talk to **Arus's Significant Other** while wearing the cursed armor. She asks you to strengthen it first.
   **Arus's Significant Other**

### 4. Reinforce the armor to +3 or higher

- Take the armor off and reinforce it to **+3 or higher** with the **Ironworks Receptionist** in the [Forge Lobby](maps.html#map:10066000), on the same map. Each try costs **5,000 G**. See [Equipment Upgrades](equipment-upgrades.md#reinforcement).

### 5. Lift the curse

- Put the reinforced armor (+3 or higher) back on and talk to **Arus's Significant Other**. Choose to **lift the curse**. She takes you to a room for the ritual.
- Talk to her there to finish lifting the curse, then talk to her once more back in her house.

## Good to know

- **The armor must be at least +3.** **Arus's Significant Other** checks the armor you are wearing on your upper body.
- **Each character must talk to [Blacksmith Smiff](maps.html#map:20003000).** Helping another character buy their armor doesn't start the quest for you.
