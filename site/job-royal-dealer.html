    Royal Dealer

    Chronicle job · Backpacker

    A job that combines Trader and Gambler skills, with card and gold attacks that grow with the gold you carry.

    Job change

    Requirement: Lv 100 or higher, Job Level 50 in either Trader or Gambler, and at least one win over the Emil Dragon.

    1. Travel to Northern Promenade. When you meet the requirements, a mysterious creature (a cat) appears there for you to talk to. If it doesn't appear, you don't meet them yet.

    2. Talk to the creature until it runs away.

      ? ? ?

    3. Go to the Northern Cafe in Northern Promenade and talk to Valeria.

      Valeria

    4. Chase the creature: find it again in Northern Promenade and follow it into the Queen Audience Room.

    5. Follow the creature into the magic circle.

    6. In the White World, walk straight ahead to the creature sleeping there. It asks you 3 questions.

      ? ? ?
      • Answers: choose not to wake it, then answer that it is real. Any answer works for the third question.
      • A wrong answer sends you back. Walk to the creature again.

    7. In the room with the book (White World), talk to Mucha.

      Mucha

    8. Look inside the White Book. It sends you to face Canon, the opponent of the Merchant line. Then return to the room with the book.

      White Book

    9. Talk to Mucha again.

    10. Look inside the White Book and choose Moment of Emptiness (Time). There you earn the Hero Proof and the Soul Proof.
    11. Defeat the monsters in that room for small fire orbs and large fire orbs.
    12. Combine 50 small fire orbs or 1 large fire orb into 1 talisman. A golem there also sells talismans.
      • The talisman comes out red or blue at random.
      • Merchant line: you need the blue one, like every Backpacker line.
    13. Use the talisman to fight a copy of yourself. You have 10 minutes.
      • Merchant line: you skip this fight, like every Backpacker line. Go on to the next step.

    14. Fight Canon, the opponent of your line.

      Canon
      • HP regenerates on its own on this map.

    15. Look inside the White Book again. Take off your equipment first. You become a Royal Dealer and reincarnate into the higher form of your race (see Races). Your level and Job Level go back to 1.

      • You keep your skills, but you raise them again.
