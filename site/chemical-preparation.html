    Chemical Preparation

    Chemical Preparation mixes herbs, water and other materials into potions, powders and paints.

    On this page
    • How it works
    • Make with an NPC
    • Potions
    • Status potions
    • Powders, paints and hair colours
    • Crafting materials
    • Related

    How it works

    • Chemical Preparation is an Alchemist skill, learned at Job Level 15, with 5 levels.
    • Each skill level opens the recipes of that level. The Crafting page lists every Chemical Preparation recipe.
    • Almost every recipe succeeds 99% of the time. A failed try gives a Failure Compound or Muddy Water.
    • Rotten Blood, PopPop Powder and Snow Drop succeed 90% of the time.

    Make with an NPC

    Talk to one of the NPCs below with the materials in your inventory. Each makes every recipe up to Lv 5.

    The fee depends on the recipe level:

    Recipe level Fee
    Lv 1 0 G
    Lv 2 200 G
    Lv 3 300 G
    Lv 4 500 G
    Lv 5 1,000 G

    Recipes made from these materials have their own fee:

    • Rainbow Water: 400 G
    • Big Rainbow Water: 4,000 G
    • True Rainbow Water: 40,000 G

    They stand in these places:
