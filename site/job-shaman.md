# Shaman

Source page: [job-shaman.html](job-shaman.html)

Base job · Spell User

A job that masters the four elements, hits elemental weaknesses and gives weapons and armor an element.

## Job change

**Requirement:** MAG 10 or higher.

1. Enter [Uptown](maps.html#map:10023000). You need an **Acropolis City Pass**.
2. Go to the [Guild Palace Lobby 1F](maps.html#map:10023000) in [Uptown](maps.html#map:10023000).
3. Go inside and take the **elevator** to the [Guild Palace Lobby 3F](maps.html#map:10023000).
4. Enter the room on the **east side** of the 3rd floor. This is the [Spell User Guild](maps.html#map:10023000).
5. Talk to the **Spell User Master** and choose **Shaman**.
   **Spell User Master**
6. When asked, **take off your armor**.
7. Talk to the master again. You receive a [Fairy Jade](equipment.html#item:50052400) and learn one spell of your choice: [Fire Bolt](job-shaman.md#skill:3006), [Ice Arrow](job-shaman.md#skill:3029), [Earth Tremor](job-shaman.md#skill:3041) or [Thunder Bolt](job-shaman.md#skill:3017).
