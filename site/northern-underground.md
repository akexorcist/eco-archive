# Northern Underground

Source page: [northern-underground.html](northern-underground.html)

An underground of three areas, reached through the [Northern Palace Secret Passage](maps.html#map:20015001).

## How to enter

The way in is through the [Northern Palace Secret Passage](maps.html#map:20015001), and it starts with two NPCs, **Doro** and **Kosso**, on [Northern Promenade](maps.html#map:10065000).

1. Talk to **Doro** and **Kosso** in the [Northern Cafe](maps.html#map:10065000) on [Northern Promenade](maps.html#map:10065000) about the **hidden treasure**.
   **Doro**
2. They warp you to the door in the west wall of [Northern Promenade](maps.html#map:10065000). Talk to [Doro](maps.html#map:10065000) or [Kosso](maps.html#map:10065000) there, and they warp you into the [Northern Palace Secret Passage](maps.html#map:20015001).
   **Doro**
3. In the [Northern Palace Secret Passage](maps.html#map:20015001), walk to the south end and enter the portal next to [Kosso](maps.html#map:20015001).
   **Kosso**
4. You arrive in the **Northern Underground**, at the bottom edge of the [Northern Underground (Civil Area)](maps.html#map:20210000).
   [Northern Underground (Civil Area)](maps.html#map:20210000)
