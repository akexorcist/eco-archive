    Catty (Yamabuki) Quest

    Help a merchant recover a stolen bag.

    Level
    30 or higher, with 10 Fame
    Items needed
    • 1 × Crystal
    • or 5 × Crystal Fragment
    Reward
    Catty (Yamabuki)
    On this page
    • Before you start
    • Walkthrough
    • Rewards
    • Good to know
    • Related

    Before you start

    • Finish the Catty (Momo) Quest and equip Catty (Momo) for the whole quest.
    • If you are an Emil, find a Titania or Dominion friend to possess for the 5th floor of the Guild Palace.

    Walkthrough

    1. Take the merchant's bag

    • Go to Battleship Island and talk to the Senior Merchant, who stands with a red dragon. Choose the first answer twice to agree to carry his bag.

      Senior Merchant

    2. The bag is stolen

    • Go to Uptown, enter the Guild Palace and go up to the Merchant Guild. Talk to the Merchant Master. You faint, and the bag is stolen.

      Merchant Master

    • When you wake up, people crowd around you. Talk to every one of them until the guard says you may go home and the Merchant Master tells you to report to the Senior Merchant.

    3. Look for clues

    • Go back to the Senior Merchant on Battleship Island and tell him. He sends you to the Guide Machine.

    • Talk to the Guide Machine in front of the Guild Palace in Uptown.

      Guide Machine

    • Talk to the Seamstress in Uptown. She thinks the thief was a child.

      Seamstress

    • Talk to the Guide Machine again. Your Catty mentions a hidden room.

    4. Get through the barrier

    • Go to the 5th floor of the Guild Palace and try the north door. A barrier blocks the way.

    • Go to Downtown and talk to the Junk Shopkeeper in the Junk Shop. Give him a Crystal, or 5 × Crystal Fragment, and he makes you a Resonance Crystal.

      Junk Shopkeeper

    5. Fight the Suspicious Boy

    • Go back to the 5th floor and enter the north door. Nobody can be possessing you here.
    • Talk to the Suspicious Boy. He calls a robot to fight you. Defeat it and pick up the robot arm it drops.
      • The robot can't move but attacks fast and casts spells on the ground; stay out of them. Ranged and magic attacks work well.
      • If you die, you can go back in and fight again without a new Crystal.

    6. Return the bag

    • Bring the robot arm to the Merchant Master in the Merchant Guild.
    • Report to the Senior Merchant on Battleship Island. He gives you Catty (Yamabuki), the most energetic of the cat spirits. Your Catty (Momo) stays with you.

    Rewards

    • Catty (Yamabuki): skills and details on Catties

    Good to know

    • If you leave the room before talking to everyone, the quest doesn't move on, and nothing tells you why.
    • No bandage needed. Unlike the Catty (Momo) Quest, this quest doesn't need a Seamstress Triangle Bandage.
