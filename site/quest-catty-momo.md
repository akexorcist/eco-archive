# Catty (Momo) Quest

Source page: [quest-catty-momo.html](quest-catty-momo.html)

Get Momo, your first Catty. You need her for every other Catty quest.

## Before you start

| Item | Used for | Where to get it |
|---|---|---|
| [Cotton Ribbon](materials.html#item:10043700) | The bandage | The **Seamstress** in [Uptown](maps.html#map:10023000) makes it from 1 × [Cloth](materials.html#item:10021300) for **100 G**. You need [Scissors](materials.html#item:10031500) in your inventory. |
| [Cloth](materials.html#item:10021300) | The bandage, and the [Cotton Ribbon](materials.html#item:10043700) | Sewing shops, **300 G** |
| [Thread](materials.html#item:10019800) | The bandage | Sewing shops, **750 G**, or a drop from [Earth Spider](monsters.html#monster:10210000) |
| [Needle](materials.html#item:10019700) | The bandage | Sewing shops, **1,000 G** |
| [Light Summonstone](materials.html#item:10011210) | The [Light Fairy](maps.html#map:10034000) | Gather from a [Light Crystal](monsters.html#monster:30030004) in [Acronia Eastern Beach](maps.html#map:10034000) |
| **Piece of Mind** | Travel to **Titus** | A drop from [Angel Feather](monsters.html#monster:10270000) in [North Plateau](maps.html#map:10049000) |

## Walkthrough

### 1. Talk to the Trash Can

- Go to the **Trash Can** in [Uptown](maps.html#map:10023000).
   **Trash Can**
- Talk to the **Trash Can**. A trade window opens: press **Cancel**, then talk to it again. Repeat until it gives you an **Unknown Mustache**.
   - If you stop and come back later, keep talking to the **same** **Trash Can**.
- Use the **Unknown Mustache**. It teleports you to the **Insmouse**.
- Talk to the **Insmouse**.
   **Insmouse**

### 2. Visit the Astrologer and the White Church

- Go to the [Astrologer's House](maps.html#map:10024000) in [Downtown](maps.html#map:10024000). Talk to **Talented Astrologer Remia**.
   **Talented Astrologer Remia**
- Go to the [White Church](maps.html#map:10023000) in [Uptown](maps.html#map:10023000). Talk to **White Church Vates**.
   **White Church Vates**

### 3. Help the Light Fairy

- Go to [Acronia Eastern Beach](maps.html#map:10034000).
- Find the [Light Fairy](maps.html#map:10034000). Give her the [Light Summonstone](materials.html#item:10011210).
   **Light Fairy**
- Return to the **White Church Vates** in the [White Church](maps.html#map:10023000).

### 4. Have the bandage made

- Go to the [Seamstress' House](maps.html#map:10023000) in [Uptown](maps.html#map:10023000). Talk to the **Seamstress**. She makes a **Seamstress Triangle Bandage** from your:
   **Seamstress**
   - [Cotton Ribbon](materials.html#item:10043700)
   - [Thread](materials.html#item:10019800)
   - [Cloth](materials.html#item:10021300)
   - [Scissors](materials.html#item:10031500)

### 5. Meet Titus

- Use the **Piece of Mind**. It teleports you to the [Titania Transmission Room](maps.html#map:10023000).
- Talk to **Titus**. At the end, choose **Live Together**.
   **Titus**
   - If you choose **Drive Away**, you can't get the Catty.
- You receive [Catty (Momo)](partners.html#item:10017900).

## Rewards

- [Catty (Momo)](partners.html#item:10017900): skills and details on [Catties](catties.md#momo)

## Good to know

- **Make the [Cotton Ribbon](materials.html#item:10043700) before you start.** Once the quest begins, the **Seamstress** won't make one for this character. Have another character make it, or buy one.
- **1 × **Piece of Mind** takes the whole party to **Titus**.**
