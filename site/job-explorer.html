    Job change

    Requirement: Job Level 30 or higher as a Ranger.

    1. Go to the Guild Palace Lobby 1F in Uptown.
    2. Go inside and take the elevator to the Guild Palace Lobby 4F.
    3. Enter the room on the south side of the 4th floor. This is the Ranger Guild.

    4. Talk to the Ranger Master and choose to change your job. He asks you to bring an Ice Flower(Event).

      Ranger Master

    5. Get the Ice Flower(Event) from an Icy. Transform into a Marionette before you talk to them. Ice Flowers melt in human hands, so they won't hand it over otherwise.

      • On Icy Island, talk to the Icy Chief. You need a Mysterious Crystal to enter the island.
      Icy Chief
      • On North Mountain, bring 1 × Happy Flower and talk to the Walking Icy. She freezes the flower into an Ice Flower(Event).
      • Only a plain Happy Flower works, not an elemental one. It is crafted from a Flower Seed and Mana Water.
      Walking Icy

    6. Return to the Ranger Master in the Ranger Guild (Guild Palace Lobby 4F, south room) and hand over the Ice Flower(Event).

    7. Choose to become an Explorer. When asked, take off your armor.
    8. You are now an Explorer. Your Job Level goes back to 1.
