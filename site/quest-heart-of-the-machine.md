# Heart of the Machine

Source page: [quest-heart-of-the-machine.html](quest-heart-of-the-machine.html)

Search the ruins under Acropolis for the past of the **DEM Girl** at [Emil's House](maps.html#map:10024000).

## Walkthrough

### 1. Go to Emil's House

- Go to [Emil's House](maps.html#map:10024000), [Downtown](maps.html#map:10024000) and talk to **Emil**.
   **Emil**

### 2. Ask Dr. Quark

- Talk to the **Lab Guide** in [Downtown](maps.html#map:10024000) and choose to go to the [Electell Laboratory](maps.html#map:10024000).
   **Lab Guide**
- In the [Electell Laboratory](maps.html#map:10024000), talk to **Dr. Quark** about the **DEM Girl**. Talk to him again to go down to the ruins.
   **Dr. Quark**

### 3. Search the ruins

- In the [Acropolis Underground Ruin](maps.html#map:10024000), go to the middle of the map and take the stairs down. Talk to the **Broken DEM**.
   **Broken DEM**
- The story moves to the **Mysterious Maid Flying Garden**. Talk to **A Strange Maid** there.
   **A Strange Maid**
   - Each time the scene changes, walk up to the NPC in front of you and talk to them.

### 4. Defeat the Foxhound E200

- Enter the warp portal and talk to **Emil** in the [Acropolis Underground Ruin](maps.html#map:10024000).
   **Emil**
- Defeat the [Foxhound E200](monsters.html#monster:10820151) together with **Emil**. He can defeat it on his own.
- Pick up the **Unlock Key (Event)** it drops and leave through the warp portal.

### 5. Collect the Heart Machine

- Go back to [Emil's House](maps.html#map:10024000), [Downtown](maps.html#map:10024000) and talk to the **DEM Girl**. She gives you the **Heart Machine**.
   **DEM Girl**
- Return to **Dr. Quark** in the [Electell Laboratory](maps.html#map:10024000), [Downtown](maps.html#map:10024000) and report to him.
   - If **Philip** is not in [Emil's House](maps.html#map:10024000) afterwards, talk to **Dr. Quark** about the **DEM Girl** once or twice more.

## Good to know

- **Keep the **Heart Machine**.** The **Automata (Amethyst)** quest needs it, together with Lv 20 and 21 Fame.
- **Emil, Titania and Dominion characters get a different story.** They use a **Container Key (Event)** to find a **Wounded Core (Event)** for the **DEM Girl** and get 1 skill point.
