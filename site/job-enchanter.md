# Enchanter

Source page: [job-enchanter.html](job-enchanter.html)

Technical job · Spell User

A support job that turns the ground into elemental areas and buffs the party with elemental auras.

## Job change

**Requirement:** MAG 30 or higher, as an Elementalist at any Job Level.

1. Go to the [Master Sage's House](maps.html#map:10023000) in [Uptown](maps.html#map:10023000).
2. Talk to the **Sage Master** and choose to browse the items **on top of the shelves**.
   **Sage Master**
3. Buy a **Forbidden Book** for **100,000 G**.
4. Talk to the **Sage Master** again and choose to change your job.
5. When asked, **take off all your equipment**.
6. Choose the skills to take with you: **1 skill**, plus 1 more for every 10 Job Levels. See [Job Switching](job-switching.md).
7. You are now an Enchanter. You can switch back to Elementalist the same way.
