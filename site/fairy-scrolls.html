    Fairy Scrolls

    Four Sage skills seal an element spell into a scroll.

    On this page
    • How it works
    • Blank scrolls
    • Scroll recipes
    • Spells by skill level
    • Related

    How it works

    • Each element has its own skill with 5 levels. They are learned at:
      • Earth Fairys Scroll: Job Level 3
      • Water Fairys Scroll: Job Level 6
      • Wind Fairys Scroll: Job Level 9
      • Fire Fairys Scroll: Job Level 12
    • Each try uses 1 × Scroll and 1 summonstone of the same element. It costs no fee.
    • The skill level decides the spell on the scroll (table below).
    • A try gives one of these:
      • 80%: the spell scroll
      • 5%: a shrink scroll
      • 5%: an enlarge scroll
      • 10%: a Failure Compound
    • Shrink and enlarge scrolls make your character smaller or bigger for 30 minutes. The effect ends when you change maps.
    • A shrink or enlarge scroll looks the same as the spell scroll of its element. The item details show which one it is: Minimum (shrink), Maximum (enlarge), or the spell name.
    • Each scroll can be used 10 times.

    Blank scrolls

    Buy a Scroll for 1,500 G from the Sage Master in the Master Sage's House, Uptown.

    Sage Master

    Any of the four skills can also make one at Lv 1 (recipe):

    • 1 × Pulp Roll
    • 1 × Feather Pen
