    Job Switching

    Switching between your Expert job and its Technical job, such as Blade Master and Bounty Hunter.

    On this page
    • How to switch
    • What happens when you switch
    • Reserve skills
    • You can switch back and forth as often as you like. There is no level requirement.
    • You can only switch within your own job line, never to another line.

    How to switch

    • Talk to the Sage Master in the Master Sage's House, Uptown and choose to change to your Technical job.

      Sage Master

    • Each switch needs a Forbidden Book. The Sage Master sells it for 100,000 gold.

    • The first switch doesn't use up the Forbidden Book. It's used up on the next switch.

    What happens when you switch

    • Each job keeps its own Job Level. Coming back to a job lets you continue from where you left off.
    • Your experience bar for the level goes back to 0%.
    • The skills of the job you switch to are reset, so you can spend their skill points again.
    • Switching back to a job you already trained lowers its Job Level. Each Memory Hourglass you bring restores 1 Job Level. The Sage Master sells it for 50,000 gold.
    • If the other job is still at Job Level 1, nothing is lost.

    Reserve skills

    When you switch, you can take some skills from the job you're leaving. They go into the Reserve slots.

    Job Level of the job you leave Skills you can take
    1–9 1
    10–19 2
    20–29 3
    30–39 4
    40–49 5
    50 6
    • Only skills you've already learned can be taken, at the level you learned them. Max out a skill before you switch if you want to take it.
    • You choose them once, when you switch. To choose different ones, switch back and switch again.
    • Reserve skills can be active or passive, but you can't level them up.
