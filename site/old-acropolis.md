# Old Acropolis

Source page: [old-acropolis.html](old-acropolis.html)

A dungeon reached through the [Underground Water Facility](maps.html#map:22198000).

## How to enter

1. Go to the [Underground Water Facility](maps.html#map:22198000) (steps under [Underground Water Facility](underground-water-facility.md#how-to-enter)).
2. Walk into the passage on the [Underground Water Facility](maps.html#map:22198000). You arrive in the [Underground Waterway](maps.html#map:12035000).
   [Underground Water Facility](maps.html#map:22198000)
3. In the [Underground Waterway](maps.html#map:12035000), walk into the passage at (213, 211). You arrive in [Old Acropolis B1F](maps.html#map:22200000).
4. For [Old Acropolis B2F](maps.html#map:22201000), walk into the passage on [Old Acropolis B1F](maps.html#map:22200000).
   [Old Acropolis B1F](maps.html#map:22200000)
