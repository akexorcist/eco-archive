# Lv 55 Armor Quest

Source page: [quest-lv55-armor.html](quest-lv55-armor.html)

Errands that unlock each job group's **Lv 55 armor shop**.

## Walkthrough

### 1. Spell Users: Talented Astrologer Remia

- Go to the [Astrologer's House](maps.html#map:10024000) in [Downtown](maps.html#map:10024000) and talk to **Talented Astrologer Remia**. You need at least **10 [Fame](fame.md)**. She asks for a Crystal filled with the power of a fairy.
   **Talented Astrologer Remia**
- Get a [Crystal](materials.html#item:10014300).
- Take the Crystal to any one of the six element fairies. The fairy fills it with its element.
   - **Light**: [Light Fairy](maps.html#map:10034000) in [Acronia Eastern Beach](maps.html#map:10034000)
      **Light Fairy**
   - **Dark**: [Dark Fairy](maps.html#map:10019000) in [Island of the Undead](maps.html#map:10019000)
      **Dark Fairy**
   - **Fire**: [Fire Fairy](maps.html#map:10042000) in [Staff Desert](maps.html#map:10042000)
      **Fire Fairy**
   - **Water**: [Water Fairy](maps.html#map:10002000) in [Snov Snowfield](maps.html#map:10002000)
      **Water Fairy**
   - **Earth**: [Earth Fairy](maps.html#map:10018000) in [East Cape](maps.html#map:10018000)
      **Earth Fairy**
   - **Wind**: [Wind Fairy](maps.html#map:10035000) in [Battleship Island](maps.html#map:10035000)
      **Wind Fairy**
- Bring the Crystal back to Remia. She gives you a Time-Space Staff of that element and adds the Lv 55 armor to her shop under **"New goods"**.
   | Fairy | Staff you get |
   |---|---|
   | Light | [Time-Space Light Staff](equipment.html#item:60072054) |
   | Dark | [Time-Space Dark Staff](equipment.html#item:60072055) |
   | Fire | [Time-Space Fire Staff](equipment.html#item:60072050) |
   | Water | [Time-Space Water Staff](equipment.html#item:60072051) |
   | Earth | [Time-Space Earth Staff](equipment.html#item:60072053) |
   | Wind | [Time-Space Wind Staff](equipment.html#item:60072052) |
   **Wizard, Sorcerer, Sage**
   [**Magic Pants(M)** 8,000 G](equipment.html#item:50014150) [**Magic Robe(M)** 45,000 G](equipment.html#item:50007050) [**Magic Robe(F)** 53,000 G](equipment.html#item:50003950)
   **Shaman, Elementalist, Enchanter**
   [**Festive Capri Pants(M)** 7,500 G](equipment.html#item:50014250) [**Festive Robe(M)** 49,500 G](equipment.html#item:50007150) [**Festive Robe(F)** 57,000 G](equipment.html#item:50004050)
   **Vates, Druid, Bard**
   [**Fortune Robe(F)** 45,500 G](equipment.html#item:50004450) [**Fortune Robe(M)** 45,500 G](equipment.html#item:50007250)
   **Warlock, Cabalist, Necromancer**
   [**Black Chaos Robe(F)** 50,000 G](equipment.html#item:50007351) [**Black Chaos Robe(M)** 50,000 G](equipment.html#item:50004551)
- Do it once more with a second filled Crystal and Remia gives you **1 skill point**.

### 2. Backpackers: Novice Adventurer Porol

- At a [Quest Counter](quest-counter.md), take the quest **Mother's Medicine**. It has no time limit.
- Collect 1 of each petal. [Flower Petals](petals.md) shows where to find them:
   A Farmer's plant skill makes them much easier to get. - Go to [Northern Promenade](maps.html#map:10065000), enter the [Northern Magic Shop](maps.html#map:10065000) and talk to the **Magic Shopkeeper**. She takes the petals and gives you **Northern Remedy(Quest)**.
   **Magic Shopkeeper**
   - [Red Petal](materials.html#item:10043201)
   - [Blue Petal](materials.html#item:10043203)
   - [White Petal](materials.html#item:10043204)
   - [Yellow Petal](materials.html#item:10043207)
   - [Orange Petal](materials.html#item:10043208)
   - [Black Petal](materials.html#item:10043209)
- Hand the Northern Remedy in at the Quest Counter. You get a **Reward Ticket for Porol**.
- Go to [Lower Part of Iron City](maps.html#map:10066000) and give the ticket to [Novice Adventurer Porol](maps.html#map:10066000). He gives you a [Metal Urchin Spine](materials.html#item:10025210) and opens his shop of boys' and girls' clothes.
   **Novice Adventurer Porol**
   Names with (M) are for men and (F) for women, but [Geologist Pants(M)](equipment.html#item:50014350) is for women.
   **Miner (Tatarabe), Blacksmith, Engineer (Machinery)**
   [**Geologist Pants(M)** 5,200 G](equipment.html#item:50014350) [**Miner Pants(M)** 5,200 G](equipment.html#item:50012850) [**Geologist Arm Guard(F)(Drapery** 58,000 G](equipment.html#item:50007450) [**Geologist Arm Guard(M)** 58,000 G](equipment.html#item:50004250)
   **Farmer, Alchemist, Marionettist**
   [**Lumberjack Pants(M)** 7,000 G](equipment.html#item:50014450) [**Garden Dress(F)** 49,000 G](equipment.html#item:50004650) [**Lumberjack T-Shirt(M)** 55,500 G](equipment.html#item:50007550)
   **Ranger, Explorer, Treasure Hunter**
   [**Safari Pants(M)** 6,000 G](equipment.html#item:50013250) [**Safari Skirt(F)** 6,000 G](equipment.html#item:50014550) [**Safari Jacket(F)** 52,000 G](equipment.html#item:50007650) [**Safari Jacket(M)** 52,000 G](equipment.html#item:50004350)
   **Merchant, Trader, Gambler**
   [**Business Pants(M)** 12,800 G](equipment.html#item:50014650) [**Business Jacket(M)** 63,000 G](equipment.html#item:50007750) [**Work Dress(F)** 65,900 G](equipment.html#item:50004750)
- Do the quest a second time and bring Porol another ticket. He gives you **1 skill point**.

### 3. Fighters: Ghost Buster Gadoo

- At the [Quest Counter](quest-counter.md) in [Northern Promenade](maps.html#map:10065000), take the delivery quest **Before Melting! (Gadoo)**. You have **30 minutes**.
- Pick up the package from **Valeria** in the [Northern Cafe](maps.html#map:10065000), next to the save point.
   **Valeria**
- Run south to [Ghost Shelter](maps.html#map:10061000) and talk to [Ghost Buster Gadoo](maps.html#map:10061000).
   **Ghost Buster Gadoo**
- Gadoo gives you [Ghost Blood](materials.html#item:10000201) and **1 skill point**, and opens his shop of fighter armor:
   **Swordsman, Blade Master, Bounty Hunter**
   [**Metal Armor(F)** 65,000 G](equipment.html#item:60002350) [**Metal Armor(M)** 65,000 G](equipment.html#item:60001450)
   **Fencer, Knight, Dark Stalker**
   [**Black Guardian Armor(F)** 67,000 G](equipment.html#item:60002450) [**Black Guardian Armor(M)** 67,000 G](equipment.html#item:60001550)
   **Scout, Assassin, Commando**
   [**Midnight Dress(M)** 98,000 G](equipment.html#item:50006950) [**Midnight Suit(F)** 98,000 G](equipment.html#item:50003851)
   **Archer, Striker, Gunner**
   [**Protection Pants(M)** 5,800 G](equipment.html#item:50014050) [**Protection Shoes(M)** 32,200 G](equipment.html#item:50062050) [**Protection Set(Bottom)(F)** 38,000 G](equipment.html#item:50012650) [**Protection Jacket(M)** 55,000 G](equipment.html#item:50006850) [**Protection Suit(Top)(F)** 55,000 G](equipment.html#item:50004150)

## Good to know

- **Buy a **Space Time Key** (500 G) from the guild merchant before the Fighter delivery.** The 30 minutes are tight on the long road south.
- **Any element works for Remia.** The element only changes which staff you get.
