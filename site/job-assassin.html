    Job change

    Requirement: STR 30 or higher, not counting equipment, and Job Level 30 or higher as a Scout.

    1. Go to the Guild Palace Lobby 1F in Uptown.
    2. Go inside and take the elevator to the Guild Palace Lobby 2F.
    3. Enter the room on the south side of the 2nd floor. This is the Scout Guild.

    4. Talk to the Scout Master and choose Assassin. The master asks you to collect four potions from four people around the continent. The first password hint is "Sky".

      Scout Master
      • Visit the four people in the order below. If you choose a wrong answer, go back to the previous person to hear the hint again.

    5. Travel to Battleship Island and talk to the Thief Guildsman. Choose "I want to fly there" (2nd option). He gives you an Assassin Potion1(Quest).

      Thief Guildsman

    6. Travel to Acronia Eastern Beach and talk to the Thief Guildsman. Choose "It's a drink!" (3rd option). He gives you an Assassin Potion2(Quest).

      Thief Guildsman

    7. Travel to Cape Northerin and talk to the Black Marketeer. Choose "I've been alright" (1st option). He gives you an Assassin Potion3(Quest).

      Black Marketeer

    8. Travel to Iron Volcano through the southeast exit of Iron South Street, and talk to Cutesy Python. Choose "It depends on what you think about the heat!" (3rd option). She gives you an Assassin Potion4(Quest).

      Cutesy Python

    9. Return to the Scout Master in the Scout Guild (Guild Palace Lobby 2F, south room) and hand over the four potions. Choose to become an Assassin.

    10. When asked, take off your armor. The master gives you the Assassin mark, and you are now an Assassin.
