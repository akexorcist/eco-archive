# Catty (Yamabuki) Quest

Source page: [quest-catty-yamabuki.html](quest-catty-yamabuki.html)

Help a merchant recover a stolen bag.

## Before you start

- Finish the [Catty (Momo) Quest](quest-catty-momo.md) and equip [Catty (Momo)](partners.html#item:10017900) for the whole quest.
- If you are an **Emil**, find a Titania or Dominion friend to possess for the 5th floor of the Guild Palace.

## Walkthrough

### 1. Take the merchant's bag

- Go to [Battleship Island](maps.html#map:10035000) and talk to the [Senior Merchant](maps.html#map:10035000), who stands with a red dragon. Choose the **first answer twice** to agree to carry his bag.
   **Senior Merchant**

### 2. The bag is stolen

- Go to [Uptown](maps.html#map:10023000), enter the **Guild Palace** and go up to the [Merchant Guild](maps.html#map:10023000). Talk to the **Merchant Master**. You faint, and the bag is stolen.
   **Merchant Master**
- When you wake up, people crowd around you. **Talk to every one of them** until the guard says you may go home and the **Merchant Master** tells you to report to the [Senior Merchant](maps.html#map:10035000).

### 3. Look for clues

- Go back to the [Senior Merchant](maps.html#map:10035000) on [Battleship Island](maps.html#map:10035000) and tell him. He sends you to the [Guide Machine](maps.html#map:10023000).
- Talk to the [Guide Machine](maps.html#map:10023000) in front of the Guild Palace in [Uptown](maps.html#map:10023000).
   **Guide Machine**
- Talk to the **Seamstress** in [Uptown](maps.html#map:10023000). She thinks the thief was a child.
   **Seamstress**
- Talk to the [Guide Machine](maps.html#map:10023000) again. Your Catty mentions a hidden room.

### 4. Get through the barrier

- Go to the **5th floor** of the Guild Palace and try the **north door**. A barrier blocks the way.
- Go to [Downtown](maps.html#map:10024000) and talk to the **Junk Shopkeeper** in the [Junk Shop](maps.html#map:10024000). Give him a [Crystal](materials.html#item:10014300), or 5 × [Crystal Fragment](materials.html#item:10014500), and he makes you a **Resonance Crystal**.
   **Junk Shopkeeper**

### 5. Fight the Suspicious Boy

- Go back to the 5th floor and enter the north door. Nobody can be possessing you here.
- Talk to the **Suspicious Boy**. He calls a robot to fight you. Defeat it and pick up the robot arm it drops.
   - The robot can't move but attacks fast and casts spells on the ground; stay out of them. Ranged and magic attacks work well.
   - If you die, you can go back in and fight again without a new Crystal.

### 6. Return the bag

- Bring the robot arm to the **Merchant Master** in the [Merchant Guild](maps.html#map:10023000).
- Report to the [Senior Merchant](maps.html#map:10035000) on [Battleship Island](maps.html#map:10035000). He gives you [Catty (Yamabuki)](partners.html#item:10017905), the most energetic of the cat spirits. Your [Catty (Momo)](partners.html#item:10017900) stays with you.

## Rewards

- [Catty (Yamabuki)](partners.html#item:10017905): skills and details on [Catties](catties.md#yamabuki)

## Good to know

- **If you leave the room before talking to everyone, the quest doesn't move on**, and nothing tells you why.
- **No bandage needed.** Unlike the [Catty (Momo) Quest](quest-catty-momo.md), this quest doesn't need a **Seamstress Triangle Bandage**.
