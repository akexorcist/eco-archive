# Fairy Scrolls

Source page: [fairy-scrolls.html](fairy-scrolls.html)

Four [Sage](job-sage.md) skills seal an element spell into a scroll.

## How it works

- Each element has its own skill with 5 levels. They are learned at:
   - [Earth Fairys Scroll](job-sage.md#skill:2033): Job Level 3
   - [Water Fairys Scroll](job-sage.md#skill:2031): Job Level 6
   - [Wind Fairys Scroll](job-sage.md#skill:2032): Job Level 9
   - [Fire Fairys Scroll](job-sage.md#skill:2030): Job Level 12
- Each try uses 1 × [Scroll](crafting.html#recipe:1406) and 1 summonstone of the same element. It costs no fee.
- The skill level decides the spell on the scroll (table below).
- A try gives one of these:
   - 80%: the spell scroll
   - 5%: a shrink scroll
   - 5%: an enlarge scroll
   - 10%: a **Failure Compound**
- Shrink and enlarge scrolls make your character smaller or bigger for 30 minutes. The effect ends when you change maps.
- A shrink or enlarge scroll looks the same as the spell scroll of its element. The item details show which one it is: Minimum (shrink), Maximum (enlarge), or the spell name.
- Each scroll can be used 10 times.

## Blank scrolls

Buy a [Scroll](crafting.html#recipe:1406) for 1,500 G from the **Sage Master** in the [Master Sage's House](maps.html#map:10023000), [Uptown](maps.html#map:10023000).

**Sage Master**

Any of the four skills can also make one at Lv 1 ([recipe](crafting.html#recipe:1406)):

- 1 × [Pulp Roll](materials.html#item:10020407)
- 1 × [Feather Pen](materials.html#item:10031900)
