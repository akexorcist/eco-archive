    Champion Battle

    Player-versus-player fighting on some Dominion World maps.

    On this page
    • How to join
    • Battle maps
    • Rules
    • WRP
    • Field Champions
    • Field Champion shops
    • Related

    How to join

    1. Talk to a Battle Tuner and choose to join the Champion Battle. One stands in each of these places:

      • Battle Tuner in West Fort City

        Battle Tuner

      • Battle Tuner in Sky Tower Island

        Battle Tuner

    2. Go to one of the battle maps and fight other participants.

    Rules

    • You can possess only other participants. Only participants can pick up a possession item.
    • Dying in the battle costs EXP as usual.
    • Some skills work differently against players: see PvP Skill Changes.

    WRP

    WRP (War Record Point) is your Champion Battle score, shown in the status window. You earn it by defeating:

    • Monsters in the Dominion World
    • Other participants:
      • A higher-level player: the level difference + 10 WRP
      • A player of the same or a lower level: 5 WRP

    In a party, the WRP is split between the members on the same map near the fight, at least 1 each. The defeated player loses half the amount.

    Check your rank on a WRP Ranking board. One stands next to each Battle Tuner:

    • WRP Ranking in West Fort City

      WRP Ranking

    • WRP Ranking in Sky Tower Island

      WRP Ranking

    Field Champions

    • The top 10 of the WRP ranking are Field Champions.
    • A Field Champion gets a Champion Mark from either Battle Tuner. If you lose it, talk to a Battle Tuner again.
    • Wear the Champion Mark to use its champion skill. It gives every player around you Champion Monster Killer for 3 minutes: full damage against Champ DEM.
    • In the City Defense Battle, Field Champions earn 1.5 × the CP.
