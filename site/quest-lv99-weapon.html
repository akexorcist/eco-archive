    Lv 99 Weapon Quest

    Turn an Ancient Weapon from the Whale Dungeon into a Lv 99 weapon.

    Level
    84 or higher for the first dragon fight
    Items needed
    • 1 × Dragon Jewel
    • 1 × Rainbow Thread
    • 1 × Dragon Scale
    • Any mix of 2: Iron Cloth, Heavenly Pearl, Illusion Flame
    Quest points
    1 point each:
    • Strongest Dragon End (Party)
    • Dragon at Day's End (Party)
    No points:
    • Lewi's Wish
    • Shiki's Wish
    • Cole's Wish
    Repeatable
    Yes. Each Lv 99 weapon needs a full set of materials
    Reward
    One Lv 99 weapon, chosen at random from a group of three

    Before you start

    • Bring eCoin, the casino coin of ECO Town. 100 G buys 1 eCoin, and eCoin can't be changed back into gold.
    • One weapon costs 50,000 eCoin in fees:
      • 10,000 for the Rainbow Piece
      • 10,000 to turn it into Rainbow Thread
      • 20,000 for the forging
      • 10,000 to free the weapon
    • The Dragon Scale costs 3,000 CP. CP comes from the City Defense Battle.
    • Bring a party for the dragon. Both dragon fights count for the whole party.

    Walkthrough

    1. Defeat the Emil Dragon

    • Go to Abyss of the Depth, the deepest area of the Whale Dungeon. Talk to the Emil Dragon. On the first talk he only invites you to a fight.

      Emil Dragon

    • Talk to him again and choose Challenge. You must be Lv 84 or higher. This starts the quest Strongest Dragon End (Party): a party hunt with a 60-minute limit.

    • Defeat the Emil Dragon (Lv 113).
    • Report back to the dragon. You get a Dragon Jewel.

    2. Ask Hagan about the materials

    • Talk to Hagan in Abyss of the Depth and ask about the Dragon Jewel. He tells you what else he needs.

      Hagan

    3. Collect the season items

    Three people in Abyss of the Depth each trade one material for a small favour. You need two of these items for one weapon. Which two decides the weapon group (see Rewards).

    • Spring: Louise wants 10 × Seismic Throat Candy. She gives you an Iron Cloth.

      Louise
      • Seismic Throat Candy drops from these monsters in Abyss of the Depth:
        • Tiduce Butterfly
        • Teriable Butterfly
        • Robe Night
        • Doom Night
        • Baku Baku
        • Devil Baku Baku
        • Acid Baku Baku
        • Demice

    • Summer: Shiki wants 10 × Super Moisture Drink. She gives you a Heavenly Pearl.

      Shiki
      • Super Moisture Drink drops from these monsters in Abyss of the Depth:
        • Cyclone
        • Death Schyte
        • Dryad
        • Feeler Plant
        • Alcid
        • Robe Night
        • Doom Night

    • Winter: Cole asks you to fetch a parcel. Talk to Erick in Sky Tower Island to get the Dominion String (Quest), then bring it back to Cole. He gives you an Illusion Flame.

      Cole
      Erick

    • Each season quest has no time limit.

    4. Get the Rainbow Thread

    • Go to the Attraction Zone in ECO Town and talk to Fuuka. Pay 10,000 eCoin for a Rainbow Piece.

      Fuuka

    • Return to Abyss of the Depth and talk to Loewen. Give him the Rainbow Piece and 10,000 eCoin. He makes a Rainbow Thread.

      Loewen

    5. Buy the Dragon Scale

    • Go to Silk Gate Passage in West Fort City and talk to Supplier Doga. Buy a Dragon Scale from his bargain goods for 3,000 CP.

      Supplier Doga

    6. Have Hagan forge the weapon

    • Bring everything to Hagan in Abyss of the Depth:
      • Dragon Jewel
      • Rainbow Thread
      • Dragon Scale
      • Two season items
      • 20,000 eCoin
    • Choose the group you want. He forges one Ancient Weapon from that group at random.
      • If you don't have the season items for that group, nothing happens and you keep everything.

    7. Free the weapon

    • Talk to the Emil Dragon while you carry the Ancient Weapon. Pay 10,000 eCoin, and he turns it into its Lv 99 weapon.

    Swords and spears

    Three Ancient Weapons don't come from Hagan. Supplier Doga in Silk Gate Passage sells them for 15,000 CP each. To free one, take it to the Emil Dragon and defeat his true form in a second fight.

    • Ancient Two Handed Sword →
    • Apocalypse Sword: Black Wolf
    • Ancient Spear →
    • Holy Dragon Spear: Titania
    • Ancient Two Handed Spear →
    • Dark Dragon Spear: Dominion
    • The second fight is the quest Dragon at Day's End (Party): a party hunt of Emil Dragon (Lv 250) with a 120-minute limit.
    • After you win, the dragon frees the weapon without an eCoin fee.

    Good to know

    • You need a new set of materials for every weapon. Hagan takes the Dragon Jewel, Rainbow Thread, Dragon Scale and both season items each time.
    • The spring and summer items can be traded. The season quests can be repeated for more.
