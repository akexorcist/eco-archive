    Fishing

    Catching fish with a fishing rod and bait.

    On this page
    • How to fish
    • Where to fish
    • Fishing rods
    • Bait
    • Fish

    How to fish

    1. Equip a fishing rod. A bait slot appears.
    2. Put bait in the bait slot.
    3. Stand by the water at a fishing spot and start fishing. You keep fishing on your own until the bait runs out or you stop.
    4. Each fish goes into your inventory and is recorded in the fishing log that a fishing shop NPC gives you. Sometimes you hook a monster instead.

    Each fish lives in one kind of water: 64 in fresh water, 64 in sea water, 64 in muddy water (see Fish below).

    • Fishing Pond: a Ring Castle attraction, given by a quest from a fishing shop NPC.
      • Its water can be fresh, sea or muddy. After a change, it stays for 60 minutes.
      • Placing it costs Expansion Points.
