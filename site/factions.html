    Factions

    The four armies of Acropolis you can join.

    Joining a faction

    Join a faction by talking to its commander. You need at least 1 Fame. Choose to join the faction, then sign the form.

    • East Faction: the East Faction Commander in the East Faction Commander's Office, through the East Faction Command Center Entrance on the Acropolis East Bridge.

      East Faction Commander

    • West Faction: the West Faction Commander in the West Faction Commander's Office, through the West Faction Command Center Entrance on the Acropolis West Bridge.

      West Faction Commander

    • South Faction: the South Faction Commander in the South Faction Commander's Office, through the South Faction Command Center Entrance on the Acropolis South Bridge.

      South Faction Commander

    • North Faction: the North Faction Commander in the North Faction Commander's Office, through the North Faction Command Center Entrance on the Acropolis North Bridge.

      North Faction Commander

    When you join, the commander gives you your faction's Knight Card and an Acropolis City Pass. Once you have 30 Fame, talk to your commander again for your faction's leather armor.

    Faction Knight Card Armor at 30 Fame
    East Faction Fareast Knight Card East Faction Leather Armor
    West Faction Morg Knight Card Western Faction Leather Armor
    South Faction Iron South Knight Card South Faction Leather Armor
    North Faction Northern Knight Card North Faction Leather Armor
    • You can't leave your faction or change to another one.
    • You don't need to have joined a faction to take part in Knight Training. The side you fight for in a battle doesn't have to be your own faction.

    Gatling Cannon License

    Only South Faction members can get the Gatling Cannon License(Event), which lets you buy a Gatling Cannon.

    1. Talk to the Receptionist in the Joint Committee Office Lobby, Upper Iron City. Choose the Gatling Cannon sales license and show your Iron South Knight Card. Pay 9,200 G for the license.

      Receptionist

    2. Talk to the Factory Receptionist in the Factory Lobby, Lower Part of Iron City. Choose buy a Gatling Cannon and show the license.

      • Pay 300,000 G for the Gatling Cannon.
      • Or say it is too expensive. He offers a quest that rewards a Gatling Cannon instead. It costs 3 quest points. The quests are on How to Get Pets.
      Factory Receptionist

    Faction Power

    • The Faction Power window shows each faction's gauge and your Contribution Points.
    • The leading faction becomes the Faction of the Week. Its members get the bonus while it is in effect. The window also shows next week's bonus.
    • Faction bonuses:
      • EXP Increased
      • Trust Increased
      • Quest Reward x1.25
    • Training bonuses:
      • Airship Flight to Fareast City
      • Airship Flight to Morg City
      • Airship Flight to Upper Iron City
      • Airship Flight to Northern Promenade
