## Job change

**Requirement:** MAG 30 or higher and Job Level 30 or higher as a Warlock.

1. Go to the [Black Church](maps.html#map:10023000) in [Uptown](maps.html#map:10023000).
2. Talk to the **Black Church Vates** and choose to change your job. He asks you to bring two offerings to the darkness.
   **Black Church Vates**
3. Collect **1** of each item.
   - [Canned Cinnamon](materials.html#item:10034000) from [Cinnamon](monsters.html#monster:10111300) on [Iron Volcano](maps.html#map:10064000)
   - **Dark Feather Wing** from [Dark Feather](monsters.html#monster:10271000) on [Eternal North Boundary](maps.html#map:10051000)
4. Return to the **Black Church Vates** in the [Black Church](maps.html#map:10023000) ([Uptown](maps.html#map:10023000)) and choose to become a **Cabalist**.
5. When asked, **take off your equipment** and confirm. The Vates takes the items and gives you the Cabalist mark. You are now a Cabalist.
