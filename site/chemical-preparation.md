# Chemical Preparation

Source page: [chemical-preparation.html](chemical-preparation.html)

[Chemical Preparation](job-alchemist.md#skill:2022) mixes herbs, water and other materials into potions, powders and paints.

## How it works

- [Chemical Preparation](job-alchemist.md#skill:2022) is an [Alchemist](job-alchemist.md) skill, learned at Job Level 15, with 5 levels.
- Each skill level opens the recipes of that level. The [Crafting](crafting.md) page lists every [Chemical Preparation](job-alchemist.md#skill:2022) recipe.
- Almost every recipe succeeds 99% of the time. A failed try gives a **Failure Compound** or [Muddy Water](materials.html#item:10000210).
- [Rotten Blood](crafting.html#recipe:595), [PopPop Powder](materials.html#item:10001400) and [Snow Drop](materials.html#item:10009150) succeed 90% of the time.

## Make with an NPC

Talk to one of the NPCs below with the materials in your inventory. Each makes every recipe up to Lv 5.

The fee depends on the recipe level:

| Recipe level | Fee |
|---|---|
| Lv 1 | 0 G |
| Lv 2 | 200 G |
| Lv 3 | 300 G |
| Lv 4 | 500 G |
| Lv 5 | 1,000 G |

Recipes made from these materials have their own fee:

- [Rainbow Water](materials.html#item:10071800): 400 G
- [Big Rainbow Water](materials.html#item:10071900): 4,000 G
- [True Rainbow Water](materials.html#item:10072000): 40,000 G
