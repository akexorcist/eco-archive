# Lv 45 Armor Quest

Source page: [quest-lv45-armor.html](quest-lv45-armor.html)

Short quests that unlock the **Lv 45 armor** shops for each job line.

## Before you start

- Each shopkeeper only sells the armor of **your current job line**. A Druid, for example, sees the Vates line armor. Change line later and the shop changes.
- Do the quest for your job group: Fighter, Spell User or Backpacker.

## Walkthrough

### 1. Fighters: Arus's Mother

- Go to the [Rich Man's House](maps.html#map:10063100) in [Upper Iron City](maps.html#map:10063100) and talk to **Arus's Mother**. You need to be level 40 or higher and carry **more than 100,000 G**. She only checks the gold; she doesn't take it.
   **Arus's Mother**
- She asks you to find her son. Go to [Uptown](maps.html#map:10023000) and talk to [Apprentice Blacksmith Arus](maps.html#map:10023000).
   **Apprentice Blacksmith Arus**
- Return to **Arus's Mother**. She opens the shop for your job line:

### 2. Spell Users: the Fallen Titania

- Get 1 × [Flower](materials.html#item:10005300).
- Go to [Northern Promenade](maps.html#map:10065000) and enter the [Spell User Guild Headquarters' Lobby](maps.html#map:10065000). Step into the portal inside and choose to **jump in**. It takes you to the [Secret Headquarter Room](maps.html#map:10065000), where the **Fallen Titania** lives. You need to be a Spell User job at level 40 or higher.
   **Fallen Titania**
- Talk to her. She asks you to stay away and sends you back to the lobby.
- Enter the portal again and choose the [Sorcerer Master Room](maps.html#map:10065000). Talk to the **Sorcerer**. He tells you she would feel better with a flower.
   **Sorcerer**
- Go back to the [Secret Headquarter Room](maps.html#map:10065000) and talk to the **Fallen Titania**. She takes the [Flower](materials.html#item:10005300).
- Talk to the **Sorcerer** once more, then return to the **Fallen Titania**. She opens the shop for your job line:

   Expert jobs and later only.
