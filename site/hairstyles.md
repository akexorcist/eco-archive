# Hairstyles

Source page: [hairstyles.html](hairstyles.html)

How to change your hairstyle at the salons in [Uptown](maps.html#map:10023000).

## The two salons

- [Nibelungen Beautician](maps.html#map:10023000) in [Uptown](maps.html#map:10023000) serves women only.
   **Nibelungen Beautician**
- [Helheim Beautician](maps.html#map:10023000) in [Uptown](maps.html#map:10023000) serves men only.
   **Helheim Beautician**

Before you talk to either salon:

- Take off your hat. The salon can't work on hair hidden under a hat.
- End any Marionette transformation first.

## Basic styles

Without a letter you can only cut or restyle your current hair.

| Service | Cost | What it does |
|---|---|---|
| Cut | 3,000 G | Makes your hair shorter. Men can go down to a buzz cut and then a shaved head. |
| Styling | 10,000 G | Changes your hair into another style of about the same length. |

## Growing your hair longer

[Hair Loss Treatment](crafting.html#recipe:513) makes your hair one step longer. Double-click it to use it. If your hair is already as long as it gets, nothing changes and you keep the item.

Make it with [Chemical Preparation](job-alchemist.md#skill:2022) Lv 1 (99% success):

- 1 × [Cuticle Extract](materials.html#item:10001905)
- 1 × [Honey](materials.html#item:10035400)
- 1 × [Resurrection Potion](materials.html#item:10000604)

[Cuticle Extract](materials.html#item:10001905) drops from [Fallen Tree](monsters.html#monster:30060000), [Paper Tree](monsters.html#monster:30040001) and [Driftwood](monsters.html#monster:30060002).
