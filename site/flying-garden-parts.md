# Flying Garden Parts

Source page: [flying-garden-parts.html](flying-garden-parts.html)

The parts that make up your [Flying Garden](flying-garden.md).

## Making it fly

You need your own Flying Garden first.

### 1. Register your Flying Garden

- Go to the [Tonka Government Office](maps.html#map:10062000) on [Tonka Island](maps.html#map:10062000) and talk to the **Tonka Mayor**. Choose **"I want to register my Flying Garden"** and pay **50,000 G**.
   **Tonka Mayor**

### 2. Order the remodel

- Talk to [Meister Selena](maps.html#map:10062000) on [Tonka Island](maps.html#map:10062000) and choose **Remodel Flying Garden**.
   **Meister Selena**
- Choose to pay **10,000 G** and bring the materials yourself. The other choice costs 99,990,000 G.

### 3. Bring 30 Iron Plates

- Bring Selena **30** × [Iron Plate](materials.html#item:10038616). A [Miner (Tatarabe)](job-miner.md) makes them with [Advanced Metal Refining](job-miner.md#skill:2051):
   | Item | Materials | Tool | Recipe |
   |---|---|---|---|
   | [Iron Plate](materials.html#item:10038616) | 1 × [Iron Bullion](materials.html#item:10016100) 1 × [Distilled Water](materials.html#item:10000204) | [Miner Furnace](materials.html#item:10033100) | [Lv 1](crafting.html#recipe:111) |

### 4. Learn the flight rules

- Talk to [Meister Dia](maps.html#map:10062000) next to Selena. She explains the flight rules and fuel.
   **Meister Dia**
- Talk to Selena again. She starts collecting the flight parts.

### 5. Hand in the flight parts

- Bring Selena these parts. You can hand them in one at a time:
   | Part | Amount | How to get it |
   |---|---|---|
   | **Airship Deck** | 1 | Talk to **Captain Pirate** in the [Pirate Assembly Hall](maps.html#map:10054100) |
   | [Turbo Engine](crafting.html#recipe:355) | 1 | Demand it from [Suspicious Man](maps.html#map:20146000) in [Light Tower Bottom Floor](maps.html#map:20146000) |
   | **Miracle** | 1 | From a Pirate Tiny on the ship at [Tiny Island](maps.html#map:10071000) |
   | [Airship Sail](crafting.html#recipe:70) | 2 | Craft it (below) |
   | [Big Airship Sail(Quest)](crafting.html#recipe:71) | 2 | Craft it (below) |
- Craft the sails with [Item Refining](job-miner.md#skill:2009):
   | Item | Materials | Tool | Recipe |
   |---|---|---|---|
   | [Airship Sail](crafting.html#recipe:70) | 1 × [Log](materials.html#item:10028300) 5 × [Waterproof Cloth](materials.html#item:10021301) 5 × [Harder Timber](materials.html#item:10016400) 5 × [Durable Braid](materials.html#item:10024003) | - | [Lv 3](crafting.html#recipe:70) |
   | [Big Airship Sail(Quest)](crafting.html#recipe:71) | 1 × [Big Log](materials.html#item:10028400) 5 × [Waterproof Cloth](materials.html#item:10021301) 5 × [Harder Timber](materials.html#item:10016400) 5 × [Durable Braid](materials.html#item:10024003) | - | [Lv 3](crafting.html#recipe:71) |
- Make their cloth parts with [Tailoring](job-farmer.md#skill:2054):
   | Item | Materials | Tool | Recipe |
   |---|---|---|---|
   | [Waterproof Cloth](materials.html#item:10021301) | 1 × [Noblest Fabric](materials.html#item:10045902) 4 × [Paraffin](materials.html#item:10034507) | - | [Lv 4](crafting.html#recipe:1281) |
   | [Durable Braid](materials.html#item:10024003) | 4 × [Durable Thread](materials.html#item:10019804) | - | [Lv 2](crafting.html#recipe:1231) |

### 6. Choose your sail

- When all parts are in, Selena gives you an **Airship Sail Material List** and finishes the remodel. Choose the sail style:
   - **Rocket**: you get a **Rocket Sail**.
   - **Magic Wing**: you get a **Wing Sail**.
- You also get **10** × **Morg Fossil Fuel** to fly with.
- If you carry a [Catty Booster](materials.html#item:10049500), Selena can fit a **Booster Sail** instead.

|---|---|

| **Wing Sail** | Remodel by [Meister Selena](maps.html#map:10062000), Magic Wing style |
| **Booster Sail** | Trade a [Catty Booster](materials.html#item:10049500) to [Meister Selena](maps.html#map:10062000) |

## Fuel

- Each flight burns **5** × **Morg Fossil Fuel**.
- The [Furniture Carpenter](maps.html#map:10023000) in [Uptown](maps.html#map:10023000) sells it, and so does the [Governmental Refinery](maps.html#map:10060000) in [Morg City](maps.html#map:10060000).

## House

- [Carpenter Tiny](maps.html#map:10071000) on [Tiny Island](maps.html#map:10071000) builds a **Tiny House**. Bring your **Airship Key** and:
   **Carpenter Tiny**
   - 3 × **Tiny Medal**
   - 100 × [Apple](materials.html#item:10002800)
   - 1 × [Small House](crafting.html#recipe:244)
