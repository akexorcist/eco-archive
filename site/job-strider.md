Source page: [job-strider.html](job-strider.html)

## Job change

**Requirement:** Lv 100 or higher, Job Level 50 in either [Explorer](job-explorer.md) or [Treasure Hunter](job-treasure-hunter.md), and at least one win over the [Emil Dragon](monsters.html#monster:14520000).

1. Travel to [Northern Promenade](maps.html#map:10065000). When you meet the requirements, a **mysterious creature** (a cat) appears there for you to talk to. If it doesn't appear, you don't meet them yet.
2. Talk to the creature until it runs away.
   **? ? ?**
3. Go to the [Northern Cafe](maps.html#map:10065000) in [Northern Promenade](maps.html#map:10065000) and talk to **Valeria**.
   **Valeria**
4. Chase the creature: find it again in [Northern Promenade](maps.html#map:10065000) and follow it into the [Queen Audience Room](maps.html#map:10065000).
5. Follow the creature into the **magic circle**.
6. In the [White World](maps.html#map:10065000), walk straight ahead to the creature sleeping there. It asks you 3 questions.
   **? ? ?**
   - Answers: choose **not to wake it**, then answer that **it is real**. Any answer works for the third question.
   - A wrong answer sends you back. Walk to the creature again.
7. In the room with the book ([White World](maps.html#map:10065000)), talk to **Mucha**.
   **Mucha**
8. Look inside the **White Book**. It sends you to face **Prish**, the opponent of the Ranger line. Then return to the room with the book.
   **White Book**
9. Talk to **Mucha** again.
10. Look inside the **White Book** and choose **Moment of Emptiness (Time)**. There you earn two proofs of strength: the **Hero Proof** and the **Soul Proof**.
11. Defeat the monsters in that room for **small fire orbs** and **large fire orbs**.
12. Combine **50** small fire orbs or **1** large fire orb into **1 talisman**. A golem there also sells talismans.
   - The talisman comes out **red** or **blue** at random.
   - **Ranger line:** you need the **blue** one.
13. Use the talisman to fight a **copy of yourself**. You have **10 minutes**.
   - **Ranger line:** you skip this fight.
14. Fight **Prish**, the opponent of your line.
   **Prish**
   - HP regenerates on its own on this map.
15. Look inside the **White Book** again. **Take off your equipment** first. You become a Strider and reincarnate into the higher form of your race (see [Races](races.md)). Your level and Job Level go back to **1**.
   - You keep your skills, but you raise them again.

**Job Lv 47 Pillage Act Active physical attack and steal · Max Lv 5**

- Deals physical damage to enemies in a 5x5 area around the caster.
- For each damaged non-boss monster that has not already been snatched, Pillage Act may steal one item.
- If the caster has matching Knowledge for the monster, the special drop table is checked first, then the normal drop table is used as fallback.
- A successful steal marks that monster so it cannot be stolen from again by Snatch-style effects.

**Job Lv 50 Artful Traps Active area buff · Max Lv 5**

- Buffs valid allies in a 3x3 area around the target.
- When a buffed target is attacked, Artful Traps has a 40% chance to trigger, or 15% in PvP.
- The triggered effect depends on skill level.
- Lv5 only affects player characters.

| Lv | SP | Cast time | Cooldown | Trigger effect |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | 80 | 2 sec | 2 sec | Restores HP equal to (Max ATK + Max MATK) x 3.5 ; undead targets take that amount as damage instead |
| 2 | 80 | 2 sec | 2 sec | Restores SP equal to (Max ATK + Max MATK) x 3.5 |
| 3 | 80 | 2 sec | 2 sec | Restores MP equal to (Max ATK + Max MATK) x 3.5 |
| 4 | 80 | 2 sec | 2 sec | Counterattacks for 100% physical damage and may apply Poison and Stun |
