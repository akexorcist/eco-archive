# Blacksmith

Source page: [job-blacksmith.html](job-blacksmith.html)

Expert job · Backpacker

A job that crafts weapons and armor and fights with two-handed hammers and very high HP.

1. [Miner (Tatarabe) Base](job-miner.md)
2. Blacksmith Expert
3. [⇄](job-switching.md)
4. [Engineer (Machinery) Technical](job-engineer.md)
5. [Maestro Chronicle](job-maestro.md)

## Job change

**Requirement:** Job Level 30 or higher as a Miner (Tatarabe).

1. Go to the [Guild Palace Lobby 1F](maps.html#map:10023000) in [Uptown](maps.html#map:10023000).
2. Go inside and take the **elevator** to the [Guild Palace Lobby 3F](maps.html#map:10023000).
3. Enter the room on the **west (left-hand) side** of the 3rd floor. This is the [Blacksmith Guild](maps.html#map:10023000).
4. Talk to the **Miner Master** and choose to change jobs. He sends you to the [Salamander](maps.html#map:10064000) at [Iron Volcano](maps.html#map:10064000).
   **Miner Master**
5. Get **1** × [Salamander Marionette](materials.html#item:10013850). It drops from [Poisonous Lizard](monsters.html#monster:26040000), which live on [Mothgue Cliff](maps.html#map:10028000) and [Island of the Undead](maps.html#map:10019000).
6. Travel to [Iron Volcano](maps.html#map:10064000).
7. Talk to the [Salamander](maps.html#map:10064000) and choose to listen. It takes the [Salamander Marionette](materials.html#item:10013850) and gives you a **Salamander Heart(Event)**.
   **Salamander**
   - If you are transformed into a marionette, the [Salamander](maps.html#map:10064000) asks you to end it first.
   - The [Salamander](maps.html#map:10064000) asks you to give it a name. Any name works.
8. Use the **Salamander Heart(Event)**.
9. Return to the **Miner Master** in the [Blacksmith Guild](maps.html#map:10023000) ([Guild Palace Lobby 3F](maps.html#map:10023000), west room) and choose to change jobs again.
10. Choose to become a **Blacksmith**. When asked, **take off all your equipment**. The master gives you the Blacksmith mark, and you are now a Blacksmith.
