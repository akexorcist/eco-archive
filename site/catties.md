# Catties

Source page: [catties.html](catties.html)

**Catties** are cat spirits that join you as a partner.

## How Catties work

- A Catty floats on your shoulder and raises your payload and capacity. Most Catties come from quests.
- Your first Catty is always Momo. You need her for every other Catty quest.
- The other quests need **level 30**, some [Fame](fame.md), and a Catty worn while you do them.
- Each colour has different skills. Not every Catty can heal.
- You keep your old Catty when you get a new one.

## Momo

- **Item:** [Catty (Momo)](partners.html#item:10017900)
- **How to get:** [Catty (Momo) Quest](quest-catty-momo.md). Needs level 20 and 10 finished quests.
- The second of the sisters, after Sumire.
- **Outfit:** **Catty (Momo) Kitty Outfit**
- **Skills:**
   - Heal
   - Blazing Spirit Sword
   - High Guard
   - Concentration
   - Resurrection

## Yamabuki

- **Item:** [Catty (Yamabuki)](partners.html#item:10017905)
- **How to get:** [Catty (Yamabuki) Quest](quest-catty-yamabuki.md). Needs level 30 and 10 Fame.
- You find a merchant's stolen bag, and your Catty sniffs out the thief.
- The most energetic Catty. She likes to joke.
- The youngest of the sisters.
- **Skills:**
   - Heal
   - Fight Cheer
   - Critical Encouragement
   - Counterattack

## Ai

- **Item:** [Catty (Ai)](partners.html#item:10017903)
- **How to get:** [Catty (Ai) Quest](quest-catty-ai.md). Needs level 30 and 20 Fame.
- She is trapped in a broken robot in [Fruit Forest](maps.html#map:10030000). You rebuild its body and heart. This quest needs the most items.
- She loves spring festivals and tea ceremonies.
- The fifth of the sisters.
- **Outfit:** **Catty (Ai) Hagoromo**
- She cannot heal.
- **Skills:**
   - Agility
   - Increase Critical
   - Parry

- **Item:** [Catty (Akane)](partners.html#item:10017907)
