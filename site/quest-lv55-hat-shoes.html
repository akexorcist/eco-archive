    Lv 55 Backpacker Hat & Shoes Quest

    Errands that earn each Backpacker job line its Lv 55 hat or shoes.

    Level
    45 or higher
    Items needed
    • Farmer: 22 kinds of food
    • Merchant: 6 golems
    • Ranger: 12 kinds of Jerico
    Reward
    A hat or shoes for your job line, depending on your gender
    On this page
    • Before you start
    • Walkthrough
    • Related

    Before you start

    • Each quest is only for its own job line, including the Expert and Technical jobs of that line.
    • The Farmer and Ranger NPCs take 1 of each item.

    Walkthrough

    1. Farmer line: the Iron City cafe

    • Go to the Iron City Cafe in Upper Iron City and talk to the Cafe Employee. She needs ingredients for her menu.

      Cafe Employee

    • Bring 1 of each:

      • Fruit Juice
      • Bacon
      • Boiled Egg
      • Mushroom Soup
      • Pea Pottage
      • Mushroom Bread
      • Organic Breadfruit Nut
      • Jam
      • Grape Jam
      • Organic Fruit Juice
      • Delicious Kebab
      • Special Stew
      • Delicious Curry
      • Carrot Stick
      • Potato Salad
      • Lettuce
      • Cheese
      • Pine Mushroom
      • Chocolate Chip Biscuit
      • Calcium Biscuit
      • Sweet Coffee
      • Marshmallow

    • She gives you Farm Lady Rubber Band Hat (female) or Lumberjack Boots(M) (male).

    2. Merchant line: Ewan's golems

    • Go to the Igloo in Eternal North Boundary and talk to Ewan. He wants to see your golems.

      Ewan

    • Bring 1 of each golem. He doesn't take them.

      • Veril Golem
      • Mandragora Golem
      • Ice Golem
      • Insmouse Golem
      • Tiny Golem
      • Electell Golem

    • He gives you Business Shoes(F) (female) or Business Beret (male).

    3. Ranger line: Hot-Blooded Red's Jericos

    • Go to Battleship Island and talk to Hot-Blooded Red. He collects Jericos.

      Hot-Blooded Red

    • Bring 1 of each Jerico:

      • Jerico
      • Red Jerico
      • Purple Jerico
      • Blue Jerico
      • Sky Blue Jerico
      • Green Jerico
      • Lime Green Jerico
      • Yellow Jerico
      • Orange Jerico
      • Black Jerico
      • Heavy Jerico
      • White Jerico

    • He gives you Safari Boots(F) (female) or Safari Boots(M) (male).
