    Base job · Fighter

    Job change

    Requirement: STR 10 or higher. There is no level requirement.

    1. Enter Uptown. You need an Acropolis City Pass.
    2. Go to the Guild Palace Lobby 1F in Uptown.
    3. Go inside and take the elevator to the Guild Palace Lobby 2F.
    4. Enter the room on the south side of the 2nd floor. This is the Scout Guild.

    5. Talk to the Scout Master and choose Scout.

      Scout Master

    6. Accept the trial. The master sends you to a field far from town, and you must walk back to the Scout Guild.

      • Emil characters start on Acronia Eastern Beach. Walk through Lake Utena and Acronia Eastern Plains back into Acropolis City.

      • Titania and Dominion characters start on Battleship Island. Walk through Mothgue Cliff, Killer Bee Hill and Acronia Western Plains back into Acropolis City.

      • If you die on the way, you return to the start of the trial.

    7. Return to the Scout Master in the Scout Guild (Guild Palace Lobby 2F, south room) and choose to become a Scout.

    8. When asked, take off your armor.
    9. Talk to the master again. You are now a Scout and receive a Phantom Thief Mask. You also learn Hiding.
