# Refining

Source page: [refining.html](refining.html)

[Item Refining](job-miner.md#skill:2009) turns raw drops into lighter, more useful materials.

## How it works

- [Item Refining](job-miner.md#skill:2009) has 3 levels. It is learned at:
   - [Farmer](job-farmer.md): Job Level 18
   - [Miner (Tatarabe)](job-miner.md): Job Level 20
   - [Ranger](job-ranger.md): Job Level 20
- Each skill level opens the recipes of that level. The [Crafting](crafting.md) page lists every [Item Refining](job-miner.md#skill:2009) recipe.
- Most recipes succeed 90% of the time. A failed try usually gives a **Failure Compound**.

## Refine with an NPC

Talk to an Item Refiner with the items in your inventory. The Item Refiner also does [Chemical Preparation](job-alchemist.md#skill:2022) and [Woodcraft](job-farmer.md#skill:2020) recipes.

- You pay the fee once per order, however many items you refine.
- The fee depends on the recipe level:

| Recipe level | Fee |
|---|---|
| Lv 1 | 0 G |
| Lv 2 | 100 G |
| Lv 3 | 1,000 G |

A few special Lv 3 recipes cost 5,000 G, such as the Potential Stones (like [Red Potential Stone](crafting.html#recipe:80)), [Engraved Jewel](crafting.html#recipe:2267) and [Liquid Fuel](materials.html#item:10032700).
