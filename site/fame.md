# Fame

Source page: [fame.html](fame.html)

**Fame** counts how many quests you have finished.

## Check your Fame

- The game doesn't show your Fame as a number. Talk to the [Bulletin Board Fairy](maps.html#map:10025000) in [Acronia Eastern Plains](maps.html#map:10025000). She tells you how many quests you have finished, which is your Fame.
   **Bulletin Board Fairy**
- [Iron South's Mother](maps.html#map:10066000) in [Lower Part of Iron City](maps.html#map:10066000) also reads your Fame. She describes it in words, not as a number.
   **Iron South's Mother**

## How it works

- Every quest you finish gives **+1 Fame**.
- Every time you **die**, you lose **1 Fame**.
- **Ring Fame** is separate: it counts the quests all members finish while in the Ring. See [Ring](ring.md).

## What needs Fame

| Fame | Unlocks |
|---|---|
| 1 or more | An **Acropolis City Pass** from the [Elegant Lady](maps.html#map:10024000) in [Downtown](maps.html#map:10024000). See [City Passes](city-passes.md) |
| 10 | The [Ancient Ruins](dungeons.md) quests |
| 10 | The Spell User part of the [Lv 55 Armor Quest](quest-lv55-armor.md) |
| 10 | [Catty (Yamabuki) Quest](quest-catty-yamabuki.md) |
| 20 | [Catty (Ai)](quest-catty-ai.md), [Catty (Sumire)](quest-catty-sumire.md), [Catty (Akane)](quest-catty-akane.md) and [Catty (Anzu)](quest-catty-anzu.md) |
| 20 | [Ride Crawler Quest](quest-ride-crawler.md) |
| 100 | The Backpacker part of the [Lv 45 Armor Quest](quest-lv45-armor.md) |

## Good to know

- **Do quests before your third job.** The Ancient Ruins need 10 Fame; a character who already has a third job can earn it in the Endless Corridor quests at [Staff Desert](maps.html#map:10042000) instead.
