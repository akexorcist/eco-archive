# Lv 90 Weapon Quest

Source page: [quest-lv90-weapon.html](quest-lv90-weapon.html)

Clear the arsenal in [Mai Mai Ruins 1st Deck](maps.html#map:20164000) for a random **Lv 90 weapon**.

## Before you start

- Get an **Arsenal Pass Card**. It opens the armory room. It drops from these monsters in [Mai Mai Ruins 1st Deck](maps.html#map:20164000):
   - [Holograph](monsters.html#monster:10970000)
   - [Holograf CD](monsters.html#monster:10970002)
   - [Rebooted Guttinger](monsters.html#monster:10850003)

## Walkthrough

### 1. Accept the mission

- Go to [Mai Mai Ruins 1st Deck](maps.html#map:20164000) and talk to [Machinery Representative](maps.html#map:20164000). Accept the mission **Gate to The Arsenal**. It has a **120-minute** limit.
   **Machinery Representative**

### 2. Defeat the Arsenal Keeper

- Enter the armory room with your **Arsenal Pass Card**.
- Defeat the [Arsenal Keeper](monsters.html#monster:26040901) (Lv 105). It drops a **Vacuum Pack (Private)**.

### 3. Report back

- Return to [Machinery Representative](maps.html#map:20164000) in [Mai Mai Ruins 1st Deck](maps.html#map:20164000) and report.
