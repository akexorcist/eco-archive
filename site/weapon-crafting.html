    Weapon Crafting

    Four crafting skills make weapons, bows, staves, arrows and throwing weapons.

    On this page

    How it works

    • Each skill level opens the recipes of that level. The Crafting page lists every recipe.
    • Finished weapons succeed 100% of the time. Many come out better some of the time:
      • 5%: the +1 version, such as Club +1
      • 3%: the +2 version
      • 1%: the +3 version

    Craft with an NPC

    Talk to one of these NPCs with the materials and the tool in your inventory. Each makes every recipe of the four skills, and also Armor Construction and Advanced Metal Refining recipes.

    Weapon Construction

    • Weapon Construction is a Blacksmith skill, learned at Job Level 10, with 10 levels.
    • Most weapons are joined from parts. Metal parts need a Miner Furnace and metal from Metal Refining.

      • Archer: Job Level 1
