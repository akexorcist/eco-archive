## Job change

**Requirement:** STR 10 or higher.

1. Enter [Uptown](maps.html#map:10023000). You need an **Acropolis City Pass**.
2. Go to the [Guild Palace Lobby 1F](maps.html#map:10023000) in [Uptown](maps.html#map:10023000).
3. Go inside and take the **elevator** to the [Guild Palace Lobby 2F](maps.html#map:10023000).
4. Enter the room on the **east side** of the 2nd floor. This is the [Swordsman Guild](maps.html#map:10023000).
5. Talk to the **Swordsman Master** and choose **Swordsman**.
   **Swordsman Master**
6. Accept his request: bring **1** × [Meat](materials.html#item:10006300).
7. Hunt [Bawoo](monsters.html#monster:10120000) for the Meat. [Bawoo](monsters.html#monster:10120000) live on [Lake Utena](maps.html#map:10032000), [Snov Mountain Road](maps.html#map:10003000) and [Lake Utena Estuary](maps.html#map:10043000).
8. Return to the **Swordsman Master** in the [Swordsman Guild](maps.html#map:10023000) ([Guild Palace Lobby 2F](maps.html#map:10023000), east room) and hand over the Meat.
9. When asked, **take off your armor**.
10. You are now a Swordsman and receive a [Swordsman Token](equipment.html#item:50051300).
