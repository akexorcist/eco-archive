# Catty (Sora) Quest

Source page: [quest-catty-sora.html](quest-catty-sora.html)

A book pulls you into the World of Books, where you save a princess from a demon.

## Before you start

- Finish the [Catty (Momo) Quest](quest-catty-momo.md) and **equip a Catty** for the whole quest.
- No Catty? Possess a player who has done the [Catty (Momo) Quest](quest-catty-momo.md), then enter the bookstore.
- Finish any other quest you have open. The bookstore won't start this one until you do.
- Hit hard enough to beat a demon with **very high magic defense**.

## Walkthrough

### 1. The job board

- Go to [Uptown](maps.html#map:10023000) and check the **Job Request Board** on the east side. Choose the **first topic**.
   **Job Request Board**

### 2. The Rental Library

- Go to [Lake Utena](maps.html#map:10032000). The [Rental Library](maps.html#map:10032000) is in the house up the cliffs in the northeast.
- Wear your Catty and talk to the **Rental Bookstore Owner**. Choose to enter the World of Books.
   **Rental Bookstore Owner**
- Inside the book, **"Stop reading"** takes you back to the real world. **"Turn to the next page"** goes on with the quest.

### 3. The Queen's chamber

- In [Acronia Royal Palace (Queen Chamber)](maps.html#map:10030002), talk to the **Maid** and the **Queen**.
   **Maid**
   **Queen**
- The Queen lets you open one **Treasure Chest**. The **big chest** gives 100 G. The **small chest** gives a [Pururu Staff](equipment.html#item:60072500).

### 4. Across the kingdom

- Take the portal into [Acronia Kingdom Territory](maps.html#map:10030002) and go to the end.
- An [Sinister-looking Adventurer](maps.html#map:10030002) and a [Sinister-looking Merchant](maps.html#map:10030002) guard the next portal. They ask for 10 mushrooms or this much gold: gold = base level × 1,000 ÷ 2 Sneak behind them and you don't pay.
- If you die in the book, you go back to your save point.

### 5. The Holy Volcano

- Climb to the top of [Holy Volcano](maps.html#map:10064002). Wear your Catty and talk to **both** maidens, [Shrine Maiden](maps.html#map:10064002) and [Shrine Maiden](maps.html#map:10064002). Leave with them.
   **Shrine Maiden**

### 6. Save the princess

- In the [Cursed Castle](maps.html#map:10064002), talk to the **Princess**. A demon appears.
   **Princess**
- Defeat the demon and pick up its drop. **Hit it yourself.** If only your pet kills it, nothing drops and you must fight it again.
- Leave through the portal and talk to the **Princess** again. You leave the World of Books.

### 7. The blue bandana

- Talk to the **Rental Bookstore Owner**.
- Bring your Catty to the **Seamstress** in [Uptown](maps.html#map:10023000). She gives you a **Blue Triangle Bandana**.
   **Seamstress**

### 8. Read the book again

- Go back to the [Rental Library](maps.html#map:10032000) and enter the book again. Bring the Catty **whose colour you want for your coat**.
- Talk to the **Queen**. Equip that Catty and talk to the **Maid**. You get the **Acronia Coat** in that Catty's colour, from [Acronia Coat (Momo)](equipment.html#item:60108800) to [Acronia Coat (Sora)](equipment.html#item:60108807). See [Acronia Coat colours](#acronia-coat-colours).
- Cross [Acronia Kingdom Territory](maps.html#map:10030002) and climb [Holy Volcano](maps.html#map:10064002) again. In the kingdom, go around behind the [Sinister-looking Adventurer](maps.html#map:10030002) and the [Sinister-looking Merchant](maps.html#map:10030002); you don't pay a second time.
- Talk to the **Princess**. Defeat the demon again and pick up its drop. This time the demon is **Catty (Sora)** in human form.

### 9. Back to the bookstore

- When the book sends you out, talk to the **Rental Bookstore Owner**. You receive **Catty (Sora)**.

## Good to know

- ****Catty (Sora)** is one per account.**
- **Use the old **Catty (Midori)** for the green coat.** The new Midori can't get a coat.
- **Sketch the demon.** Catty (Sora) in human form can be drawn with [Monster Sketch](job-sage.md#skill:2294) for your [Flying Garden](flying-garden.md). Draw it before you kill it, or leave the quest open to draw again.
- **The [Pururu Staff](equipment.html#item:60072500) fits any job.** It needs level 30 and has 35 magic attack.
