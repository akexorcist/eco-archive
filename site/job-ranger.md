## Job change

**Requirement:** You are a Novice. There is no level requirement.

1. Enter [Uptown](maps.html#map:10023000). You need an **Acropolis City Pass**.
2. Go to the [Guild Palace Lobby 1F](maps.html#map:10023000) in [Uptown](maps.html#map:10023000).
3. Go inside and take the **elevator** to the [Guild Palace Lobby 4F](maps.html#map:10023000).
4. Enter the room on the **south side** of the 4th floor. This is the [Ranger Guild](maps.html#map:10023000).
5. Talk to the **Ranger Master** and choose **Ranger**.
   **Ranger Master**
6. Accept his test: bring **2** × [Bone](materials.html#item:10006600).
7. Hunt [Bawoo](monsters.html#monster:10120000) for the [Bone](materials.html#item:10006600). [Bawoo](monsters.html#monster:10120000) live on [Lake Utena](maps.html#map:10032000), [Snov Mountain Road](maps.html#map:10003000) and [Lake Utena Estuary](maps.html#map:10043000).
8. Return to the **Ranger Master** in the [Ranger Guild](maps.html#map:10023000) ([Guild Palace Lobby 4F](maps.html#map:10023000), south room) and hand over the [Bone](materials.html#item:10006600).
9. When asked, **take off your armor**.
10. Talk to the master again. You are now a Ranger and receive [Neck Goggles](equipment.html#item:50051600). You also learn [Bivouac](job-ranger.md#skill:713).

**Job Lv 1 Bivouac Active self-buff · Max Lv 5**

- Can only be used outside dungeons.
- The character sits in place and recovers HP and SP every 5 seconds; moving or acting cancels Bivouac.
