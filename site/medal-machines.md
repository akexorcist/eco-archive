# Medal Machines

Source page: [medal-machines.html](medal-machines.html)

**Medal machines** give you a random item for a medal.

## Medals

| Medal | How to get it |
|---|---|
| **Emil Medal** | From the beginner tutorial |
| **Copper Medal** | Give 10 × **Torn Magic Book** to the [Village Boy](maps.html#map:10024000) in [Downtown](maps.html#map:10024000) |
| **Silver Medal** | Trade 10 × **Copper Medal** with the [Northern Border Guard](maps.html#map:10001000) in [Cape Northerin](maps.html#map:10001000) |
| **Gold Medal** | Trade 10 × **Silver Medal** with the [Iron South Border Guard](maps.html#map:10046000) in [Cape Southerin](maps.html#map:10046000) |

### Copper Medals from torn books

- Talk to the [Village Boy](maps.html#map:10024000) in [Downtown](maps.html#map:10024000), near the [Elegant Lady](maps.html#map:10024000). He trades **10** × **Torn Magic Book** for a **Copper Medal**.
   **Village Boy**
- **Torn Magic Book** drops often from [Young CockKo](monsters.html#monster:10620000) and sometimes from [Polar Bear](monsters.html#monster:10030400).

### Trade up

- The [Northern Border Guard](maps.html#map:10001000) in [Cape Northerin](maps.html#map:10001000) trades **10** × **Copper Medal** for **1** × **Silver Medal**.
   **Northern Border Guard**
- The [Iron South Border Guard](maps.html#map:10046000) in [Cape Southerin](maps.html#map:10046000) trades **10** × **Silver Medal** for **1** × **Gold Medal**.
   **Iron South Border Guard**

## What you can get

Put in a medal and pick a handle (**Cute**, **Cool** or **Useful**). You get a random item from that group: clothes, accessories, furniture or useful items.

Each machine has its own prize list, and higher medals give better prizes. Some examples:
