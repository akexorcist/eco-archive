    Job change

    Requirement: MAG 30 or higher and Job Level 30 or higher as a Shaman.

    1. Go to the Guild Palace Lobby 1F in Uptown.
    2. Go inside and take the elevator to the Guild Palace Lobby 3F.
    3. Enter the room on the east side of the 3rd floor. This is the Spell User Guild.

    4. Talk to the Spell User Master and choose Elementalist. The master sends you to visit the four elemental fairies.

      Spell User Master

    5. Travel to Staff Desert and talk to the Fire Fairy.

      Fire Fairy

    6. Travel to Snov Snowfield and talk to the Water Fairy.

      Water Fairy

    7. Travel to Battleship Island and talk to the Wind Fairy.

      Wind Fairy

    8. Travel to East Cape and talk to the Earth Fairy.

      Earth Fairy
      • You can visit the four fairies in any order.

    9. Return to the Spell User Master in the Spell User Guild (Guild Palace Lobby 3F, east room) and answer his three questions. Answers: Water → Fire → Wind.

      • The questions ask for the strongest element in East Cape (Water), Snov Snowfield (Fire) and Cape Southerin (Wind).
      • A wrong answer fails the test. Visit all four fairies again before you retry.
    10. When asked, take off all your equipment. The master gives you the Elementalist mark, and you are now an Elementalist.
