    Merchant

    Base job · Backpacker

    A trading job that carries more weight, gets better shop prices and can open a shop without a golem.

    Job change

    Requirement: Novice. There is no level requirement.

    1. Enter Uptown. You need an Acropolis City Pass.
    2. Go to the Guild Palace Lobby 1F in Uptown.
    3. Go inside and take the elevator to the Guild Palace Lobby 4F.
    4. Enter the room on the north side of the 4th floor. This is the Merchant Guild.

    5. Talk to the Merchant Master and choose to become a Merchant.

      Merchant Master

    6. Accept his request. He gives you a Letter(Quest) for the Forgetful Old Man in Downtown.

    7. Talk to the Forgetful Old Man in Downtown and give him the letter. He forgot his wallet.

      Forgetful Old Man

    8. Fetch the old man's things one at a time. Bring each one back to him before you fetch the next.

      • Grandfather Wallet from Old Man's Child in Uptown
      Old Man's Child
      • Grandfather Handkerchief from Old Man's Grandson in Downtown
      Old Man's Grandson
      • Grandfather Cane from Old Man's Friend in Uptown
      Old Man's Friend
      • Grandfather Glasses from Old Man's Subordinate in Downtown
      Old Man's Subordinate
      • Grandfather Pants from Old Man's Daughter-in-Law in Uptown
      Old Man's Daughter-in-Law

    9. After the pants, the old man only mumbles. Talk to the Crybaby in Downtown. He gives you the Grandfather Denture.

      Crybaby

    10. Give the dentures to the Forgetful Old Man. He gives you the Merchant Master Stamp.

    11. Return to the Merchant Master in the Merchant Guild (Guild Palace Lobby 4F, north room), hand over the stamp and choose to become a Merchant.
    12. When asked, take off all your equipment.
    13. Talk to the master again. You are now a Merchant and receive a Left-Hand Tote Bag. You also learn Packing.
