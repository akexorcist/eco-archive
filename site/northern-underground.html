    Northern Underground

    An underground of three areas, reached through the Northern Palace Secret Passage.

    On this page
    • How to enter
    • Monsters
    • Related

    How to enter

    The way in is through the Northern Palace Secret Passage, and it starts with two NPCs, Doro and Kosso, on Northern Promenade.

    1. Talk to Doro and Kosso in the Northern Cafe on Northern Promenade about the hidden treasure.

      Doro

    2. They warp you to the door in the west wall of Northern Promenade. Talk to Doro or Kosso there, and they warp you into the Northern Palace Secret Passage.

      Doro

    3. In the Northern Palace Secret Passage, walk to the south end and enter the portal next to Kosso.

      Kosso

    4. You arrive in the Northern Underground, at the bottom edge of the Northern Underground (Civil Area).
