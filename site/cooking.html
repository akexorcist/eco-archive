    Cooking

    Turning food ingredients into dishes.

    On this page
    • How it works
    • Cook with an NPC
    • Notable recipes
    • Related

    How it works

    • Cook is a Farmer skill, learned at Job Level 12, with 5 levels.
    • Each skill level opens the recipes of that level. The Crafting page lists every Cook recipe.
    • Most recipes succeed 99% of the time. A failed try gives a Failure Compound.
    • Cooked food is lighter than its ingredients. An Apple weighs 10, and a Dried Apple made from it weighs 2 and heals more.
    • Many base ingredients come from Refining: Sugar, Salt, Butter, Giant Barley Flour, Mineral Water and Spice.

    Cook with an NPC

    Any character can bring the ingredients to one of these NPCs and order a dish. They cook every recipe up to Lv 5.

    • You pay the fee once per order, however many dishes you make.
    • The fee depends on the recipe level:
    Recipe level Fee
    Lv 1 0 G
    Lv 2 200 G
    Lv 3 300 G
    Lv 4 400 G
    Lv 5 500 G
