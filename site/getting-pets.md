# How to Get Pets

Source page: [getting-pets.html](getting-pets.html)

The ways to get a pet, a partner that follows you and fights.

Every pet and where it comes from: [Partners](partners.md). How pets grow: [Pet Raising](pet-raising.md).

## Buy a pet

**Mama** in the [Commoner's House](maps.html#map:10018000), [East Cape](maps.html#map:10018000) runs a pet shop.

**Mama**

| Pet | Price |
|---|---|
| **Puppy** | 55,000 G |
| **Black Tan** | 78,000 G |
| **Cockko Mom** | 36,000 G |

- She opens her shop step by step as players bring her [Kitin](materials.html#item:10007500). The count is shared by the whole server.
- **Puppy** goes on sale at 100,000 × [Kitin](materials.html#item:10007500), **Black Tan** at 500,000 and **Cockko Mom** at 1,000,000.

Other merchants sell riding partners and machines:

| Partner | Price | Sold by |

| **Off Road Buggy** | 60,000 G | [Resistance Sentinel](maps.html#map:12021000) in [West Fort City](maps.html#map:12021000) |
| **Bike (Blue)** | 30,000 G | [Resistance Sentinel](maps.html#map:12021000) in [West Fort City](maps.html#map:12021000) |
| **Bike (Green)** | 30,000 G | [Resistance Sentinel](maps.html#map:12021000) in [West Fort City](maps.html#map:12021000) |

- [2 Legged Walking Robot](crafting.html#recipe:351) needs the [Pilot Mastery](job-engineer.md#skill:132) skill to drive.

## Dragon eggs

A dragon egg hatches into a dragon you can ride.

| Egg | Hatches into | Dropped by |
|---|---|---|
| **Fire Dragon Egg** | **Fire Dragon** | [Pefang](monsters.html#monster:10440000) [Fatty Pepen](monsters.html#monster:10440002) [Proon](monsters.html#monster:10440200) [Cool Pepen](monsters.html#monster:10440300) |
| **Blue Dragon Egg** | **Blue Dragon** | [Cyclops](monsters.html#monster:10340000) [Ogre](monsters.html#monster:10340300) |

1. Get an egg from a monster, or trade 3 × **Tiny Medal** for one with [Resting Tiny](maps.html#map:10071000) in [Tiny Island](maps.html#map:10071000). Choose **red** for a **Fire Dragon Egg** or **blue** for a **Blue Dragon Egg**.
   **Resting Tiny**
2. Bring the egg to [Senior Merchant](maps.html#map:10035000) in [Battleship Island](maps.html#map:10035000) and ask him to hatch it. He only does this after you finish his quest.
   **Senior Merchant**

## Archer pets

The first time an Archer-line character talks to [Lady Pamela](maps.html#map:10034000) in [Acronia Eastern Beach](maps.html#map:10034000) after meeting her, she offers one pet. Choose one:

**Lady Pamela**

- **Roque Bird Egg**, which becomes a **Roque Bird**.
- **White Baby Wolf**, which becomes a **White Wolf**.

## Quest pets

- **Crawlers:** finish the crawler quest on [Momo Plains](maps.html#map:10056000), then talk to **Pet Hermit** in the [Pet House](maps.html#map:10056000), [Momo Plains](maps.html#map:10056000). Merchant-line characters get a Crawler Carrier, such as the **Crawler Carrier (Green)**. Everyone else gets a Mini Carrier, such as the **Mini Carrier (Red)**. The colour is random.
   **Pet Hermit**
- **Ride Crawler:** see the [Ride Crawler Quest](quest-ride-crawler.md).
- **Gatling Cannon:** these [Quest Counter](quest-counter.md) quests reward a **Gatling Cannon**:
   - Ghost Shelter Guardian (Hunt)
   - Iron South Street Guardian (Hunt)
   - Iron Volcano Guardian (Hunt)
   - Collect 10 Flame Iron! (Collect)
   - Collect 10 Ice Iron! (Collect)
   - Collect 10 Ghost Iron! (Collect)
   - Collect 10 Spiegel Iron! (Collect)
