    Woodcraft

    Woodcraft turns timber and branches into rods, tools, fishing rods and furniture.

    On this page
    • How it works
    • Make with an NPC
    • Tools
    • Rods and materials
    • Fishing rods

    How it works

    • Woodcraft is a Farmer skill, learned at Job Level 15, with 3 levels.
    • Each skill level opens the recipes of that level. The Crafting page lists every Woodcraft recipe.
    • Its level also raises the quality of woodwork repair kits made with Woodwork Repair Kit Craft. See Item Repair.

    Make with an NPC

    Talk to one of these NPCs with the materials and the tool in your inventory. They make every recipe up to Lv 3.

    The fee depends on the recipe level:

    Recipe level Fee
    Lv 1 100 G
    Lv 2 5,000 G
    Lv 3 10,000 G

    Recipes made from Rainbow Branch, Big Rainbow Branch or True Rainbow Branch cost 400 G, 4,000 G and 40,000 G.
