    Job change

    Requirement: STR 30 or higher and Job Level 30 or higher as an Archer.

    1. Go to the Guild Palace Lobby 1F in Uptown.
    2. Go inside and take the elevator to the Guild Palace Lobby 2F.
    3. Enter the room on the north side of the 2nd floor. This is the Archer Guild.

    4. Talk to the Archer Master and choose to change jobs. The master sends you to Lady Pamela in Acronia Eastern Beach.

      Archer Master

    5. Travel to Acronia Eastern Beach.

    6. Talk to Lady Pamela and agree to take the test. She asks you to bring 100 × Fire Arrow.

      Lady Pamela

    7. Go to the Fire Fairy in Staff Desert. Bring 100 × Wooden Arrow and 1 × Flame Summonstone. The fairy turns them into 100 × Fire Arrow.

      • The Archer Master sells Wooden Arrow.
      • Flame Summonstone drops from Fire Crystal in Staff Desert.
      • 1 × Flame Summonstone converts up to 200 × Wooden Arrow at a time.
      Fire Fairy

    8. Return to Lady Pamela in Acronia Eastern Beach and hand over 100 × Fire Arrow. She tells you to prepare potions for the trial.

    9. Talk to Lady Pamela again and accept the Striker Trial (Party) quest.
      • The quest needs 1 quest point and has no time limit.

    10. Go to Continental Cave B5F.

    11. Defeat 1 Sandfish.

    12. Return to Lady Pamela in Acronia Eastern Beach. She gives you a Striker License(Event).
    13. Return to the Archer Master in the Archer Guild (Guild Palace Lobby 2F, north room) and choose to become a Striker.
    14. When asked, take off your armor. You are now a Striker.
