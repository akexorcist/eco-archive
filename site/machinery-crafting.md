# Machinery Crafting

Source page: [machinery-crafting.html](machinery-crafting.html)

[Machinery Assembly](job-engineer.md#skill:2039) assembles machine parts into guns, engines and marionette parts.

## How it works

- [Machinery Assembly](job-engineer.md#skill:2039) is an [Engineer (Machinery)](job-engineer.md) skill, learned at Job Level 2, with 3 levels.
- Each skill level opens the recipes of that level. The [Crafting](crafting.md) page lists every [Machinery Assembly](job-engineer.md#skill:2039) recipe.
- Every recipe succeeds. Only the [Foot-operated Sewing Machine](crafting.html#recipe:377) and the [Weaving Machine](crafting.html#recipe:378) fail 1% of the time.
- Many guns can also come out better:
   - 5%: the +1 version, such as **Automatic (Silver) +1**
   - 3%: the +2 version
   - 1%: the +3 version
- Its level also raises the quality of machinery repair kits made with [Machinery Repair Kit Craft](job-engineer.md#skill:2088). See [Item Repair](item-repair.md).

## Assemble with an NPC

Talk to one of these NPCs with the materials and the tool in your inventory. They assemble every recipe up to Lv 3.

The fee depends on the recipe level:

| Recipe level | Fee |
|---|---|
| Lv 1 | 100 G |
| Lv 2 | 5,000 G |
| Lv 3 | 10,000 G |

The dual pirate muskets, such as [Dual Pirates Musket (Gold)](equipment.html#item:60097102), cost 1,000 G.

- **Junk Shopkeeper** in the [Junk Shop](maps.html#map:10024000), [Downtown](maps.html#map:10024000).
   **Junk Shopkeeper**
- **Junk Shopkeeper** in the [Iron City Junk Shop](maps.html#map:10066000), [Lower Part of Iron City](maps.html#map:10066000).
   **Junk Shopkeeper**
- **Camillo** in [Camillo's Machinery Craftshop](maps.html#map:10062000), [Tonka Island](maps.html#map:10062000).
   **Camillo**
