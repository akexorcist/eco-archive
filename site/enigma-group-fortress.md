# Enigma Group Fortress

Source page: [enigma-group-fortress.html](enigma-group-fortress.html)

The pass that gets you into the [Enigma Group Fortress](maps.html#map:10054001).

## Get the pass

- Go to [Fareast City](maps.html#map:10057000) and enter the [Fareast Hut](maps.html#map:10057000), the house with the hidden portal.
- Talk to the **Enigma Group Communicator**. When he asks for the password, type one of these:
   | Language | Password |
   |---|---|
   | English | Friend of Enigma Group |
   | Japanese | フシギだんとおともだち |
   | Chinese | 謎語團和朋友 |
   **Enigma Group Communicator**
- Say it right and he gives you an **Enigma Group Pass**. Say it wrong and he throws you out into [Fareast City](maps.html#map:10057000).
- You can get a pass this way as often as you need.

## Enter the fortress

- Go to the [Enigma Group Fortress](maps.html#map:10054001) and talk to an [Enigma Group Member](maps.html#map:10054001) at the gate. He takes the pass and lets you in.
   **Enigma Group Member**
