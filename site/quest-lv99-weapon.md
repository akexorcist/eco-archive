# Lv 99 Weapon Quest

Source page: [quest-lv99-weapon.html](quest-lv99-weapon.html)

Turn an **Ancient Weapon** from the Whale Dungeon into a **Lv 99 weapon**.

## Before you start

- Bring **eCoin**, the casino coin of ECO Town. 100 G buys 1 eCoin, and eCoin can't be changed back into gold.
- One weapon costs **50,000 eCoin** in fees:
   - 10,000 for the **Rainbow Piece**
   - 10,000 to turn it into **Rainbow Thread**
   - 20,000 for the forging
   - 10,000 to free the weapon
- The **Dragon Scale** costs **3,000 CP**. CP comes from the Dominion war.
- Bring a **party** for the dragon. Both dragon fights count for the whole party.

## Walkthrough

### 1. Defeat the Emil Dragon

- Go to [Abyss of the Depth](maps.html#map:11027000), the deepest area of the Whale Dungeon. Talk to the **Emil Dragon**. On the first talk he only invites you to a fight.
   **Emil Dragon**
- Talk to him again and choose **Challenge**. You must be **Lv 84** or higher. This starts the quest **Strongest Dragon End (Party)**: a party hunt with a **60-minute** limit.
- Defeat the [Emil Dragon](monsters.html#monster:14520000) (Lv 113).
- Report back to the dragon. You get a **Dragon Jewel**.

### 2. Ask Hagan about the materials

- Talk to **Hagan** in [Abyss of the Depth](maps.html#map:11027000) and ask about the **Dragon Jewel**. He tells you what else he needs.
   **Hagan**

### 3. Collect the season items

Three people in [Abyss of the Depth](maps.html#map:11027000) each trade one material for a small favour. You need **two** of these items for one weapon. Which two decides the weapon group (see [Rewards](#rewards)).

- **Spring:** **Louise** wants **10** × **Seismic Throat Candy**. She gives you an **Iron Cloth**.
   **Louise**
   - **Seismic Throat Candy** drops from these monsters in [Abyss of the Depth](maps.html#map:11027000):
      - [Tiduce Butterfly](monsters.html#monster:14540000)
      - [Teriable Butterfly](monsters.html#monster:14540100)
      - [Robe Night](monsters.html#monster:14570000)
      - [Doom Night](monsters.html#monster:14570100)
      - [Baku Baku](monsters.html#monster:14580000)
      - [Devil Baku Baku](monsters.html#monster:14580100)
      - [Acid Baku Baku](monsters.html#monster:14580200)
      - [Demice](monsters.html#monster:19080900)
- **Summer:** **Shiki** wants **10** × [Super Moisture Drink](materials.html#item:10067200). She gives you a **Heavenly Pearl**.
   **Shiki**
   - [Super Moisture Drink](materials.html#item:10067200) drops from these monsters in [Abyss of the Depth](maps.html#map:11027000):
      - [Cyclone](monsters.html#monster:14530000)
      - [Death Schyte](monsters.html#monster:14530100)
      - [Dryad](monsters.html#monster:14560000)
      - [Feeler Plant](monsters.html#monster:14560300)
      - [Alcid](monsters.html#monster:14560500)
      - [Robe Night](monsters.html#monster:14570000)
      - [Doom Night](monsters.html#monster:14570100)
- **Winter:** **Cole** asks you to fetch a parcel. Talk to [Erick](maps.html#map:10058000) in [Sky Tower Island](maps.html#map:10058000) to get the **Dominion String (Quest)**, then bring it back to **Cole**. He gives you an **Illusion Flame**.
   **Cole**
   **Erick**
- Each season quest has no time limit.

### 4. Get the Rainbow Thread

- Go to the [Attraction Zone](maps.html#map:11027000) in ECO Town and talk to **Fuuka**. Pay **10,000 eCoin** for a **Rainbow Piece**.
   **Fuuka**
- Return to [Abyss of the Depth](maps.html#map:11027000) and talk to **Loewen**. Give him the **Rainbow Piece** and **10,000 eCoin**. He makes a **Rainbow Thread**.
   **Loewen**

### 5. Buy the Dragon Scale

- Go to [Silk Gate Passage](maps.html#map:12021000) in [West Fort City](maps.html#map:12021000) and talk to **Supplier Doga**. Buy a **Dragon Scale** from his bargain goods for **3,000 CP**.
   **Supplier Doga**

### 6. Have Hagan forge the weapon

- Bring everything to **Hagan** in [Abyss of the Depth](maps.html#map:11027000):
   - **Dragon Jewel**
   - **Rainbow Thread**
   - **Dragon Scale**
   - two season items
   - **20,000 eCoin**
- Choose **the group** you want. He forges one Ancient Weapon from that group at random.
   - If you don't have the season items for that group, nothing happens and you keep everything.

### 7. Free the weapon

- Talk to the **Emil Dragon** while you carry the Ancient Weapon. Pay **10,000 eCoin**, and he turns it into its Lv 99 weapon.

### Swords and spears

Three Ancient Weapons don't come from **Hagan**. **Supplier Doga** in [Silk Gate Passage](maps.html#map:12021000) sells them for **15,000 CP** each. To free one, take it to the **Emil Dragon** and defeat his true form in a second fight.

- **Ancient Two Handed Sword** →
- [Apocalypse Sword: Black Wolf](equipment.html#item:60021500)

- **Ancient Spear** →
- [Holy Dragon Spear: Titania](equipment.html#item:60061700)

- **Ancient Two Handed Spear** →
- [Dark Dragon Spear: Dominion](equipment.html#item:60061800)

- The second fight is the quest **Dragon at Day's End (Party)**: a party hunt of [Emil Dragon](monsters.html#monster:14520001) (Lv 250) with a **120-minute** limit.
- After you win, the dragon frees the weapon without an eCoin fee.

## Good to know

- **You need a new set of materials for every weapon.** **Hagan** takes the **Dragon Jewel**, **Rainbow Thread**, **Dragon Scale** and both season items each time.
- **The spring and summer items can be traded.** The season quests can be repeated for more.
