    Item Repair

    Equipment loses durability as you use it, and a repair kit brings it back.

    On this page
    • How repair works
    • Which kit an item needs
    • Making repair kits
    • Where to buy the tools
    • Related

    How repair works

    • Use a repair kit on the item. The item's info shows which kit it needs.
    • No NPC repairs items. Players craft the kits from a tool and a material.
    • Each repair lowers the item's Max Durability by 1. More rules are on Equipment Upgrades.
    • An item with Max Durability 1 can't be repaired again.
    • You can't use a kit on an item at full durability.
    • A kit restores a random amount. The range depends on the skill level of the player who made it:
    Kit made at skill Lv Durability restored
    1 1–8
    2 2–11
    3 3–14
    4 4–17
    5 5–20

    Which kit an item needs

    Each item names one kit. The material matches the item, and the grade matches its level:

    • Copper for most items below Lv 30.
    • Silver for most items from Lv 30 to 59.
    • Gold for most items from Lv 60.
    Kit Used on
    Metal Swords, axes, hammers, spears, knives, shields and metal armor
    Garment Clothes, dresses, lower armor, hats and socks
    Woodwork Staffs, books, bows, instruments and wooden shoes
    Leather Boots, shoes, backpacks and leather armor
    Machinery Guns, dual guns and rifles
    Accessory Head, face, neck and finger accessories
    DEM Part DEM parts

    Making repair kits

    Every kit needs one tool and some materials. Each recipe succeeds 100% of the time.

    Metal Repair Kit

    Skill: Craft Metal Repair Kit, Miner (Tatarabe) Job Lv 30.

    Kit Materials
    Copper Metal Repair Kit
    • Copper Hammer
    • 1 × Iron Fragment
    Silver Metal Repair Kit
    • Silver Hammer
    • 2 × Iron Lump
    Gold Metal Repair Kit
    • Golden Hammer
    • 2 × Iron Fragment
    • 2 × Iron Lump
    • 2 × Iron Bullion

    Garment Repair Kit

    Skill: Garment Repair Kit Craft, Farmer Job Lv 40.

    Kit Materials
    Copper Garment Repair Kit
    • Copper Needle
    • 1 × Cloth
    Silver Garment Repair Kit
    • Silver Needle
    • 2 × Noble Fabric
    Gold Garment Repair Kit
    • Gold Needle
    • 2 × Cloth
    • 2 × Noble Fabric
    • 2 × Noblest Fabric

    Woodwork Repair Kit

    Skill: Woodwork Repair Kit Craft, Farmer Job Lv 30.

    Kit Materials
    Copper Woodwork Repair Kit
    • Copper Scalper
    • 1 × Timber
    Silver Woodwork Repair Kit
    • Silver Scalper
    • 2 × Harder Timber
    Gold Woodwork Repair Kit
    • Gold Scalper
    • 2 × Wood Chip
    • 2 × Timber
    • 2 × Harder Timber

    Leather Repair Kit

    Skill: Leather Repair Kit Craft, Ranger Job Lv 35.

    Kit Materials
    Copper Leather Repair Kit
    • Copper Scissors
    • 1 × Bawoo Leather Piece
    Silver Leather Repair Kit
    • Silver Scissors
    • 2 × Leather
    Gold Leather Repair Kit
    • Gold Scissors
    • 2 × Leather
    • 2 × Processed Leather
    • 2 × High Quality Processed Leather

    Machinery Repair Kit

    Skill: Machinery Repair Kit Craft, Engineer (Machinery) Job Lv 3.

    Kit Materials
    Copper Machinery Repair Kit
    • Copper Screwdriver
    • 1 × Tin Parts
    Silver Machinery Repair Kit
    • Silver Screwdriver
    • 3 × Iron Parts
    Gold Machinery Repair Kit
    • Gold Screwdriver
    • 1 × Silver Parts
    • 1 × Iron Parts
    • 1 × Tin Parts

    Accessory Repair Kit

    Kit Materials
    Copper Accessory Repair Kit
    • Copper Pliers
    • 1 × Copper Lump
    • One of:
      • 10 × Crystal Fragment
      • 10 × Lapis Lazuli Piece
    Silver Accessory Repair Kit
    • Silver Pliers
    • 1 × Silver Lump
    • One gem of:
      • Imperial Topaz
      • Heart Ruby
      • Aquamarine
      • Emerald
    Gold Accessory Repair Kit
    • Gold Pliers
    • 1 × Gold Lump
    • One gem of:
      • Imperial Topaz

      • Emerald
