## Reinforcement

Reinforcement raises an item from **+1** up to **+10** using crystals.

Talk to the **Ironworks Receptionist** in the [Forge Lobby](maps.html#map:10066000), [Lower Part of Iron City](maps.html#map:10066000).

**Ironworks Receptionist**

- Take the item off first. Each try costs **5,000 gold**.
- **+1 to +3 always succeed.** From +4 on, a try can fail.
- If a try fails, the item is **lost**. An insurance item such as **Insurance Book: Reinforce** prevents this.
- Every crystal counts toward the same total. For example, 4 × [Life Crystal](upgrade-crystals.md#item:90000043) and then 1 × [Strength Crystal](upgrade-crystals.md#item:90000044) makes the item +5.
- Element crystals such as [Enhanced Fire Crystal](crafting.html#recipe:183) count toward the same +10, up to 6 of them.
- Backpacker jobs earn bonus EXP when they reinforce.

### What each crystal raises

| Crystal | Raises |
|---|---|
| [Strength Crystal](upgrade-crystals.md#item:90000044) | ATK on weapons DEF on armor and shields |
| [Magic Crystal](materials.html#item:90000045) | MATK on weapons MDEF on armor, shields and accessories |
| [Critical Crystal](upgrade-crystals.md#item:90000046) | Critical on weapons Avoid Critical on armor |
| [Life Crystal](upgrade-crystals.md#item:90000043) | HP on armor |

## Appearance fusion

Fusion joins two items into one. One item gives the **look**. The other gives everything else: stats, level, jobs and durability.

Talk to **Yoko** in [Yoko's House](maps.html#map:10054001), [Enigma Group Fortress](maps.html#map:10054001). Put in the look item first, then the stats item.

**Yoko**

The [Tailor](maps.html#map:12021000) in [West Fort City](maps.html#map:12021000) also fuses items.

**Tailor**

- It costs: gold = 1,000 × the stats item's level A level 55 stats item costs 55,000 gold.
- It succeeds **80%** of the time. If it fails, you keep both items and only lose the gold.
- You can fuse a fused item again, and a broken item can still be used as the look item.
- Some items can't be traded after fusion.

**Rules:**

- Both items must be the same type, for example two hats.
- The look item's level must be **lower** than the stats item's level.
- The look item must suit at least the same races, genders and jobs as the stats item.
- You can't fuse a one-handed item with a two-handed one.
- Pet items can't be fused.

## Durability and repair

Equipment loses durability as you use it. At 0 durability you can't wear it until you repair it with a repair kit. See [Item Repair](item-repair.md).

## Iris card slots

Before you can set an [Iris Card](iris-cards.md), the item needs a **card slot**.

- You can add slots to weapons (right hand, two-handed and bows), upper body clothes, overalls, coats and chest accessories.
- [Dream Hallelujah](maps.html#map:10024000) in [Downtown](maps.html#map:10024000) sells the materials.
   **Dream Hallelujah**
- Talk to [Ash Colchica](maps.html#map:10024000) in [Downtown](maps.html#map:10024000) to add a slot. It costs 1 × [Mind Crystal](crafting.html#recipe:2001), [Big Mind Crystal](crafting.html#recipe:2002) or [True Mind Crystal](crafting.html#recipe:2003), plus **1,000 G × the item's level**.
   **Ash Colchica**
- Up to **10 slots** on an item, and **5** on a chest accessory. Each slot you already have lowers the chance.
- If it fails, the material and the item are **lost**. A support item keeps the item.

## Dyeing

Talk to the [Dye House Lady](maps.html#map:10024000) in [Downtown](maps.html#map:10024000) to dye clothes. Other towns have one too.

**Dye House Lady**

- Only items marked **Dyeable** in their item info can be dyed.
- The colors are red, purple, blue, sky blue, green, yellow, golden, orange, black, grey, silver, white and a special color.
