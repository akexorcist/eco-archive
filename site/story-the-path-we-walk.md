## Story

Fresh off your beginner lessons on the airship, your adventure begins in [Fareast City](maps.html#map:10057000). There, you meet [Emil](maps.html#map:10057000) and [Marsha](maps.html#map:10057000), who show you what it takes to become a real adventurer.

Together, you clear out monster-infested ruins, use light against the darkness of [Undead Castle](maps.html#map:10069000), and learn elemental strengths in the Light Tower, where you defeat powerful foes like Gattengar Sigma. Along the way, you befriend Emil's close Dominion companions, [Belial](maps.html#map:10060000) and [Rlyeh](maps.html#map:10060000), and help [Titus](maps.html#map:10059000), a stranded Titania, retrieve a lost key from ruins overrun by the DEM. After braving an undersea cave to find a pearl for your return voyage, your journey brings you to the base of the tower that links the worlds, ready to ascend toward the heavens.
