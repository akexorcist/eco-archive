# Cleaning Quests

Source page: [quest-cleaning.html](quest-cleaning.html)

**Cleaning** quests, where you bring in a common monster drop.

## How it works

- Go to [Downtown](maps.html#map:10024000) and talk to the [Elegant Lady](maps.html#map:10024000). She offers the Cleaning quest for your level.
   **Elegant Lady**
- Collect **30** of the item named in the quest and bring them back.
