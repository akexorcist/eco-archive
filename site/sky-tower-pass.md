# Sky Tower Pass

Source page: [sky-tower-pass.html](sky-tower-pass.html)

The pass for the airship from [Morg City](maps.html#map:10060000) to [Sky Tower Island](maps.html#map:10058000).

## How to get it

1. Talk to [Smuggling Old Man](maps.html#map:10060000) in [Morg City](maps.html#map:10060000) and ask about **[Sky Tower Island](maps.html#map:10058000)**.
   **Smuggling Old Man**
2. Talk to **Billionaire Diermant** in the [Merchant Guild Headquarters](maps.html#map:10060000), [Morg City](maps.html#map:10060000). He gives you a **number code**. Remember it: if you forget it, you have to go back and talk to him again.
   **Billionaire Diermant**
3. Go to the [Weapon Black Marketeer](maps.html#map:10060000) in [Morg City](maps.html#map:10060000): walk down all the stairs to the mining ground. Talk to the **Firearms Store**:
   **Firearms Store**
   - Tell him the number code.
   - Pay **500 G**.
   - Answer the daily question:
      | Day | Question | Answer |
      |---|---|---|
      | Sunday | **Violeta**'s job | **Assassin** |
      | Monday | [Binet](maps.html#map:10060000)'s hairstyle | **Twin Tail** |
      | Tuesday | What [Auto Craftsman](maps.html#map:10060000) holds in his left hand | **Shovel** |
      | Wednesday | The color of the [Titania Researcher](maps.html#map:20163000)'s ribbon | **Black** |
      | Thursday | The color of the [Suspicious Man](maps.html#map:20146000)'s tie | **Yellow** |
      | Friday | The color of **Highly Esteemed Rose**'s tights | **White** |
      | Saturday | **Newsman Jiggi**'s job | **Newsman** |
   - He tells you the pass is in the **Pot** in the [Miner's Room](maps.html#map:10060000).
4. Go to the [Miner's Room](maps.html#map:10060000) in [Morg City](maps.html#map:10060000). Choose **Look** at the Pot to get the **Sky Tower Pass**.
   [Morg City](maps.html#map:10060000)

## How to use it

- Show the pass at the gate next to [Smuggling Old Man](maps.html#map:10060000) to board the airship to [Sky Tower Island](maps.html#map:10058000). See [Airships & Rides](airships-and-rides.md#sky-tower-island).
   **Smuggling Old Man**
