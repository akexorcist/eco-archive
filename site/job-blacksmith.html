    Expert job · Backpacker

    Job change

    Requirement: Job Level 30 or higher as a Miner (Tatarabe).

    1. Go to the Guild Palace Lobby 1F in Uptown.
    2. Go inside and take the elevator to the Guild Palace Lobby 3F.
    3. Enter the room on the west (left-hand) side of the 3rd floor. This is the Blacksmith Guild.

    4. Talk to the Miner Master and choose to change jobs. He sends you to the Salamander at Iron Volcano.

      Miner Master

    5. Get 1 × Salamander Marionette. It drops from Poisonous Lizard, which live on Mothgue Cliff and Island of the Undead.

    6. Travel to Iron Volcano.

    7. Talk to the Salamander and choose to listen. It takes the Salamander Marionette and gives you a Salamander Heart(Event).

      Salamander
      • If you are transformed into a marionette, the Salamander asks you to end it first.
      • The Salamander asks you to give it a name. Any name works.

    8. Use the Salamander Heart(Event).

    9. Return to the Miner Master in the Blacksmith Guild (Guild Palace Lobby 3F, west room) and choose to change jobs again.
    10. Choose to become a Blacksmith. When asked, take off all your equipment. The master gives you the Blacksmith mark, and you are now a Blacksmith.
