    Tiny Island

    Tiny Island, home of the Tinies, the bear-like spirits of ECO.

    On this page
    • Getting there
    • Tiny Medals
    • Pirate Tiny's trades
    • Dragon Eggs
    • Related

    Getting there

    • Go to Uptown and talk to the Tiny in the middle of the town. The first time, he only greets you. Talk to him again and choose to go: he sends you to Tiny Island.

      Tiny

    Tiny Medals

    • Tiny Medal drops from bosses, and can be found in Wooden Box 10 and 11 and in the red treasure boxes from 9 up.
    • Wooden Box 10 and 11 come from instance dungeon quests.

    Pirate Tiny's trades

    • Talk to the Pirate Tiny on the west side of the island.

      Pirate Tiny
    You give You get
    1 × Tiny Medal Tiny Rod
    1 × Tiny Medal Tiny Blue Elegant Table
    1 × Tiny Medal Tiny Picnic Chair
    1 × Tiny Medal Calendar(Tiny)
    3 × Tiny Medal Tiny Navy Blue Suitcase
    3 × Tiny Medal Tiny Light Pink Suitcase
    10 × Tiny Medal Tiny Rainbow Medal
    1 × Tiny Rainbow Medal Purple Waitress Uniform(F)
    3 × Tiny Rainbow Medal Left-Hand Tiny Navy Blue Suitcase
    3 × Tiny Rainbow Medal Left-Hand Tiny Baby Pink Suitcase

    Dragon Eggs

    • The Resting Tiny on the south side trades 3 × Tiny Medal for a Fire Dragon Egg or a Blue Dragon Egg. You need to carry a Tiny Medal before he will talk to you.

      Resting Tiny

    • Hatch the egg on Battleship Island. See Dragon Mount.
