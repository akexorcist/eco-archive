# Marionettes & Golems

Source page: [marionettes.html](marionettes.html)

## Making golems

A golem is made with [Marionette Construction](job-marionettist.md#skill:2038), a [Marionettist](job-marionettist.md) skill learned at Job Level 2, from:

- 1 × [Golem Goblet](materials.html#item:10002100)
- 1 × its marionette

[Marionette Construction](job-marionettist.md#skill:2038) also makes the parts of the [Electell Marionette](materials.html#item:10030001):

- [Motor](materials.html#item:10030100)
- [Electell Model](materials.html#item:10030002)
- [Pocket Computer](materials.html#item:10030200)
- [Galvanized Iron Body](materials.html#item:10030003)

Every recipe is on the [Crafting](crafting.md) page.

### Make with an NPC

These NPCs make golems for 1,000 G.

- **Marionettist Master** in the [Marionettist Guild](maps.html#map:10023000), [Uptown](maps.html#map:10023000). Also sells the [Golem Goblet](materials.html#item:10002100).
   **Marionettist Master**
- **Rita** in the [Rita's Marionette Craftshop](maps.html#map:10062000), [Tonka Island](maps.html#map:10062000).
   **Rita**

## Free marionette

[Elina](maps.html#map:10023000) in [Uptown](maps.html#map:10023000) gives you 1 free marionette.

- You must be **Lv 8** or higher.
- Once per account.
- You choose one of:
   - [Salamander Marionette](materials.html#item:10013850)
   - [Insmouse Marionette](materials.html#item:10019400)
   - [Mandragora Marionette](materials.html#item:10019200)
   - [Electell Marionette](materials.html#item:10030001)

**Elina**
