    Novice

    Starting job

    The job every new character starts with, with few equipment limits and no special benefits.

    Next job:

    • Any base job: Swordsman, Fencer, Scout, Archer, Wizard, Shaman, Vates, Warlock, Miner (Tatarabe), Farmer, Ranger or Merchant.
    • Joker: only if you never change jobs, at Lv 100 and Job Level 50. A Novice who changes to a base job can't become a Joker.
    On this page
    • Job change

    Job change

    Requirement: each base job has its own requirement, listed below.

    1. Enter Uptown. You need an Acropolis City Pass. Every master below is in Uptown.

    2. Choose a job and check that you meet its requirement.

      Job Series Requirement Master's place in Uptown
      Swordsman Fighter STR 10 Swordsman Guild (Guild Palace Lobby 2F, east room)
      Fencer Fighter STR 10 Fencer Guild (Guild Palace Lobby 2F, west room)
      Scout Fighter STR 10 Scout Guild (Guild Palace Lobby 2F, south room)
      Archer Fighter INT 10 Archer Guild (Guild Palace Lobby 2F, north room)
      Wizard Spell User MAG 10 Spell User Guild (Guild Palace Lobby 3F, east room)
      Shaman Spell User MAG 10 Spell User Guild (Guild Palace Lobby 3F, east room)
      Vates Spell User MAG 10 White Church
      Warlock Spell User MAG 10 Black Church
      Miner (Tatarabe) Backpacker - Blacksmith Guild (Guild Palace Lobby 3F, west room)
      Farmer Backpacker Job Level 10 Farmer Guild (Guild Palace Lobby 3F, north room)
      Ranger Backpacker - Ranger Guild (Guild Palace Lobby 4F, south room)
      Merchant Backpacker - Merchant Guild (Guild Palace Lobby 4F, north room)

    1. Go to the job master's place and talk to the master. Choose the job and follow the steps on its page.

    A Novice who never changes jobs can become a Joker at Lv 100 and Job Level 50.
