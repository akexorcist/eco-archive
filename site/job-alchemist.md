# Alchemist

Source page: [job-alchemist.html](job-alchemist.html)

Expert job · Backpacker

A job that makes potions, supports plant partners and attacks groups with chemicals and bombs.

1. [Farmer Base](job-farmer.md)
2. Alchemist Expert
3. [⇄](job-switching.md)
4. [Marionettist Technical](job-marionettist.md)
5. [Harvester Chronicle](job-harvester.md)

## Job change

**Requirement:** Job Level 30 or higher as a Farmer.

1. Go to the [Guild Palace Lobby 1F](maps.html#map:10023000) in [Uptown](maps.html#map:10023000).
2. Go inside and take the **elevator** to the [Guild Palace Lobby 3F](maps.html#map:10023000).
3. Enter the room on the **north side** of the 3rd floor. This is the [Farmer Guild](maps.html#map:10023000).
4. Talk to the **Farmer Master** and choose to change jobs. He sends you to the **Alchemist Master**.
   **Farmer Master**
5. Take the **elevator** to the [Guild Palace Lobby 4F](maps.html#map:10023000). Enter the room on the **east side**. This is the [Alchemist Guild](maps.html#map:10023000).
6. Talk to the **Alchemist Master** and choose **Take me as your apprentice** (the 3rd choice). Then choose to **take the test**.
   **Alchemist Master**
7. Talk to him again with the same choice to start the **Chemical Synthesis Skill Test**. He asks **10** questions. You answer each one by bringing him the item it asks for. There is no time limit.
   - If you don't have the right item, he repeats the question.
8. Questions 1 to 5 are always the same. Bring **1** of each item:
   - [Heal Potion](materials.html#item:10000103): [Marika Herb](materials.html#item:10004901) and [Abori Herb](materials.html#item:10004903), from [Herb](monsters.html#monster:30150000)
   - [Magic Potion](materials.html#item:10000102): [Ril Herb](materials.html#item:10004906) and [Regna Herb](materials.html#item:10004907), from [Herb](monsters.html#monster:30150001)
   - [Stamina Potion](materials.html#item:10000108): [Gikogiko Herb](materials.html#item:10004905) and [Rune Herb](materials.html#item:10004902), from [Herb](monsters.html#monster:30150002) and [Herb](monsters.html#monster:30150003)
   - [Multi Condition Drink](materials.html#item:10000106): [Miti Herb](materials.html#item:10004904), from [Herb](monsters.html#monster:30150003)
   - [Resurrection Potion](materials.html#item:10000604): [Tamtam Herb](materials.html#item:10004908), from [Herb](monsters.html#monster:30150002)
   - All four kinds of Herb plants grow on [Momo Plains](maps.html#map:10056000) and [Breadbasket](maps.html#map:10068000).
9. Questions 6 to 10 come from one of **three sets**, chosen at random. Bring **1** of each item for your set:
   - **Set 1**
      - [Leather Conditioner](materials.html#item:10038007): [Ether](materials.html#item:10000304), sold by the [North Vending Shopkeeper](maps.html#map:10024000) in [Downtown](maps.html#map:10024000)
      - [Sleeping Potion](crafting.html#recipe:543): [Wild Rosebush Thorn](materials.html#item:10025205), from elemental flowers such as [Fire Flower](monsters.html#monster:30210100) and [Earth Flower](monsters.html#monster:30210800)
      - Hair Color: [Carrier Oil](materials.html#item:10001907), from [Crash Ice](monsters.html#monster:10070300) on [Cape Northerin](maps.html#map:10001000) and [Snov Fork](maps.html#map:10004000)
      - [Light Perfume](crafting.html#recipe:512): [White Petal](materials.html#item:10043204), from [Light Flower](monsters.html#monster:30211000)
      - [Molten Gold](materials.html#item:10000207): [Poison Potion](materials.html#item:10000302), made from [Spider Wine](materials.html#item:10045500) and [Distilled Water](materials.html#item:10000204)
   - **Set 2**
      - [Paint Remover](crafting.html#recipe:552): [Colorless Powder](materials.html#item:10001112), made from **4** × [Jerico](materials.html#item:10032800) and **1** × [Dry Powder](materials.html#item:10001110)
      - [Mute Potion](materials.html#item:10000303): [Snow Drop](materials.html#item:10009150), from [Pefang](monsters.html#monster:10440000) and [Proon](monsters.html#monster:10440200)
      - [Jerico Potion](materials.html#item:10000104): [Jerico](materials.html#item:10032800), from [Pururu](monsters.html#monster:10000000) and other Pururus
      - [Water Perfume](crafting.html#recipe:509): [Blue Petal](materials.html#item:10043203), from [Ice Flower](monsters.html#monster:30210300)
      - [Poison Potion](materials.html#item:10000302): [Spider Wine](materials.html#item:10045500), from [Earth Spider](monsters.html#monster:10210000) on [Lake Utena](maps.html#map:10032000) and [Acronia Eastern Beach](maps.html#map:10034000)
   - **Set 3**
      - [Abradant](materials.html#item:10045800): [Pebble](materials.html#item:10014600), from [Rock](monsters.html#monster:30000000)
      - [Stone Curse Potion](materials.html#item:10000310): [Cockatrice Eye](materials.html#item:10012550), from [Cockatrice](monsters.html#monster:10010100) on [Acronia Eastern Beach](maps.html#map:10034000)
      - [Hair Loss Treatment](crafting.html#recipe:513): [Cuticle Extract](materials.html#item:10001905), from [Fallen Tree](monsters.html#monster:30060000) and [Paper Tree](monsters.html#monster:30040001)
      - [Flame Perfume](crafting.html#recipe:508): [Red Petal](materials.html#item:10043201), from [Fire Flower](monsters.html#monster:30210100) in [Staff Desert](maps.html#map:10042000)
      - [Glue](materials.html#item:10024300): [Snakehead Fish Sweat](materials.html#item:10008600), from [Snakehead Fish](monsters.html#monster:26100000) in [Continental Cave B5F](maps.html#map:20004000)
10. After the last right answer, the master offers the job change. Choose to **become an Alchemist**.
11. **Take off all your equipment** first. The master gives you the Alchemist mark, and you are now an Alchemist.
