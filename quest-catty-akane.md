# Catty (Akane) Quest

Source page: [quest-catty-akane.html](quest-catty-akane.html)

Help [Rita's Marionette Craftshop](maps.html#map:10062000) find a computer part for an airship.

## Before you start

- Finish the [Catty (Momo) Quest](quest-catty-momo.md) and **equip a Catty** for the whole quest.
- Part of the quest takes place in the **[Light Tower](light-tower.md)**, among [Onyx](monsters.html#monster:26090900) and [Incubus](monsters.html#monster:10410200).

## Walkthrough

### 1. Rita's craftshop

- Go to [Tonka Island](maps.html#map:10062000) and enter the [Rita's Marionette Craftshop](maps.html#map:10062000) in the middle of the town. Talk to **Rita** and her [Hallely](maps.html#map:10062000) helper.
   **Rita**

### 2. Ask around

- Talk to [Meister Will](maps.html#map:10062000) in the **northwest** of [Tonka Island](maps.html#map:10062000).
   **Meister Will**
- Go to [Fareast Main Street](maps.html#map:10017000) and talk to the [Airship Engineer](maps.html#map:10017000).
   **Airship Engineer**
- Go back to [Tonka Island](maps.html#map:10062000) and talk to the [Hallely](maps.html#map:10062000) helper again. You board his airship. He asks you to find **Marcia**.

### 3. Find Marcia

- Go to [Downtown](maps.html#map:10024000) and talk to the [Elegant Lady](maps.html#map:10024000).
   **Elegant Lady**
- Go to the [South Faction Command Center Entrance](maps.html#map:10023300) on the [Acropolis South Bridge](maps.html#map:10023300) and talk to **Marcia**. You board her airship.
- On her airship, talk to **Emil**, then to **Marcia** again. They take you to the **[Light Tower](light-tower.md)**.

### 4. The Light Tower

- Go down the stairs, through the room, and out to the open area of the **4th floor**, where [Onyx](monsters.html#monster:26090900) and [Incubus](monsters.html#monster:10410200) live.
- Destroy the [Machine Cluster](monsters.html#monster:30330003) that appears in the **northwest** and pick up the **Computer ROM(Event)**.
- If you die here, come back to the [Light Tower](light-tower.md) yourself, without anyone possessing you, and look for the Computer ROM again.

### 5. Back to Rita

- Go back to the [Rita's Marionette Craftshop](maps.html#map:10062000) and give the **Computer ROM(Event)** to the [Hallely](maps.html#map:10062000) helper.
- Talk to **Rita** and choose to board the airship. Talk to **Rita** there, then talk to her again back in the shop. You receive [Catty (Akane)](partners.html#item:10017907).

## Rewards

- [Catty (Akane)](partners.html#item:10017907): a hasty, sharp-tongued cat spirit. She can Heal, Revive herself and cast Solid Aura
