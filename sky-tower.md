# Sky Tower

Source page: [sky-tower.html](sky-tower.html)

The tower stands on [Sky Tower Island](maps.html#map:10058000). Its [Elevator Room](maps.html#map:10058000) links the three worlds: see [Between the Worlds](between-the-worlds.md).

## How to enter the boss room

1. Talk to [Erick](maps.html#map:10058000) on [Sky Tower Island](maps.html#map:10058000) and ask for a **Fallen Dragon Stone**. It costs **5,000 G**.
   **Erick**
2. Walk into the **Sky Tower Door** in the middle of the island with the stone. Choose to **raise the stone**. It breaks, and you arrive in the boss room of the [Elevator Room](maps.html#map:10058000). Each try uses one stone.
   [Sky Tower Island](maps.html#map:10058000)
