## Job change

**Requirement:** STR 30 or higher, not counting equipment, and Job Level 30 or higher as a Scout.

1. Go to the [Guild Palace Lobby 1F](maps.html#map:10023000) in [Uptown](maps.html#map:10023000).
2. Go inside and take the **elevator** to the [Guild Palace Lobby 2F](maps.html#map:10023000).
3. Enter the room on the **south side** of the 2nd floor. This is the [Scout Guild](maps.html#map:10023000).
4. Talk to the **Scout Master** and choose **Assassin**. The master asks you to collect four potions from four people around the continent. The first password hint is **"Sky"**.
   **Scout Master**
   - Visit the four people in the order below. If you choose a wrong answer, go back to the previous person to hear the hint again.
5. Travel to [Battleship Island](maps.html#map:10035000) and talk to the [Thief Guildsman](maps.html#map:10035000). Choose **"I want to fly there"** (2nd option). He gives you an **Assassin Potion1(Quest)**.
   **Thief Guildsman**
6. Travel to [Acronia Eastern Beach](maps.html#map:10034000) and talk to the [Thief Guildsman](maps.html#map:10034000). Choose **"It's a drink!"** (3rd option). He gives you an **Assassin Potion2(Quest)**.
   **Thief Guildsman**
7. Travel to [Cape Northerin](maps.html#map:10001000) and talk to the [Black Marketeer](maps.html#map:10001000). Choose **"I've been alright"** (1st option). He gives you an **Assassin Potion3(Quest)**.
   **Black Marketeer**
8. Travel to [Iron Volcano](maps.html#map:10064000) through the southeast exit of [Iron South Street](maps.html#map:10063000), and talk to [Cutesy Python](maps.html#map:10064000). Choose **"It depends on what you think about the heat!"** (3rd option). She gives you an **Assassin Potion4(Quest)**.
   **Cutesy Python**
9. Return to the **Scout Master** in the [Scout Guild](maps.html#map:10023000) ([Guild Palace Lobby 2F](maps.html#map:10023000), south room) and hand over the four potions. Choose to become an **Assassin**.
10. When asked, **take off your armor**. The master gives you the Assassin mark, and you are now an Assassin.
