# Carmine Brick

Source page: [story-saga18.html](story-saga18.html)

A bonus Main Story from SAGA 18: you follow the Enigma Group and the Tonka researchers to [Carmine Brick City](maps.html#map:10055000) and its ruins.

## Story

Your expedition starts at the [Fortress Cafe](maps.html#map:10054001) inside the [Enigma Group Fortress](maps.html#map:10054001), where you meet the **Cafe Master** before hearing [Executive Buru](maps.html#map:10055000) out at the [Enigma Group Headquarter](maps.html#map:10054001). After questioning a **Tonka Researcher** on [Carmine Coast](maps.html#map:10080000), you follow the Enigma Group into the **Carmine Desert (Day)**, gathering leads at the oasis both by night and by day from a **Tonka Researcher**, [Executive Buru](maps.html#map:10055000), and the **Cafe Master**.

Following a given route into [Carmine Brick City](maps.html#map:10055000), you gather information about a treasure from [Charles](maps.html#map:10055000) and the Tonka Survey Team, track down three researchers, and report back to [Executive Buru](maps.html#map:10055000). Finally, you join the Tonka Survey Team at the front entrance of the ruins, descend to the deepest room of the [Carmine Brick Ruins B1F](maps.html#map:20260000) to inspect it, and let those waiting outside know that everything is clear.

## Characters

[Executive Buru](maps.html#map:10055000)

First met at the [Enigma Group Headquarter](maps.html#map:10054001); you report back in [Carmine Brick City](maps.html#map:10055000).

[Charles](maps.html#map:10055000)

Found at the **Tonka Researcher Headquarters Tent**; goes with you into the [Carmine Brick Ruins B1F](maps.html#map:20260000).

**Cafe Master**

First met at the [Fortress Cafe](maps.html#map:10054001); asked for information again at the oasis.

**Tonka Researcher**

Questioned on [Carmine Coast](maps.html#map:10080000) and at [Carmine Desert Oasis (Night)](maps.html#map:10090001).
