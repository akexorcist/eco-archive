    City Defense Battle

    The battle to defend West Fort City from the DEM, and the CP it pays.

    On this page
    • What it is
    • How to join
    • Rules
    • Earning CP
    • CP shops
    • Related

    What it is

    • Every player in the Dominion World can join. Everyone who joins defends the city together.
    • A win pays CP, the points you spend at the CP shops.

    How to join

    1. Go to Silk Gate Passage from West Fort City.

    2. During registration, talk to Receptionist - Badera and choose to join the battle.

      Receptionist - Badera

    3. Talk to the Silk Gate Gate Keeper and choose Ready. You enter the battlefield at once.

      Silk Gate Gate Keeper

    Rules

    • Defend the 7 Symbols in the city for 45 minutes. If all 7 are destroyed, the battle is lost.
    • The DEM come in waves. A new wave appears around every Symbol each minute. Some waves are stronger and bring a Champ DEM.
    • Champ DEM take only 1/10 damage from players who aren't Field Champions.
    • Field Champions are the top 10 players of the WRP (War Record Point) ranking. You earn WRP by winning PvP fights in the Champion Battle on the Dominion World maps.
    • Every player gets a Symbol repair skill during the battle. Backpacker jobs repair more with it.
    • Use the traps that lie around the battlefield against the DEM.
    • Up to 600 players can join.

    Earning CP

    • A win pays up to 3,000 CP. Field Champions get 1.5 × as much, up to 4,500 CP.
    • A loss pays 0.75 × your share. A win always pays at least 200 CP.
    • Your share depends on:
      • How many Symbols survive
      • The DEM you hit and the damage you deal
      • The Symbols you repair, 12 CP per repair
      • Your deaths: each one lowers your CP
    • The main story in the Dominion World, The World Begins to Move, gives a one-time 50 CP.
