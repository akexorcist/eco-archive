# Eastern Dungeon

Source page: [eastern-dungeon.html](eastern-dungeon.html)

A dungeon entered from [Fareast City](maps.html#map:10057000).

## How to enter

- Enter the [Eastern Dungeon](maps.html#map:20090000) from [Fareast City](maps.html#map:10057000).
- The [Eastern Dungeon](maps.html#map:20090000) leads on to the [Poison Swamp](maps.html#map:20091000) and the [Virgin Forest](maps.html#map:20092000).
- [Hell King's Forest](maps.html#map:20092001) opens only with a **Violet Golden Jewel**, which breaks when you go in.
