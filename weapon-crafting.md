# Weapon Crafting

Source page: [weapon-crafting.html](weapon-crafting.html)

Four crafting skills make weapons, bows, staves, arrows and throwing weapons.

## How it works

- Each skill level opens the recipes of that level. The [Crafting](crafting.md) page lists every recipe.
- Finished weapons succeed 100% of the time. Many come out better some of the time:
   - 5%: the +1 version, such as **Club +1**
   - 3%: the +2 version
   - 1%: the +3 version

## Craft with an NPC

Talk to one of these NPCs with the materials and the tool in your inventory. Each makes every recipe of the four skills, and also [Armor Construction](job-blacksmith.md#skill:2017) and [Advanced Metal Refining](job-miner.md#skill:2051) recipes.
