# How to Get Pets

Source page: [getting-pets.html](getting-pets.html)

The ways to get a pet, a partner that follows you and fights.

Every pet and where it comes from: [Partners](partners.md). How pets grow: [Pet Raising](pet-raising.md).

## Buy a pet

**Mama** in the [Commoner's House](maps.html#map:10018000), [East Cape](maps.html#map:10018000) runs a pet shop.

**Mama**

| Pet | Price |
|---|---|
| [Puppy](partners.html#item:10048900) | 55,000 G |
| [Black Tan](partners.html#item:10048950) | 78,000 G |
| [Cockko Mom](partners.html#item:10055500) | 36,000 G |

- She opens her shop step by step as players bring her [Kitin](materials.html#item:10007500). The count is shared by the whole server.
- [Puppy](partners.html#item:10048900) goes on sale at 100,000 × [Kitin](materials.html#item:10007500), [Black Tan](partners.html#item:10048950) at 500,000 and [Cockko Mom](partners.html#item:10055500) at 1,000,000.

## Dragon eggs

A dragon egg hatches into a dragon you can ride.

| Egg | Hatches into | Dropped by |
|---|---|---|
| **Fire Dragon Egg** | [Fire Dragon](partners.html#item:10052301) | [Pefang](monsters.html#monster:10440000) [Fatty Pepen](monsters.html#monster:10440002) [Proon](monsters.html#monster:10440200) [Cool Pepen](monsters.html#monster:10440300) |
| **Blue Dragon Egg** | [Blue Dragon](partners.html#item:10052303) | [Cyclops](monsters.html#monster:10340000) [Ogre](monsters.html#monster:10340300) |

1. Get an egg from a monster, or trade 3 × **Tiny Medal** for one with [Resting Tiny](maps.html#map:10071000) in [Tiny Island](maps.html#map:10071000). Choose **red** for a **Fire Dragon Egg** or **blue** for a **Blue Dragon Egg**.
   **Resting Tiny**
2. Bring the egg to [Senior Merchant](maps.html#map:10035000) in [Battleship Island](maps.html#map:10035000) and ask him to hatch it. He only does this after you finish his quest.
   **Senior Merchant**

## Archer pets

The first time an Archer-line character talks to [Lady Pamela](maps.html#map:10034000) in [Acronia Eastern Beach](maps.html#map:10034000) after meeting her, she offers one pet. Choose one:

**Lady Pamela**

- **Roque Bird Egg**, which becomes a [Roque Bird](partners.html#item:10018310).
- **White Baby Wolf**, which becomes a [White Wolf](partners.html#item:10035602).

## Quest pets

- **Crawlers:** finish the crawler quest on [Momo Plains](maps.html#map:10056000), then talk to **Pet Hermit** in the [Pet House](maps.html#map:10056000), [Momo Plains](maps.html#map:10056000). Merchant-line characters get a Crawler Carrier, such as the [Crawler Carrier (Green)](partners.html#item:10007581). Everyone else gets a Mini Carrier, such as the [Mini Carrier (Red)](partners.html#item:10007590). The colour is random.
   **Pet Hermit**
- **Ride Crawler:** see the [Ride Crawler Quest](quest-ride-crawler.md).
- **Gatling Cannon:** these [Quest Counter](quest-counter.md) quests reward a [Gatling Cannon](partners.html#item:10018900):
   - Ghost Shelter Guardian (Hunt)
   - Iron South Street Guardian (Hunt)
   - Iron Volcano Guardian (Hunt)
   - Collect 10 Flame Iron! (Collect)
   - Collect 10 Ice Iron! (Collect)
   - Collect 10 Ghost Iron! (Collect)
   - Collect 10 Spiegel Iron! (Collect)
