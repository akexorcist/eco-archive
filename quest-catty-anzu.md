# Catty (Anzu) Quest

Source page: [quest-catty-anzu.html](quest-catty-anzu.html)

A strange pot turns you into a cat spirit, and you must get your body back.

## Before you start

- Finish the [Catty (Momo) Quest](quest-catty-momo.md) and **equip a Catty**.
- Carry **only the Catty you wear.** Later you collect a heart for every Catty you carry.
- Get a **Piece of Mind**. It's a somewhat rare drop from [Angel Feather](monsters.html#monster:10270000). You can't use it on a Flying Garden.
- Free up **200 capacity and weight**. You receive two quest items of 100 each.

## Walkthrough

### 1. The pot on Tonka Island

- Go to [Uptown](maps.html#map:10023000) and talk to the **Seamstress**. Choose the **second answer twice**.
   **Seamstress**
- Go to [Tonka Island](maps.html#map:10062000) and talk to [Miranda the Flower Lover](maps.html#map:10062000) in the south of the island, then enter her house.
   **Miranda the Flower Lover**
- Talk to the **pot** inside and choose to take it. You turn into Catty (Anzu). Your stats and equipment stay the same.

### 2. To Titania

- Go back to the **Seamstress** in [Uptown](maps.html#map:10023000). She gives you an apricot-coloured triangle bandage.
- Go to the **5th floor** of the Guild Palace and talk to the **Titanian Committee President**.
   **Titanian Committee President**
- Use the **Piece of Mind**. Talk to **Titus** and **Tita**.
- Talk to **Titus** again to leave. He gives you a **Titus Crystal(Event)**.
- Talk to **Emil**, then go inside the house and talk to **Marcia**. Talk to **Emil** again: he sends you to [North Mountain](maps.html#map:10050000).

### 3. Icy Island

- Go to [Eternal North Boundary](maps.html#map:10051000) and take the northwest exit toward [Icy Island](maps.html#map:10051100). Use the **Titus Crystal(Event)** to pass the invisible wall.
- Talk to the [Icy Chief](maps.html#map:10051100). He gives you an **Icy Prism(Event)**.
   **Icy Chief**

### 4. Collect the Catty hearts

- Collect a heart for each Catty you carry. Carry that Catty and talk to:
   | Catty you carry | Talk to |
   |---|---|
   | [Catty (Momo)](partners.html#item:10017900) | Any **Trash Can** |
   | [Catty (Ai)](partners.html#item:10017903) | The [Broken Electell](maps.html#map:10030000) in [Fruit Forest](maps.html#map:10030000) |
   | [Catty (Yamabuki)](partners.html#item:10017905) | The [Senior Merchant](maps.html#map:10035000) on [Battleship Island](maps.html#map:10035000) |
   | [Catty (Sumire)](partners.html#item:10017906) | [Ghost Buster Gadoo](maps.html#map:10061000) at [Ghost Shelter](maps.html#map:10061000) |
   | [Catty (Akane)](partners.html#item:10017907) | **Rita** in the [Rita's Marionette Craftshop](maps.html#map:10062000) on [Tonka Island](maps.html#map:10062000) |
- If you wear [Catty (Midori)](partners.html#item:10017902), you don't need any hearts.
- **Rita** is also on [Tonka Island](maps.html#map:10062000), so collect her heart last.

### 5. Back to your body

- Go back to Miranda's house on [Tonka Island](maps.html#map:10062000). Talk to **your own body** and enter it.
- Talk to the pot again. The hearts gather around it, and you receive [Catty (Anzu)](partners.html#item:10017908).

## Rewards

- [Catty (Anzu)](partners.html#item:10017908): the first boy Catty. Skills and details on [Catties](catties.md#anzu)

## Good to know

- **Logging out keeps your progress.** You are still Catty (Anzu) and still on the quest when you log back in.
- **Check the pot before you finish.** It tells you which Catties are missing if you don't carry them all.
