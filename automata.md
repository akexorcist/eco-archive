# Automata

Source page: [automata.html](automata.html)

Automata are five partners that only DEM characters can get.

## How Automata work

- Automata fight with the act cubes listed below.
- Each Automata's full data is on the [Partners](partners.html#item:10080100) page.
- For the [Backpack Familiar: Automata (Sapphire)](partners.html#item:10080103) and [Backpack Familiar: Automata (Diamond)](partners.html#item:10080104) quests, talk to the quest NPC in your normal form while wearing an Automata.

## Amethyst

- **Item:** [Automata (Amethyst)](partners.html#item:10080100)
- **How to get:** The DEM story quest [Heart of the Machine](quest-heart-of-the-machine.md). Needs Lv 20 and 21 Fame.
- **Attack:** Ranged
- **Act cubes:**
   - Defrag
   - Optimization
   - Concentration
   - Attack Burst
   - High Guard
   - Revive

## Ruby

- **Item:** [Automata (Ruby)](partners.html#item:10080101)
- **How to get:** After [Automata (Amethyst)](partners.html#item:10080100). Needs Lv 45.
- **Attack:** Melee
- **Act cubes:**
   - Defrag
   - Optimization
   - Avoid Burst
   - Attack Burst
   - Critical
   - Rouse Body
   - Assault
   - Stun Blow
   - Charge

## Topaz

- **Item:** [Automata (Topaz)](partners.html#item:10080102)
- **How to get:** After [Automata (Ruby)](partners.html#item:10080101). Needs Lv 60.
- **Attack:** Melee
- **Act cubes:**

   - Optimization
   - Solid Aura
   - Magic Shield

## Combining Automata

Once you have all five, they can join into DEM parts.

| You get | What it is | What it takes |
|---|---|---|
| **Set Automata Night Form** | One set of DEM parts made from all five Automata | You must be Lv 99 to finish.Bring:All five Automata The five Optimization Modules, one from each Automata's quest Pay **Dr. Disciple** in [DEM Customize Room](maps.html#map:10024000):10,000,000 base EXP 10,000,000 job EXP |
| **Automata Boost** | Back part that turns the five Automata into a fast form; the Ignition skill raises your movement speed | Bring to [Snub](maps.html#map:12021000) in [West Fort City](maps.html#map:12021000):5 × **Broken Flight Unit** 10 × **Power Unit** 20 × **Heat Exhaust Unit** |

The five Optimization Modules:

- **Optimization Module (Amethyst)**
- **Optimization Module (Ruby)**
- **Optimization Module (Topaz)**
- **Optimization Module (Sapphire)**
- **Optimization Module (Diamond)**
