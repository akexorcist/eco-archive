# Iris Cards

Source page: [iris-cards.html](iris-cards.html)

## Card Assemble

[Ash Colchica](maps.html#map:10024000) in [Downtown](maps.html#map:10024000) combines cards into a higher rank for **5,000 G**.

- Rank 1: 10 × the same card.
- Rank 2 to Rank 4: 2 × the same card of the rank below.
- Each rank is harder. If it fails, the cards and the gold are lost.

**Ash Colchica**
