# Elementalist

Source page: [job-elementalist.html](job-elementalist.html)

Expert job · Spell User

A job that casts stronger elemental spells, including large area attacks.

## Job change

**Requirement:** MAG 30 or higher and Job Level 30 or higher as a Shaman.

1. Go to the [Guild Palace Lobby 1F](maps.html#map:10023000) in [Uptown](maps.html#map:10023000).
2. Go inside and take the **elevator** to the [Guild Palace Lobby 3F](maps.html#map:10023000).
3. Enter the room on the **east side** of the 3rd floor. This is the [Spell User Guild](maps.html#map:10023000).
4. Talk to the **Spell User Master** and choose **Elementalist**. The master sends you to visit the four elemental fairies.
   **Spell User Master**
5. Travel to [Staff Desert](maps.html#map:10042000) and talk to the [Fire Fairy](maps.html#map:10042000).
   **Fire Fairy**
6. Travel to [Snov Snowfield](maps.html#map:10002000) and talk to the [Water Fairy](maps.html#map:10002000).
   **Water Fairy**
7. Travel to [Battleship Island](maps.html#map:10035000) and talk to the [Wind Fairy](maps.html#map:10035000).
   **Wind Fairy**
8. Travel to [East Cape](maps.html#map:10018000) and talk to the [Earth Fairy](maps.html#map:10018000).
   **Earth Fairy**
   - You can visit the four fairies in any order.
9. Return to the **Spell User Master** in the [Spell User Guild](maps.html#map:10023000) ([Guild Palace Lobby 3F](maps.html#map:10023000), east room) and answer his three questions. Answers: **Water → Fire → Wind**.
   - The questions ask for the strongest element in [East Cape](maps.html#map:10018000) (Water), [Snov Snowfield](maps.html#map:10002000) (Fire) and [Cape Southerin](maps.html#map:10046000) (Wind).
   - A wrong answer fails the test. Visit all four fairies again before you retry.
10. When asked, **take off all your equipment**. The master gives you the Elementalist mark, and you are now an Elementalist.
