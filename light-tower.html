    Light Tower

    A tower of many floors, reached by airship from Morg City.

    On this page
    • How to enter
    • Floor map
    • Monsters
    • NPCs
    • Related

    How to enter

    1. Talk to Billionaire Diermant in the Merchant Guild Headquarters, Morg City, and choose to buy an airship ticket. The ticket costs Lv × 100 G (8,000 G at Lv 80).

      Billionaire Diermant

    2. Board the Light Tower airship in Morg City. It lands on the Light Tower Bottom Floor.

    • Other airship routes are on Airships & Rides.

    • To fly back to Morg City, talk to Airship Staff on the Light Tower Bottom Floor and board the airship next to him.

      Airship Staff
