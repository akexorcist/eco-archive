# Lv 65 Armor Quest

Source page: [quest-lv65-armor.html](quest-lv65-armor.html)

The Queen of the North, **Beldegard**, lets trusted adventurers take **Lv 65 armor** from the palace treasury.

## Before you start

- Bring a **party**. The giant [Minidoo](monsters.html#monster:10110200) is a party fight.
- Most jobs need **2 to 4** hunts for the full set.

## Walkthrough

### 1. Get your first Queen Key

- Go to [Northern Promenade](maps.html#map:10065000) and climb to the top of the town, to the [Queen Audience Room](maps.html#map:10065000). Talk to the queen, **Beldegard**. She gives you a **Queen Key**. You only get this free key once.
   **Beldegard**

### 2. Open the treasury

- Go back down to the [Northern Palace Ballroom](maps.html#map:10065000), the room before the Queen's. Talk to the **Guardswoman** in the **bottom-right corner** and choose to enter the treasury.
   **Guardswoman**
- In the [Repositorium](maps.html#map:10065000), open any chest with your first key. It gives you a [Resurrection Potion](materials.html#item:10000604).

### 3. Hunt the giant Minidoo

- Talk to the **Guardswoman** again and take her hunt quest, **Escaped Rabbit (Party)**. It costs **1 quest point** and has no time limit. A party can take it together.
- Go to the **bottom-left area** of [Northern Dungeon](maps.html#map:20014000) and defeat the giant [Minidoo](monsters.html#monster:10110200).
   - It doesn't attack first.
   - It grows bigger and hits harder the more you hit it.
   - Near death it **blows itself up**, hitting everyone nearby for about 2,000 damage. If at least one party member survives, the quest still counts.
- Report back to the **Guardswoman**. She gives you another **Queen Key**.

### 4. Open a chest

- Enter the [Repositorium](maps.html#map:10065000) and open the chest for the piece you want. Each key opens one chest and gives you the piece **for your job**.

## Good to know

- **A chest your job has no piece in won't open.** If the hat chest won't open, your job has no Lv 65 hat.
- **Party members don't all need the quest.** Friends below level 60 can still join the fight.
