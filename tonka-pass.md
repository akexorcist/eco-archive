# Tonka Pass

Source page: [tonka-pass.html](tonka-pass.html)

The pass for your first airship trip from [Malchut Dock](maps.html#map:20023000) to [Tonka Island](maps.html#map:10062000).

## Where to buy it

- The four guild masters in the guild rooms off the [Guild Palace Lobby 2F](maps.html#map:10023000), [Uptown](maps.html#map:10023000), sell it for **500 G**.
- **Swordsman Master** in the [Swordsman Guild](maps.html#map:10023000)
   **Swordsman Master**
- **Fencer Master** in the [Fencer Guild](maps.html#map:10023000)
   **Fencer Master**
- **Scout Master** in the [Scout Guild](maps.html#map:10023000)
   **Scout Master**
- **Archer Master** in the [Archer Guild](maps.html#map:10023000)
   **Archer Master**

## How to use it

- Show the pass to [South Faction Soldier](maps.html#map:20023000) in [Malchut Dock](maps.html#map:20023000) before your first airship trip to [Tonka Island](maps.html#map:10062000). He keeps the pass, and later trips are free. See [Airships & Rides](airships-and-rides.md#tonka-island).
   **South Faction Soldier**
- The **Iron South Knight Card** does not replace the pass.
