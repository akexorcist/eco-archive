    Dimension Dungeons

    Dimension dungeons (their names start with D-, for example D-Continental Cave B1F) are harder versions of normal dungeons.

    How to enter

    Each player needs one Dimensional Stone per entry, so a party needs one for every member.

    1. Talk to Dr. Dim in Uptown. He explains how to make the stone.

      Dr. Dim

    2. Make the Dimensional Stone with Item Refining (90% success), from either recipe:

      • Recipe 1:
        • 1 × Mana Water
        • 1 × Solid Pebble
        • 1 × Crest Paper
      • Recipe 2:
        • 1 × Mana Water
        • 1 dimension material, such as Dimension Coal or Dimension Ice Stone

    3. Carry the stone and walk into the normal dungeon's entrance. The game asks if you want to use it.

    4. Choose Use to spend one stone and go to the dimension version. Choose Don't use to enter the normal dungeon and keep the stone.

    Private dungeons

    A PD- dungeon (Private Dungeon) is a private copy of a dimension dungeon.

    • Use a Private Dungeon Generator (cash shop item) before you enter the dungeon. Every party member needs one.
    • Only you and your party (up to 8 players) can enter.
    • Monsters are as strong as in the D- dungeon, but give 1.5 × EXP and Job EXP.
    • No bosses or treasure chests appear.
    • It closes when the party breaks up, or when the last member leaves or logs out.
