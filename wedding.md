# Wedding

Source page: [wedding.html](wedding.html)

How two characters marry during the June Bride season event, and the outfits for it.

## How it works

- **Weddings are a season event, not a lasting system.** They take place during the June Bride event.
- **Any two characters can marry**, also two of the same gender.
- **A wedding gives no stats, skills or other bonuses.** The **Couple Certification(Event)** carries both names.
- **There is no divorce.** The certificate and the rented dresses disappear when the event ends.

## Getting married

### 1. Go to Acronia Church

- Talk to **Que** in [Uptown](maps.html#map:10023000) and choose to go. You are warped to **Acronia Church**.
   [Uptown](maps.html#map:10023000)

### 2. Collect Piece of Bond

- Talk to the **Priest of God** in **Acronia Church**. He sends you to the **Sacred Place**.
- Defeat the gem Pururu there. Each one drops **Piece of Bond**. Collect **10** or more.
   - [Aqua Marine Pururu](monsters.html#monster:10000007)
   - [Ruby Pururu](monsters.html#monster:10000102)
   - [Topaz Pururu](monsters.html#monster:10000803)
- The hunt also counts for the party quest **Good Place! Our Bond (Party)**. It has no time limit and gives no quest points.
   - Hunt: 1 × [Aqua Marine Pururu](monsters.html#monster:10000007), 10 × [Topaz Pururu](monsters.html#monster:10000803) and 20 × [Ruby Pururu](monsters.html#monster:10000102)
   - Reward: 10 × **Piece of Bond**
- Follow the path to the open area at its end. Talk to **Pit** to return to the church.

### 3. Get the Couple Certification

- Talk to the **Priest of God** in **Acronia Church** and exchange **Piece of Bond** for church furniture.
   - **Carpet of Bond**
   - **Church Floor**
   - **Church Wallpaper**
- After your first exchange, he also gives you the **Couple Certification(Event)**.

### 4. Hold the ceremony

- Form a party of just the two who marry, and stand next to each other.
- Use the **Couple Certification(Event)**. Both names are written on it.

## Wedding dresses

The wedding dresses are rented during the event.

- **To rent one**, trade **Love Bouquet** to Tiny on [Tiny Island](maps.html#map:10071000) for a **Wedding Dress Exchange Ticket**. Bring at least **1** bouquet; Tiny takes up to 3.
- [Tiny Flower](monsters.html#monster:30220000) on [Tiny Island](maps.html#map:10071000) drops the **Love Bouquet**.

Dresses you can rent:

- **Wedding Dress(F)**
- **Titania Wedding Dress(F)**
- **Dominion Dress(F)**
- **DEM Dress (F)**
- **Gothic Dress(F)**
- **Emil Wedding Dress (with Bouquet)**
- **Titania Wedding Dress (with Bouquet)**
- **Dominion Wedding Dress (with Bouquet)**
- **Gothic Wedding Dress (with Bouquet)**

## Sound Pururu quest

The church event quest that gives a skirt or pants and the Sound Pururu Heads.

### 1. Go to Valentine Church

- Get a **Love Bouquet** from [Tiny Flower](monsters.html#monster:30220000) on [Tiny Island](maps.html#map:10071000).
- With the bouquet in your bag, talk to the [Rainbow Maker](maps.html#map:10071000) and choose to go across the rainbow. You are warped to [Valentine Church](maps.html#map:10024000). The bouquet stays in your bag.
   **Rainbow Maker**

### 2. Find the Sound Pururu eggs

- Talk to the **Conductor Frog** and take the party quest **Sound Pururu Dungeon (Party)**. It has a 10-minute limit and gives no quest points.
- Go up to the heart platform in the middle at the back to enter.
- Defeat the Sound Pururu inside. Each one drops the egg of its note. Not every room has them.
- Collect one egg of each note:
   - **Sound Pururu Egg (Do)**
   - **Sound Pururu Egg (Re)**
   - **Sound Pururu Egg (Mi)**
   - **Sound Pururu Egg (Fa)**
   - **Sound Pururu Egg (Sol)**
   - **Sound Pururu Egg (La)**
   - **Sound Pururu Egg (Si)**
- In a party, each kill drops one egg, and every member hands in their own quest.

### 3. Get the reward

- Give the 7 eggs to the **Conductor Frog**, one at a time. If you are missing some, take the quest again.
- Talk to **Darling** or **Honey** behind the frog and choose a skirt or pants. You can do this once per character.

### 4. Sound Pururu Heads

Bring **10** eggs of one note to the Pururu of that note in the church to get its head. You can repeat this for every note.

- **Sound Pururu Head (Do)**
- **Sound Pururu Head (Re)**
- **Sound Pururu Head (Mi)**
- **Sound Pururu Head (Fa)**
- **Sound Pururu Head (So)**
- **Sound Pururu Head (La)**
- **Sound Pururu Head (Si)**
