# Dragon Mount

Source page: [dragon-mount.html](dragon-mount.html)

A **dragon mount** is a dragon you can ride, hatched from a dragon egg.

## Get an egg

| Egg | Hatches into | How to get it |
|---|---|---|
| **Fire Dragon Egg** | [Fire Dragon](partners.html#item:10052301) | Very rare drop from:[Pefang](monsters.html#monster:10440000) in [North Plateau](maps.html#map:10049000) and [North Mountain](maps.html#map:10050000) [Proon](monsters.html#monster:10440200) in [North Mountain](maps.html#map:10050000) |
| **Blue Dragon Egg** | [Blue Dragon](partners.html#item:10052303) | Very rare drop from:[Cyclops](monsters.html#monster:10340000) in [North Plateau](maps.html#map:10049000) and [North Mountain](maps.html#map:10050000) [Ogre](monsters.html#monster:10340300) in the [Northern Dungeon](maps.html#map:20014000) |

- You can also trade **3** × **Tiny Medal** with the [Resting Tiny](maps.html#map:10071000) on [Tiny Island](maps.html#map:10071000) for either egg.
- Get **Tiny Medal** from bosses.
- The [Resting Tiny](maps.html#map:10071000) only talks to you if you carry a **Tiny Medal**.
   **Resting Tiny**

## Hatch the egg

### 1. Talk to the Abashed Merchant

- Go to [Northern Promenade](maps.html#map:10065000) and talk to the [Abashed Merchant](maps.html#map:10065000). Choose to **listen** to his story.
   **Abashed Merchant**

### 2. Give the egg to the Senior Merchant

- Go to [Battleship Island](maps.html#map:10035000) and talk to the [Senior Merchant](maps.html#map:10035000), who stands next to a red dragon. Agree twice to hand over your egg.
   **Senior Merchant**
- He takes the egg and gives you the dragon. If you carry both eggs, he asks which one to hatch.

## Good to know

- **There's no quest for the egg itself.** You can hunt eggs before you talk to anyone; you only need the [Abashed Merchant](maps.html#map:10065000) before hatching.
- **The monsters that drop the eggs attack first if you use a skill near them** (an attack skill, magic, a campfire or a heal), and so do other monsters around them:
   - [Cyclops](monsters.html#monster:10340000)
   - [Ogre](monsters.html#monster:10340300)
   - [Pefang](monsters.html#monster:10440000)
   - [Proon](monsters.html#monster:10440200)
