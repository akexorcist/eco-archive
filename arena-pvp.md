# Arena PvP

Source page: [arena-pvp.html](arena-pvp.html)

Fighting other players in the Arena.

## Where you can fight players

- You can attack other players in the [Arena](#how-to-join), the [Champion Battle](champion-battle.md) and [Knight Training](knight-training.md). There is no one-on-one duel request.
- Members of the same party can't attack each other.

## How to join

1. Go to the **Arena** from [Downtown](maps.html#map:10024000).
   Downtown — Arena
2. Cancel any [possession](possession.md), then talk to the **Owner** and choose the **Arena**.
   **Owner**
3. After 15 seconds you can fight.

## Rules

- Everyone who joined can attack everyone else who joined.
- You can possess only players who are in the **Arena** too.
- Dying in the **Arena** costs no EXP, and your equipment loses no durability.
- To stop, talk to the **Owner** again and give up. Cancel any possession first.
- Leaving the **Arena** also ends it. The **Owner** can send you back to [Downtown](maps.html#map:10024000).
- Some skills work differently against players: see [PvP Skill Changes](pvp-skill-changes.md).
