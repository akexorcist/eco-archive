## Story

The stakes reach their peak as the villain in yellow robes, **Hastur**, steals the energy of human hopes and dreams, unleashing a "Yellow Wind of Ruin" across every world. He turns El Ciel's sealing barrier into a power source to awaken **Cthulhu**, a terrifying entity that once devoured the Titania World.

Initially outmatched by Hastur's power, your team trains with the **Titania Dragon** and travels the skies in a Flying Garden to rescue besieged towns and free possessed allies. Even gaining the aid of another Hastur who fights on your side, you and your companions make one final stand. Carrying everyone's hopes and connected feelings, you defeat Hastur and Cthulhu to save all the worlds.

## After the story

- Once you finish the story, talk to **Emil** in [Uptown](maps.html#map:10023000) to open **\[HIGH LEVEL\] Decisive Battle - Connecting Feelings -**. It's a much harder version of the final battle for Rebirth Lv 110+, with 8 players recommended.
- Clear it for a title and a **Soul of the Strong**, which gives a special ride partner.
- **Emil** also gives a Chronicle armor set when you finish the story.

## Chronicle armor

- Both versions look the same and need **Lv 110**. Any race and job can wear them.
- **Powered** has STR and ATK, for physical jobs. **Magical** has MAG and MATK, for magic jobs.
