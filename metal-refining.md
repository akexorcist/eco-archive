# Metal Refining

Source page: [metal-refining.html](metal-refining.html)

[Advanced Metal Refining](job-miner.md#skill:2051) smelts ore into metal, makes metal parts and melts old parts back down.

## How it works

- [Advanced Metal Refining](job-miner.md#skill:2051) is a [Miner (Tatarabe)](job-miner.md) skill, learned at Job Level 13, with 3 levels.
- Each skill level opens the recipes of that level. The [Crafting](crafting.md) page lists every [Advanced Metal Refining](job-miner.md#skill:2051) recipe.
- Most metal recipes succeed 99% of the time. A failed try gives an [Iron Fragment](materials.html#item:10020209).
- Most recipes past the ore stage need a [Miner Furnace](materials.html#item:10033100).

## Refine with an NPC

Talk to one of these NPCs with the materials and the tool in your inventory. Each makes every [Advanced Metal Refining](job-miner.md#skill:2051) recipe, and also weapon and armor recipes.

The fee depends on the recipe level:

| Recipe level | Fee |
|---|---|
| Lv 1 | 100 G |
| Lv 2 | 5,000 G |
| Lv 3 | 10,000 G |

A few special recipes cost more, such as the [True Heart Stone](materials.html#item:10072300) from a [True Rainbow Stone](materials.html#item:10071400) (40,000 G).

They stand in these places:

- **Weaponsmith** in the [Weaponsmith](maps.html#map:10024000), [Downtown](maps.html#map:10024000).
   **Weaponsmith**
- **Weaponsmith** in the [Iron City Weaponsmith](maps.html#map:10066000), [Lower Part of Iron City](maps.html#map:10066000).
   **Weaponsmith**
- **Weaponsmith** in the [Fortress Weaponsmith](maps.html#map:10054001), [Enigma Group Fortress](maps.html#map:10054001).
   **Weaponsmith**
- **Ironworks Receptionist** in the [Forge Lobby](maps.html#map:10066000), [Lower Part of Iron City](maps.html#map:10066000).
   **Ironworks Receptionist**
