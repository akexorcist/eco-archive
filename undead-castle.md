# Undead Castle

Source page: [undead-castle.html](undead-castle.html)

A castle of many rooms, entered from [Breadbasket](maps.html#map:10068000).

## How to enter

- Enter [Undead Castle](maps.html#map:10069000) from [Breadbasket](maps.html#map:10068000).
   [Breadbasket](maps.html#map:10068000)
- The [Lv 60 Weapon Quest](quest-lv60-weapon.md) walks you through the castle rooms.
