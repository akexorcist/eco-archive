# Flying Garden

Source page: [flying-garden.html](flying-garden.html)

Your **Flying Garden** is your own airship with a house and a garden.

## Getting your Flying Garden

You get the **Airship Key** in the last part of the beginner tutorial.

1. Talk to **Amis** in [Uptown](maps.html#map:10023000) and choose to board the airship.
   **Amis**
2. On the airship, talk to **Amis** to start "Excitement and let's go back to school".
3. Talk to **Cockko Alma** in [Acronia Eastern Plains](maps.html#map:10025000) and agree to take a lost child home.
   **Cockko Alma**
4. Go to [East Cape](maps.html#map:10018000), at the far east end of [Fareast Main Street](maps.html#map:10017000), and talk to **Balulu Alma**.
   **Balulu Alma**
5. Find **Balulu Alma** again at the airship workshop on [Fareast Main Street](maps.html#map:10017000). You get the **Airship Key** here.
   **Balulu Alma**

## Using the key

- Use the **Airship Key** at a **landing spot**: a fenced area with a wooden ladder. It only works on maps that have one.
- Click the rope to:
   - Go aboard your Flying Garden
   - Pack the garden up again
   - Choose who may come aboard
   - Show a sign with a name on the rope
- You can fly between towns that have landing spots, and take **4–5 friends** with you.
- Friends can come along even if their level is more than 30 apart from yours.

## Storage

Your Flying Garden has storage for **100 more items**.

## Decorating

The edit window has these parts:

- House
- Wall
- Roof
- Room wall
- Garden
- Room floor
- Base
- Sail

Some tips:

- Double-click furniture to place it. You can turn it in 4 directions and set snap or exact positions.
- Paint the base and the sails with a paint can while you're on board.
- Seeds can't be planted, but flower seeds for greenhouses make decorative flower pots.

## Recommendations

- Use your Flying Garden to buy goods in far towns: let another character wait in that town, buy there, and put the goods in the garden's storage.
