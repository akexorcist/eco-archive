# Warlock

Source page: [job-warlock.html](job-warlock.html)

Base job · Spell User

A dark magic job that, unlike other Spell Users, fights well up close and can use shields.

## Job change

**Requirement:** MAG 10 or higher.

1. Enter [Uptown](maps.html#map:10023000). You need an **Acropolis City Pass**.
2. Go to the [Black Church](maps.html#map:10023000) in [Uptown](maps.html#map:10023000).
3. Talk to the **Black Church Vates** and choose to become a **Warlock**.
   **Black Church Vates**
4. **Emil and Titania only:** travel to the [Island of the Undead](maps.html#map:10019000) and talk to [Guglielmo](maps.html#map:10019000). He sends you across to the island. Dominion characters skip steps 4 and 5.
   **Guglielmo**
5. **Emil and Titania only:** talk to the [Dark Fairy](maps.html#map:10019000), then return to the **Black Church Vates**.
   **Dark Fairy**
6. The **Black Church Vates** sends you to **Mr. Black** in [Downtown](maps.html#map:10024000). Talk to him and choose to hear about dark magic.
   **Mr. Black**
7. Return to the **Black Church Vates** in the [Black Church](maps.html#map:10023000) ([Uptown](maps.html#map:10023000)). Answer his three questions: **There's nothing like that** → **Light property** → **Dagger**.
   - If you give a wrong answer, talk to **Mr. Black** again before you retry.
8. Choose to become a Warlock. When asked, **take off your armor**.
9. Talk to him again. You are now a Warlock and receive a [Brooch of the Darkness](equipment.html#item:50050600). You also learn [Black Widow](job-warlock.md#skill:3083).
