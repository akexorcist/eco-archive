    Joker

    Chronicle job · Novice

    A job that can use every weapon and wear any job's armor, within its level, race and gender limits.

    Job change

    Requirement: Lv 100 or higher, Job Level 50 as a Novice, and at least one win over the Emil Dragon. You must never have changed jobs.

    1. Travel to Northern Promenade. When you meet the requirements, a mysterious creature (a cat) appears there for you to talk to. If it doesn't appear, you don't meet them yet.

    2. Talk to the creature until it runs away.

      ? ? ?

    3. Go to the Northern Cafe in Northern Promenade and talk to Valeria.

      Valeria

    4. Chase the creature: find it again in Northern Promenade and follow it into the Queen Audience Room.

    5. Follow the creature into the magic circle.

    6. In the White World, walk straight ahead to the creature sleeping there. It asks you 3 questions.

      ? ? ?
      • Answers: choose not to wake it, then answer that it is real. Any answer works for the third question.
      • A wrong answer sends you back. Walk to the creature again.

    7. In the room with the book (White World), talk to Mucha, then look inside the White Book.

      Mucha
      White Book

    8. You receive the Book of Rebirth: A Journey to Find Yourself. Travel to each spot below and use the book there.

      1

      Middle of the town

      2

      In front of the Guild Palace Lobby 1F

      3

      In the pit in the middle of the city where logs appear

      4

      In front of the wind-up doll (Pino) statue

      5

      Next to the Wizard Pilgrim, near the shop

      6

      Top left of the map, in front of the Air Force Camp Guard

      7

      On the road near Michelle, who is plowing the field

    9. When the book is complete, return to the White World and show it to Mucha.

    10. Look inside the White Book again. Take off your equipment first. You become a Joker and reincarnate into the higher form of your race (see Races). Your level and Job Level go back to 1.
      • You keep your skills, but you raise them again.
