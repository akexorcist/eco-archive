# Lv 35 Armor Quest

Source page: [quest-lv35-armor.html](quest-lv35-armor.html)

Help shopkeepers in Acropolis open their **armor and shoe** shops.

## Before you start

- Talk to each shopkeeper once. He or she picks **one request at random** and keeps asking for it until you bring it.
- What they ask for depends on your level **when you first talk to them**. The level 20+ requests are smaller and easier to collect.
- You don't have to open all four.

## Walkthrough

### 1. Metal armor: Apprentice Blacksmith Arus

- Go to [Uptown](maps.html#map:10023000) and talk to [Apprentice Blacksmith Arus](maps.html#map:10023000) on the south side.
   **Apprentice Blacksmith Arus**
- He asks for one of these:
   | Your level | He asks for one of |
   |---|---|
   | Below 20 | 170 × [Tree Branch](materials.html#item:10016605) 10 × [Timber](materials.html#item:10016300) 40 × [Pebble](materials.html#item:10014600) 10 × [Solid Pebble](materials.html#item:10014650) 10 × [Iron Lump](materials.html#item:10015600) 40 × [Bone](materials.html#item:10006600) |
   | 20 and higher | 1 of each:[Flame Summonstone](materials.html#item:10011201) [Water Summonstone](materials.html#item:10011203) [Earth Summonstone](materials.html#item:10011205) [Wind Summonstone](materials.html#item:10011207) or 1 of each:[Light Summonstone](materials.html#item:10011210) [Dark Summonstone](materials.html#item:10011209) |
- Bring the items back. He opens his shop of **metal armor** for Swordsman, Fencer, Scout, Archer ([Archer Mail](equipment.html#item:60001300)) and Ranger ([Camouflage Armor](equipment.html#item:60000050)):
   **Swordsman, Fencer and Scout lines**
   [**Warrior Armor** 33,750 G](equipment.html#item:60001600)
   **Swordsman, Blade Master, Bounty Hunter**
   [**Swordsman Armor(F)** 45,000 G](equipment.html#item:60002300) [**Swordsman Armor(M)** 45,000 G](equipment.html#item:60001400)
   **Fencer, Knight, Dark Stalker**
   [**Fencer Armor(F)** 43,500 G](equipment.html#item:60002400) [**Fencer Armor(M)** 43,500 G](equipment.html#item:60001500)
   **Archer, Striker, Gunner**
   [**Archer Mail** 30,000 G](equipment.html#item:60001300)
   **Ranger, Explorer, Treasure Hunter**
   [**Camouflage Armor** 36,000 G](equipment.html#item:60000050)

- When all four shops are open, go to [Downtown](maps.html#map:10024000) and talk to the [Elegant Lady](maps.html#map:10024000). She gives you **1 skill point**.
   **Elegant Lady**
