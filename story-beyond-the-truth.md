## Story

In the aftermath of the war with the DEM, your journey shifts to the Titania World with a clear goal: reviving Titus's sister, **Tita**. Following ancient clues, you help repair a flying castle and board it with Emil, eventually reaching El Ciel's tower where Tita is finally brought back to life.

Yet, a deeper mystery remains regarding Emil's lost memories. Your search leads into the depths of Whale Rock, where an ancient dragon, **Emil Dragon**, holds the secret to Emil's forgotten past. Just as Emil confronts his memories, a sinister figure forces the giant whale into a violent rampage. With the dragon holding back the beast's power, you stand your ground and clash with the mastermind behind the chaos, **Hastur**.
