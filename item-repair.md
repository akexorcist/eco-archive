# Item Repair

Source page: [item-repair.html](item-repair.html)

Equipment loses durability as you use it, and a repair kit brings it back.

## How repair works

- Use a repair kit on the item. The item's info shows which kit it needs.
- No NPC repairs items. Players craft the kits from a tool and a material.
- Each repair lowers the item's **Max Durability by 1**. More rules are on [Equipment Upgrades](equipment-upgrades.md#durability-and-repair).
- An item with Max Durability 1 can't be repaired again.
- You can't use a kit on an item at full durability.
- A kit restores a random amount. The range depends on the skill level of the player who made it:

| Kit made at skill Lv | Durability restored |
|---|---|
| 1 | 1–8 |
| 2 | 2–11 |
| 3 | 3–14 |
| 4 | 4–17 |
| 5 | 5–20 |

## Which kit an item needs

Each item names one kit. The material matches the item, and the grade matches its level:

- **Copper** for most items below Lv 30.
- **Silver** for most items from Lv 30 to 59.
- **Gold** for most items from Lv 60.

| Kit | Used on |
|---|---|
| Metal | Swords, axes, hammers, spears, knives, shields and metal armor |
| Garment | Clothes, dresses, lower armor, hats and socks |
| Woodwork | Staffs, books, bows, instruments and wooden shoes |
| Leather | Boots, shoes, backpacks and leather armor |
| Machinery | Guns, dual guns and rifles |
| Accessory | Head, face, neck and finger accessories |
| DEM Part | DEM parts |
