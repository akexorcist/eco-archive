    Eastern Dungeon

    A dungeon entered from Fareast City.

    On this page
    • How to enter
    • Monsters
    • Related

    How to enter

    • Enter the Eastern Dungeon from Fareast City.

    • The Eastern Dungeon leads on to the Poison Swamp and the Virgin Forest.

    • Hell King's Forest opens only with a Violet Golden Jewel, which breaks when you go in.
