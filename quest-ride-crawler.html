    Ride Crawler Quest

    Raise a Petite Crawler into a Ride Crawler you can ride.

    Level
    31 or higher, with 20 Fame
    Items needed
    One pet to trade, any of:
    • Pegasus
    • Bawoo Bawoo
    • Roque Bird
    • Puppy
    • Momo
    Reward
    Petite Crawler, which becomes one Ride Crawler:
    • Riding Crawler (Green)
    • Riding Crawler (Red)
    • Riding Crawler (Purple)
    • Riding Crawler (Blue)
    • Riding Crawler (Yellow)
    • Riding Crawler (Rock)
    • Riding Crawler (Fluffy)
    • Riding Crawler (White)

    Before you start

    • Pierre in Fareast City asks for one particular pet. Where to find them:

      Pet Where
      Puppy The pet shop at East Cape
      Bawoo Bawoo A very rare drop from Crimson Bawoo
      Roque Bird A drop from Thunderbird
      Pegasus A drop from Vega
      Momo From its quest

    Walkthrough

    1. Meet the Riding Crawler

    • Go to Momo Plains and talk to the Riding Crawler, next to the Pet Breeder.

      Riding Crawler

    2. Trade a pet with Pierre

    • Go to Fareast City and talk to Pierre, who stands with a group of small crawlers. He asks for one pet.

      Pierre

    • If his pet is hard to find, answer that you promise to bring it, then talk to him again. He offers to change his request once. This doesn't work if he first asked for the Puppy or Momo.

    • Give him the pet. He gives you a Petite Crawler.

    3. Raise your crawler

    • Raise the Petite Crawler as your partner. See Pet Raising for how partners grow.
    • Its Base Lv, Partner Rank and Trust carry over to the Ride Crawler.

    4. Turn it into a Ride Crawler

    • Leave the Petite Crawler with the Pet Breeder on Momo Plains. It comes back as a Ride Crawler. The colour pattern is random.

      Pet Breeder

    Good to know

    • Riding hides you from most monsters. Monsters that attack first ignore you, except bosses and monsters in dungeons.
    • It carries a lot but isn't fast. A Ride Crawler is slower than a dragon.
