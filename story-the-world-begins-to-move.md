## Story

Peace does not last long. Urgent word arrives that the Dominion World is in danger. You and your friends rush to help, only to get separated right in the middle of a massive DEM invasion.

After regrouping at the Resistance headquarters in [West Fort City](maps.html#map:12021000), you take on vital missions across the land: assisting a scientist, [Dr. Stein](maps.html#map:12021000), scouting enemy territory at night, and rallying major cities to join the fight. Everything builds up to a daring counterattack called Operation: Land of War Songs. While the Resistance launches a surface assault, you and [Emil](maps.html#map:10057000) infiltrate the DEM base through underground tunnels, fighting past tough guardians to rescue your struggling friends in the heart of the enemy stronghold.
