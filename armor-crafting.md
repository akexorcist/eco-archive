# Armor Crafting

Source page: [armor-crafting.html](armor-crafting.html)

[Armor Construction](job-blacksmith.md#skill:2017) makes body armor, helmets, shoes, boots and shields.

## How it works

- [Armor Construction](job-blacksmith.md#skill:2017) is a [Blacksmith](job-blacksmith.md) skill, learned at Job Level 15, with 10 levels. Its recipes go up to Lv 5.
- Each skill level opens the recipes of that level. The [Crafting](crafting.md) page lists every [Armor Construction](job-blacksmith.md#skill:2017) recipe.
- Armor succeeds 100% of the time. Most pieces come out better some of the time:
   - 5%: the +1 version, such as **Leather Armor +1**
   - 3%: the +2 version
   - 1%: the +3 version
- Plate armor and metal shields use [Iron Plate](materials.html#item:10038616), made from 1 × [Iron Bullion](materials.html#item:10016100) and 1 × [Distilled Water](materials.html#item:10000204) with a [Miner Furnace](materials.html#item:10033100) ([Lv 4](crafting.html#recipe:1064)).

## Craft with an NPC

Talk to one of these NPCs with the materials and the tool in your inventory. Each makes every [Armor Construction](job-blacksmith.md#skill:2017) recipe, and also weapon and [Advanced Metal Refining](job-miner.md#skill:2051) recipes.

The fee depends on the recipe level:

| Recipe level | Fee |
|---|---|
| Lv 1 | 100 G |
| Lv 2 | 1,000 G |
| Lv 3 | 2,000 G |
| Lv 4 | 4,000 G |
| Lv 5 | 5,000 G |

They stand in these places:

- **Weaponsmith** in the [Weaponsmith](maps.html#map:10024000), [Downtown](maps.html#map:10024000).
   **Weaponsmith**
- **Weaponsmith** in the [Iron City Weaponsmith](maps.html#map:10066000), [Lower Part of Iron City](maps.html#map:10066000).
   **Weaponsmith**
- **Weaponsmith** in the [Fortress Weaponsmith](maps.html#map:10054001), [Enigma Group Fortress](maps.html#map:10054001).
   **Weaponsmith**
- **Ironworks Receptionist** in the [Forge Lobby](maps.html#map:10066000), [Lower Part of Iron City](maps.html#map:10066000).
   **Ironworks Receptionist**
