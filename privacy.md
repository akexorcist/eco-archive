# Privacy Policy

Source page: [privacy.html](privacy.html)

What ECO Archive collects, which cookies it uses, and how to change your choice.

## Who runs this site

ECO Archive, an unofficial fan wiki for Emil Chronicle Online.

Questions or requests: open an issue at [github.com/akexorcist/eco-archive/issues](https://github.com/akexorcist/eco-archive/issues). GitHub's own privacy policy applies to what you post there.

## What we collect

- **Without your consent:** nothing. Google Analytics is not loaded and no analytics cookies are set.
- **With your consent:** Google Analytics usage data. This covers pages viewed, approximate location (city or country), device and browser, the site you came from, and scrolls, outbound link clicks and site searches.
- There are no accounts, forms or ads on this site.

Google Analytics 4 does not log or store IP addresses. It uses them only to work out an approximate location.

## Cookies and local storage

Google Analytics cookies are set only after you accept.

| Name | Set by | Purpose | Kept for |
|---|---|---|---|
| `\_ga` | Google Analytics | Tells visits from the same browser apart | Up to 2 years |
| `\_ga\_<container-id>` | Google Analytics | Keeps the current visit together | Up to 2 years |

These are Google's default durations. `<container-id>` is the site's Google Analytics ID without the `G-`.

The site also saves your choice (accept or reject, the date and a version number) in your browser's local storage. It never leaves your browser. Clearing your browser's site data removes it.

## Third parties

- **Google Analytics** (Google Ireland Limited in the EEA and Switzerland, Google LLC elsewhere): measures site use, only with your consent. Your data may be processed outside your country.
- **Cloudflare**: delivers the site and protects it from attacks. It processes IP addresses to do this. The site sets no tracking cookies through Cloudflare.

## How long data is kept

Google Analytics keeps user-level data for 2 months, then deletes it. Reports keep only totals.

## Your choices and rights

- Change your choice at any time with here. Rejecting deletes the Google Analytics cookies.
- Under the GDPR and Thailand's PDPA you can ask to access or delete your data, and object to its use.
- Google Analytics data is aggregated and not linked to your name or identity. So we usually cannot find one person's data, but you can still ask.
