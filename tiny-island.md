# Tiny Island

Source page: [tiny-island.html](tiny-island.html)

[Tiny Island](maps.html#map:10071000), home of the Tinies, the bear-like spirits of ECO.

## Getting there

- Go to [Uptown](maps.html#map:10023000) and talk to the [Tiny](maps.html#map:10023000) in the middle of the town. The first time, he only greets you. Talk to him again and choose to go: he sends you to [Tiny Island](maps.html#map:10071000).
   **Tiny**

## Tiny Medals

- **Tiny Medal** drops from **bosses**, and can be found in Wooden Box 10 and 11 and in the red treasure boxes from 9 up.
- Wooden Box 10 and 11 come from instance dungeon quests.

## Pirate Tiny's trades

- Talk to the [Pirate Tiny](maps.html#map:10071000) on the west side of the island.
   **Pirate Tiny**

| You give | You get |
|---|---|
| 1 × **Tiny Medal** | **Tiny Rod** |
| 1 × **Tiny Medal** | **Tiny Blue Elegant Table** |
| 1 × **Tiny Medal** | **Tiny Picnic Chair** |
| 1 × **Tiny Medal** | **Calendar(Tiny)** |
| 3 × **Tiny Medal** | [Tiny Navy Blue Suitcase](equipment.html#item:50071051) |
| 3 × **Tiny Medal** | [Tiny Light Pink Suitcase](equipment.html#item:50071052) |
| 10 × **Tiny Medal** | **Tiny Rainbow Medal** |
| 1 × **Tiny Rainbow Medal** | **Purple Waitress Uniform(F)** |
| 3 × **Tiny Rainbow Medal** | **Left-Hand Tiny Navy Blue Suitcase** |
| 3 × **Tiny Rainbow Medal** | **Left-Hand Tiny Baby Pink Suitcase** |

## Dragon Eggs

- The [Resting Tiny](maps.html#map:10071000) on the south side trades **3** × **Tiny Medal** for a **Fire Dragon Egg** or a **Blue Dragon Egg**. You need to carry a **Tiny Medal** before he will talk to you.
   **Resting Tiny**
- Hatch the egg on [Battleship Island](maps.html#map:10035000). See [Dragon Mount](dragon-mount.md).
