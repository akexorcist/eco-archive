# Cooking

Source page: [cooking.html](cooking.html)

Turning food ingredients into dishes.

## How it works

- [Cook](job-farmer.md#skill:2040) is a [Farmer](job-farmer.md) skill, learned at Job Level 12, with 5 levels.
- Each skill level opens the recipes of that level. The [Crafting](crafting.md) page lists every [Cook](job-farmer.md#skill:2040) recipe.
- Most recipes succeed 99% of the time. A failed try gives a **Failure Compound**.
- Cooked food is lighter than its ingredients. An [Apple](materials.html#item:10002800) weighs 10, and a [Dried Apple](materials.html#item:10003900) made from it weighs 2 and heals more.
- Many base ingredients come from [Refining](refining.md): [Sugar](materials.html#item:10045300), [Salt](materials.html#item:10045400), [Butter](materials.html#item:10008450), [Giant Barley Flour](materials.html#item:10001100), [Mineral Water](materials.html#item:10001800) and [Spice](materials.html#item:10008700).

## Cook with an NPC

Any character can bring the ingredients to one of these NPCs and order a dish. They cook every recipe up to Lv 5.

- You pay the fee once per order, however many dishes you make.
- The fee depends on the recipe level:

| Recipe level | Fee |
|---|---|
| Lv 1 | 0 G |
| Lv 2 | 200 G |
| Lv 3 | 300 G |
| Lv 4 | 400 G |
| Lv 5 | 500 G |

- [Mix Juice](crafting.html#recipe:418) restores HP and SP.
- [Partner Food](crafting.html#recipe:550) feeds partners. See [Pet Raising](pet-raising.md).
- [Pizza(Magical 4 ECO)](crafting.html#recipe:491) heals every player around you. Each of its four pizzas needs [Pizza Dough](materials.html#item:10052700) and [Pizza Topping](materials.html#item:10052800), both Lv 4 recipes.
- A few Lv 5 dishes fail often: [Mixed Stew](crafting.html#recipe:481) succeeds 10% of the time and [Miso Soup](crafting.html#recipe:485) 33%.
