# Possession

Source page: [possession.html](possession.html)

**Possession** lets your character become a spirit inside another player's equipment.

## How it works

- You can only possess a player within **30 levels** of you. You don't need to be in the same party.
- You possess one equipment slot: **right hand** (weapon), **left hand** (shield), **chest** (accessory) or **body** (armor).
- An item someone possesses **doesn't lose durability**.
- While possessing, your skills cost **twice the SP**.
- You can't turn into a marionette while you possess someone.
- One player can carry at most **3** possessors at a time.
- While possessing, you can't pick up items, talk to NPCs or trade. Your HP, MP and SP regenerate.
- Possession ends when the host's HP reaches 0.
- The **Possession Catalog** lists the possession items on your map, with the stats they give.

## EXP

| Situation | The host gets | You get |
|---|---|---|
| Not in a party | Full EXP | **10%** of the host's EXP |
| In the same party | EXP is shared out across the party as normal |  |
| You log out while possessing | Full EXP | Still 10% |

- If you are a spell user possessing someone outside a party and your spell defeats the monster, the **host** gets its full EXP.

## Taking damage

- When monsters hit the host, the hit can land on the person possessing them instead. The **shield** slot is hit most often, then the weapon, armor and accessory.
- A hit on you checks **your** dodge first, then **your** DEF.
- If you log out while possessing, you can't be hit.

## Skills that use Possession

| Skill | Effect |
|---|---|
| [Spirit skills](#spirit-skills) | Add part of **your** stats to the host, by the slot you possess. Only the person possessing needs the skill, and it keeps working after you log out |
| Attack Assist | Raises the host's attack |
| Defence Assist | You take a share of the host's damage: 20/30/40/50/70% by skill level |
| Protect | The person possessing you takes no damage |
| Expand | +5% EXP for people nearby; usable while possessing, but the host gets no bonus |
| Possessed Out | A Necromancer skill that forces a target out of Possession and makes them Undead for a while |

Many attack and placement skills **can't** be used while you possess someone, for example Elemental Ball, [Elemental Quartet](job-elementalist.md#skill:3302), Dark Grove, [Sonic Whip](job-treasure-hunter.md#skill:2430), [Chaos Gate](job-necromancer.md#skill:3332) and [Gardening](job-gardener.md#skill:1007).

## Special cases

- **Iris cards** that aren't locked don't work while someone possesses the item. Locked cards do.
- **DEM** in Machina Form can't be possessed, except through Set Form Parts, which have 3 Possession slots.
- **Merchants** can't set up a shop while possessing.
- A city guard won't let you in while someone possesses you. Check your equipment window (Ctrl+Q) if a guard refuses you.
- Some rooms only let in certain jobs. A player of another job can still enter by possessing someone who is allowed in.

## Recommendations

- **Log out after you possess someone for EXP.**
- **New spell users level fast by possessing a Fighter or Backpacker friend** who walks while you cast.
- **In the cold north you can't log out normally outside towns.** Possessing your own weapon first lets you leave safely.
