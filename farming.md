# Farming

Source page: [farming.html](farming.html)

Growing vegetables, fruit and grain on your ring's **Flying Castle**.

## Before you start

- Your ring needs a **Flying Castle** with a **Farm** placed on it.
   **Farm Fairy**
   - Get the Farm from the request of the [Farm Fairy](maps.html#map:10022000) in [Acronia Western Plains](maps.html#map:10022000).
   - Only members who can spend Expansion Points can place it.
- Get **seeds**. There are vegetable, fruit and grain seeds, in three patterns (Stripe, Prickle and Gloss), plus Large and Rare seeds.

## How to grow crops

1. Plant a seed in an empty plot on the farm.
2. Look after it every day.
3. Harvest the crop after **2 days**. Every crop you harvest is recorded in your farm log.

| **Water** | ✓ | ✓ |  | ✓ |  |

| **Play Music** | ✓ | ✓ |  |  | ✓ |
| **Gather Light** | ✓ | ✓ |  |  | ✓ |
| **Fertilize** | ✓ | ✓ |  |  | ✓ |

- Crops go to the ring's **Harvest Box**: up to 30 kinds, 99 of each.
- Removing the Farm deletes its crops and the Harvest Box contents.
- Ring members who **Talk** to the crops on another ring's farm get **2 points** per plant, up to 80 points a day.
- When players from another ring Talk to the crops on your farm, your ring gets **2 points** per plant, up to 100 points a day.
