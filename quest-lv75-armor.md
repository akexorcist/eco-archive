# Lv 75 Armor Quest

Source page: [quest-lv75-armor.html](quest-lv75-armor.html)

Pass [Princess Chunsoon](maps.html#map:10023000)'s tests to buy the **Lv 75 armor**.

## Before you start

- Once a Technical job reaches **Job Level 30**, you can switch to another job and still take the quest.
- Keep at least **1** quest point free.

## Walkthrough

### 1. Accept her challenge

- Go to [Uptown](maps.html#map:10023000) and talk to [Princess Chunsoon](maps.html#map:10023000). Accept her challenge, **Princess Chunson Challenge! (Party)**.
   **Princess Chunsoon**

### 2. Defeat 999 monsters

- Defeat **999 monsters** of any kind. There is no time limit. Trees, grass, boxes and rocks don't count.
- Report back to [Princess Chunsoon](maps.html#map:10023000). She gives you the **Princess Chunsoon Airship Key**.

### 3. Her last challenge

- Talk to her again and accept **Selfish Princess' Last Challenge!**. You can't have anyone possessing you for this part.
- She takes the key back and sends you to her airship garden. You have **3 minutes**.
- A chicken and its chicks run around the garden. Defeating them changes nothing: **wait until the time runs out**.
- Talk to [Princess Chunsoon](maps.html#map:10023000). She wants you to defeat another 999 monsters, but her grandfather steps in and ends the quest.
