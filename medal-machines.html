    Medal Machines

    Medal machines give you a random item for a medal.

    On this page
    • Medals
    • Where the machines are
    • What you can get
    • Related

    Medals

    Medal How to get it
    Emil Medal From the beginner tutorial
    Copper Medal Give 10 × Torn Magic Book to the Village Boy in Downtown
    Silver Medal Trade 10 × Copper Medal with the Northern Border Guard in Cape Northerin
    Gold Medal Trade 10 × Silver Medal with the Iron South Border Guard in Cape Southerin

    Copper Medals from torn books

    • Talk to the Village Boy in Downtown, near the Elegant Lady. He trades 10 × Torn Magic Book for a Copper Medal.

      Village Boy

    • Torn Magic Book drops often from Young CockKo and sometimes from Polar Bear.

    Trade up

    • The Northern Border Guard in Cape Northerin trades 10 × Copper Medal for 1 × Silver Medal.

      Northern Border Guard

    • The Iron South Border Guard in Cape Southerin trades 10 × Silver Medal for 1 × Gold Medal.

      Iron South Border Guard

    What you can get

    Put in a medal and pick a handle (Cute, Cool or Useful). You get a random item from that group: clothes, accessories, furniture or useful items.

    Each machine has its own prize list, and higher medals give better prizes. Some examples:
