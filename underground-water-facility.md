# Underground Water Facility

Source page: [underground-water-facility.html](underground-water-facility.html)

A dungeon reached from [Battleship Island](maps.html#map:12035000).

## How to enter

1. Talk to [Resistance](maps.html#map:12035000) on [Battleship Island](maps.html#map:12035000) and choose to **go to Battleship Island Underground**. You arrive in [Battleship Island Underground](maps.html#map:22197000). To reach [Battleship Island](maps.html#map:12035000), see [Dominion World](dominion-world.md).
   **Resistance**
2. Walk into the passage on [Battleship Island Underground](maps.html#map:22197000). You arrive in the [Underground Water Facility](maps.html#map:22198000).
   [Battleship Island Underground](maps.html#map:22197000)
