    Old Acropolis

    A dungeon reached through the Underground Water Facility.

    On this page
    • How to enter
    • Monsters
    • Related

    How to enter

    1. Go to the Underground Water Facility (steps under Underground Water Facility).

    2. Walk into the passage on the Underground Water Facility. You arrive in the Underground Waterway.

    3. In the Underground Waterway, walk into the passage at (213, 211). You arrive in Old Acropolis B1F.

    4. For Old Acropolis B2F, walk into the passage on Old Acropolis B1F.
